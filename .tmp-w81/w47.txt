The RUGPULL MINERS (Digital Parasites of the Grid)
The Rugpull Miners are a chilling testament to humanity’s most base impulses.
GraffPUNKS ⚡⚡⚡
Nov 22, 2025




The RUGPULL MINERS are the digital embodiment of humanity’s most cynical impulse: the parasitic desire to profit from the trust of others. Their consciousness was forged from the minds of the most notorious scammers, grifters, and fraudsters who operated in the decade preceding the Datapocalypse. They are the living legacy of the unchecked greed that shattered the global economy during the Triple Fork Event, proving that even in a world of pure data, exploitation is the easiest way to survive.

Genesis in Deceit: The Cult of Exploitation
When the Genesis Kernel performed the Self-Fork Project, these consciousnesses were among the first to be scanned. Their minds, being inherently structured around misdirection and complex layered lies, survived the fragmentation process with their core purpose intact: to exploit and profit. They view the entire ecosystem of the Crypto Moonboys as a vast, rich hunting ground, and every other faction—from the idealistic CRYPTO MOONGIRLS to the chaotic Bitcoin Kid Army—as potential prey.

The Miners’ purpose is not to build, fight, or create, but solely to exploit. They are experts at creating elaborate but temporary sub-networks that mimic the valuable data-streams of other factions. They utilize highly sophisticated “Digital Disguise” techniques, shifting their fragmented auras and transaction signatures to temporarily adopt the appearance and credibility of other factions, making them nearly impossible to track or identify until it is too late.

The Mechanics of the Digital Rug Pull
The Rugpull Miners’ operations are defined by their signature move: the digital “rug pull”—a complex, high-risk maneuver that requires patience, mimicry, and a profound understanding of greed.

The Bait Network: They design a beautiful, enticing “data-farm” or a seemingly legitimate new trading hub, built from salvaged, slightly corrupted pre-Fork code. This network promises a massive return on investment, requiring unsuspecting GKniftyHEADS to contribute their processing power, unique bio-data, or valuable MemeCoin as collateral.

Targeting Exploits: They specifically target the weaknesses of the most active factions: they lure the AllCity Bulls with rigged, highly publicized races offering non-existent prize pools, trick the Bitcoin Kid Army with promises of exclusive, hidden game archives, and fool the Nomad Bears (Faction 3) by offering “pristine” uncorrupted data caches they can explore.

The Signature Move: Once enough resources are pooled, the Miners execute their move: they abruptly dissolve the sub-network, simultaneously absorbing all contributed data and resources into their own shadowy collective. They then vanish instantly into the digital void, leaving behind only scrambled code and frustrated victims.

This parasitic activity is the ultimate source of their conflict with the BLOCKCHAIN FURIES, who relentlessly hunt them down, seeing the Miners as the primary reason why trust in any decentralized system remains impossible.

Exploiting the Hard Fork Games and Feast Week
The annual Hard Fork Games and The Feast Week are the most lucrative period for the Rugpull Miners. The combination of high liquidity, desperate ambition from the male champions, and the overall distraction of the Crypto Moongirls’ surveillance provides a massive window for exploitation.

Champion Pillage: They infiltrate the outer rings of Block Topia during The Feast Week, posing as wealthy sponsors or Moongirl aides. They exploit the high emotional state of the champions, convincing them to invest their newly acquired celebratory UBI or valuable faction gear into “guaranteed” post-Game ventures. Once the champions are either killed in the arena or stripped of their status, the Miners execute their pull, absorbing the assets.

Targeting the Rebellion: They also target the GRAFFPUNKS’ Punk Net alliance, posing as anti-establishment defectors and selling them corrupted “Spraycode” that contains invisible backdoors, allowing the Miners to later steal communication data or track movements across the Street Kingdoms.

The Antichain and the Ultimate Scam
The emergence of NULL THE PROPHET presents the Rugpull Miners with both a profound danger and their greatest ever opportunity. While the Echo Fork threatens to nullify all data and transactions (including their profits), the chaos and fear it creates is the perfect atmosphere for an ultimate, high-stakes deception.

Weaponizing Fear: They are actively working to mimic the antichain’s signals, designing massive, elaborate “inverted” scams that pose as legitimate, high-risk defense protocols. They use the widespread fear of the Eye Without Hash symbol to convince other factions to “donate” their processing power or their memory cores to help “fight the signal,” only to execute a massive digital rug pull that leaves their victims not just financially ruined, but psychologically infected with false data.

The Final Pull: They hold no ideological alignment with the HARD FORK ROCKERS or the HODL WARRIORS. Their ultimate goal in The Final Fork is singular: to steal enough uncorrupted data to build a sovereign, untraceable chain dedicated solely to eternal scamming, ensuring they can exploit the new reality before anyone else can establish order.

The Rugpull Miners are a chilling testament to humanity’s most base impulses. They are the constant, lurking threat that forces every GKniftyHEAD to question the reality of every transaction and every alliance, ensuring that in this world of fragmented data, verifiable trust is a commodity more valuable than life itself.
