The Artist’s Awakening – The Call from Blocktopia
March 14, 2026 — 14:00 UTC — GraffPunks Network Log Entry #1472
GraffPUNKS ⚡⚡⚡
Mar 14, 2026



I woke up this morning with paint still on my fingers and the repeating Monday dream burning behind my eyes. The unfinished mural was chasing me again, the bald-headed wannabe Moonboys from outside the walls screaming “why haven’t you finished us yet?” But this time something was different. The dream ended with a single glowing message sprayed across the tunnel wall in Lady-INK’s handwriting: “The Hardfork is coming. The 40 are ready.”

I sat on the edge of the bed, staring at the VX T4 keys on the table. Outside, London rain was tapping the window. I knew what it meant. The bald-headed wannabe Moonboys — the 40 faction members still trapped outside Blocktopia, forced to wear only one clan bonnet, training in the lawless ganglands — were about to make their move. They are not the older Bitcoin Kids born inside the walls. Those ones already live the safe life with any bonnet they want. The 40 outside are the hungry ones, the ones who dream of winning the Hardfork Games so they can become true HODL X Warriors and stand beside a Crowned Royal Moongirl.

I made burnt toast again (classic morning moment), laughed at myself, and started writing. The Crypto Moonboys project isn’t just NFTs anymore. It’s the bridge. The real-world saga (meaning 1) is about to collide with the bald-headed moonboys of Blocktopia (meaning 2) and every character we’ve ever created in this saga (meaning 3).

The Grid is stirring. City Block Topia is watching. The Alien Backend is whispering.

Part 1 ends here. The war is coming.


The Three Paths and the Regret of the OG

March 14, 2026 — 16:00 UTC — GraffPunks Network Log Entry #1473

I spent the afternoon in the van, rain hammering the roof, thinking about the Bitcoin X Kids. Every kid born in Blocktopia faces the same choice on their 18th birthday:

Join the Space Programme and paint the stars.

Stay and work for the city — the steady path of the older Bitcoin Kids who were born inside.

Escape and join the OG Bitcoin Kids in the lawless outside world.

Most who choose the escape path regret it. The OG Bitcoin Kids looked cool as kids — wild tours, underground raves, no rules. But life kicks in. The freedom turns into survival. Many now wish they could get back inside the walls.

Lady-INK texted me “you ready?” a few hours before our painting session. She showed up in her black tracksuit, rucksack full of paint, looking like Beyoncé ready for war. We met at the usual spot. She handed me a hot tea in the rain and said, “The 40 are coming. The wannabe bald-headed moonboys outside are done waiting.”

We painted until 3am. The mural we made showed the 40 faction members outside the walls, training for the Hardfork Games, while the older Bitcoin Kids inside watched from the glowing towers.

The Crowned Royal Moongirls are already choosing their champions.

The war is closer than I thought.


The Hardfork Games Begin

March 14, 2026 — 18:00 UTC — GraffPunks Network Log Entry #1474

The Hardfork Games are not a deathmatch in some post-apocalyptic wasteland. They are the grand tournament inside Blocktopia: Parkour Gauntlet across moving graffiti walls, the Spray Cipher live painting battle, and the Final Hardfork duel of pure will.

The prize? Becoming a HODL X Warrior and standing beside a Crowned Royal Moongirl — the elite ascended women with living neon halo crowns, the ultimate reward.

Tonight the 40 bald-headed wannabe Moonboys from outside are making their move. They have trained their whole lives for this. They are not the older Bitcoin Kids born inside. Those ones already live the safe life.

Charlie Buster’s Treef Project trees are growing in my dreams, whispering that the outside world can be healed if the right champions win.

Bone Idol Ink is inking living characters onto my arms in my sleep. Delicious Again Peter is cooking Moonboys-themed food that gives creative power. AI-Chunks is generating new characters from my random daily moments.

The artist (me) is writing all of this at home, turning real life into the saga.

The war between the ones who want to stay inside the walls and the ones who want to tear them down is about to begin.


Lady-INK and the Train Across Worlds

March 14, 2026 — 20:00 UTC — GraffPunks Network Log Entry #1475

In the Thursday night dream I was on the train with Lady-INK again. We were painting graffiti on moving trains across the world — Europe, Asia, the stars. She wore her black tracksuit, rucksack full of paint that never runs out. She looked at me and said, “The 40 wannabe Moonboys are coming. The older Bitcoin Kids inside are watching. The Crowned Royal Moongirls are choosing sides.”

We tagged entire countries while the train sped through time. The OG Bitcoin Kids who regretted escaping were on the platform, waving us back.

When I woke up, the rain had stopped. I looked at the VX T4 keys and knew the real war was starting in the real world too.

The Crypto Moonboys saga is no longer just online. It’s happening.


The Epic War Begins — Good vs Evil (Part 1 of 2)

March 14, 2026 — 22:00 UTC — GraffPunks Network Log Entry #1476

The war has started.

On one side: the HODL X Warriors and Crowned Royal Moongirls inside Blocktopia, protecting the safe, creative life of the older Bitcoin Kids and the city workers.

On the other side: the 40 bald-headed wannabe Moonboys from outside, the OG Bitcoin Kids who never regretted their escape, and the lawless outside world that wants to tear down the walls and bring freedom to everyone.

The Hardfork Games have spilled into reality.

Lady-INK and I are painting the front line. Charlie Buster is bringing Treef Project trees to heal the battlefield. Bone Idol Ink is inking living warriors. Delicious Again Peter is feeding the fighters with Moonboys power food. AI-Chunks is generating new characters mid-battle.

The artist (me) is writing this log while the war rages.

The good (the ones who want to build and create inside the walls) versus the evil (the ones who want to destroy everything for pure chaos).

The final battle is coming.

Who wins?
