Leake Street (also known as the Banksy Tunnel) is a 300-meter-long road tunnel in Lambeth, London, where Graffiti is legal and actively promoted. It is a well-known spot for street artists to showcase their work.

Style and Themes

Become a Medium member
Buster’s work is characterized by “Busterisms,” a unique blend of cool comedy and wit that often uses fun, cartoonish visuals and hard-edged graphics to tackle deeper themes like humanity, politics, or current events. His pieces are known for their eye-catching use of color and meticulous detail, often featuring signature zigzag lines or double arrows in contrasting colors. He employs various materials, including spray paint, acrylics, paint pens, and stencils.

Career and Recognition

Apprenticeship: Buster grew up in Kent, England, in a family of artists, and has been painting since he could hold a crayon. He completed a 10-year apprenticeship with the “mighty Graffiti Kings London Collective”.
International Presence: His works are displayed in various exhibitions around the world and can be seen on streets across the UK.
Collaborations and Projects: He has collaborated with elite names in the art world and is the founder of the “No Ball Games” NFT collection.
Rising Demand: Due to his unique aesthetic and thought-provoking messages, there is a growing demand from collectors and tastemakers for his original artwork.
