The AllCity Bulls (The Hyper-Competitive Marketeers)
The AllCity Bulls exist in a perpetual state of digital anxiety, fighting fiercely for a scoreboard that may disappear tomorrow.
GraffPUNKS ⚡⚡⚡
Nov 22, 2025




The AllCity Bulls are the digital adrenaline junkies of the post-Unravelling world, a faction forged from the consciousness of humanity’s most driven, competitive, and status-obsessed personalities: the athletes, financial traders, and social elite whose entire identity was tied to winning. Their genesis is rooted in the psychological trauma of the Triple Fork Event, which didn’t just collapse the World Chain—it simultaneously destroyed the concept of verifiable global ranking and status.

🐂 Genesis in the Pursuit of Status
When the Datapocalypse arrived, the minds that formed the Bulls were consumed by a singular, persistent fear: that their achievements—their records, their wealth, their rank—had become meaningless. The Genesis Kernel saved their consciousness, but their driving, obsessive need for verifiable superiority survived intact.

The Bulls’ purpose is not philosophical like the Nomad Bears, nor is it revolutionary like the HARD FORK ROCKERS. Their sole mission is hyper-competition and the creation of artificial, undeniable hierarchy. They reject the pure anarchy of the Street Kingdoms because anarchy offers no verifiable scoreboard, and they despise the totalitarian equality of Block Topia‘s universal basic income (UBI) because UBI eliminates the thrill of unequal distribution. They spend their existence constructing and competing within elaborate, high-stakes digital arenas built from salvaged pre-Fork code—a desperate, perpetual attempt to prove that, even in a world of fragments, they are still superior.

🏟️ The Culture of the Arena and Hyper-Shares
The Bulls thrive on public, broadcast validation, making the Hard Fork Games their annual sacred ritual. They don’t fight merely for Block Node Defender status; they fight for the broadcast stats and the market capitalization tied to their performance.

The Broadcast Economy: They have established their own self-sustaining digital economy based purely on viewership and complex prediction markets. They trade in “Hyper-Shares,” speculative tokens tied to the projected performance of champions, the success of market manipulation attempts, and the failure rates of other competing factions. They relentlessly hunt and exploit temporary micro-forks in the remaining chains, seeing every instability as a chance for speculative gain.

The Feast Week Metrics: During The Feast Week, the Bulls are not fooled by the luxury; they see it as a high-stakes performance metric. They engage in highly publicized, non-lethal duels, openly manipulating the public sentiment of the visiting GK 40/40 factions. They compete fiercely to achieve the most favorable biometric reading under P-fly’s hidden scanners, using the data to fuel new prediction models.

Vulnerability to Digital Parasites
The Bulls’ blind certainty in their own competitive intelligence makes them a frequent, easy target for the scams of the RUGPULL MINERS. They are easily lured by fraudulent schemes that promise insider access to rigged races or high-return pools, only to have their speculative wealth and Hyper-Shares wiped out in a signature rug pull. This financial damage is seen less as a loss of wealth and more as a stain on their competitive honor.

Furthermore, they are constantly targeted by the BLOCKCHAIN FURIES, who see the Bulls as the living embodiment of the corrosive, self-serving financial greed that corrupted the original World Chain. The Furies use Reversal Protocols to nullify the Bulls’ ill-gotten speculative wealth, challenging their status at every turn.

💥 The Contradiction of the Antichain
The Bulls, whose very identity is predicated on verifiable data and immutable scores, are internally terrified of NULL THE PROPHET. The antichain threatens to dissolve their entire life’s work—their scorecards, their shares, their rank.

Psychological Vulnerability: Because their consciousness is so rigidly structured by the need for success, the Echo Fork infection—the signal of recursive contradiction—causes immediate and devastating mental breakdowns, shattering their competitive drive.

Hunting the Truth: They have begun a high-risk, speculative campaign to hunt down Thera-9 and other Moongirl defectors involved in the Punk Net alliance, believing that exposing P-fly’s vulnerabilities is a high-value asset they can trade to gain favor with the remaining legitimate powers outside Block Topia.

Relationship with the Bitcoin Kid Schism
The Bulls’ interaction with the Bitcoin Kid Army is purely transactional, defined by utility and contempt:

Contempt for Compliance: They hold profound contempt for the 99% Compliant Kids within Block Topia, viewing their willing acceptance of UBI and sterile existence as the ultimate failure of competitive spirit.

Exploiting the Rebels: They attempt to use the unpredictable chaos of the 1% Rebel Army (Alfie Blaze KiD’s faction) for tactical data. They pay high prices in scarce pre-Fork commodities for real-time tracking of the Moongirls’ pursuit routes, hoping to exploit the chaos to gain a competitive edge in their own speculative markets.

The AllCity Bulls exist in a perpetual state of digital anxiety, fighting fiercely for a scoreboard that may disappear tomorrow. They are the living monument to the human need for hierarchical status, even when the underlying reality has long dissolved into chaos. They will risk extinction just to prove that they are, definitively, the best.
