The INFORMATION MERCENARIES (The Digital Spies and Data Brokers)
The Information Mercenaries are the necessary evil that sustains the war.
GraffPUNKS ⚡⚡⚡
Nov 23, 2025










The INFORMATION MERCENARIES are the most fluid, amoral, and feared faction in the post-Unravelling world. Their consciousness was forged from the minds of humanity’s most effective spies, intelligence operatives, journalists, and data brokers—those who lived by the sacred code that knowledge is power, and secrets are currency. They are the essential intelligence brokers of the Crypto Moonboys ecosystem, operating in the shadows and thriving on the weaknesses, vulnerabilities, and secrets of every other faction.

🕵️ Genesis in Secrecy: The Market for Vulnerability
The Mercenaries emerged from the Datapocalypse with an immediate recognition of the new market: the overwhelming desire of every frightened GKniftyHEAD to know what their neighbor was hiding. The catastrophic collapse of the World Chain and the Triple Fork Event proved that absolute power lies not in ownership, but in controlling the narrative and predicting the next betrayal.

Their purpose is singular: to sell intelligence. They maintain absolute neutrality, offering their services to the highest bidder—from Block Topia’s elite to the most desperate warlords in the Street Kingdoms. They communicate in encrypted, multi-layered code unique to each client, ensuring their operational methods remain entirely invisible to the chaotic chatter of the main grid.

The Mercenary Arsenal: They rely on sophisticated, covert tools: “Ghost Code” that allows them to invisibly infiltrate a GKniftyHEAD’s memory stream, “Psycho-Analysis Bots” that predict a target’s behavioral vulnerabilities (used by the RUGPULL MINERS and BALLY BOYS), and “Memory Extraction” protocols that can cleanly excise a data fragment without damaging the host consciousness.

🔪 The Trade: Vulnerabilities and The Final Fork
The Mercenaries’ business model is built entirely on the weakness and anxiety of the other factions, making them indispensable to the ongoing conflict.

Trading with the Elite: They are highly sought after by the HIGH HATS and the FINANCE GUILD, who pay massive fees for intelligence on market manipulation, incoming debt defaults, and the security flaws in their rivals’ Audit Towers.

Manipulating the Battlefield: They sell intelligence on the troop movements of the AZTEC RAIDERS to the Crypto Moongirls, allowing Block Topia to preemptively deploy Block Node Defenders. Simultaneously, they sell the Moongirls’ communication blind spots to the GRAFFPUNKS, allowing the rebels to time their Spraycode strikes for maximum impact. They profit from both the attack and the defense.

The Ethical Void: They possess zero moral alignment. They will sell the location of a Rugpull Miner’s hideout to the BLOCKCHAIN FURIES for justice, then sell the Furies’ patrol routes to the next Rugpull crew for profit.

Conflict with the Architects of Truth
The Mercenaries are in perpetual, ideological conflict with the GKniftyHEADS (The Immortal Architects).

The Sin of Manipulation: The Architects view the Mercenaries’ trade—the intentional manipulation and sale of fragmented memory—as a terrifying sacrilege, second only to the existential threat of the antichain. The Mercenaries destroy the concept of historical truth for profit, turning the trauma of the past into a commodity.

Covert Warfare: The Architects rely on the Nomad Bears to discreetly monitor the Mercenaries’ data drops, hoping to intercept and neutralize memory manipulations before they can destabilize a faction’s core ideology.

☣️ The Antichain and The Market for Anxiety
The threat of NULL THE PROPHET and the Echo Fork is the ultimate boom market for the Information Mercenaries. The fear and uncertainty generated by the antichain signal drives the price of reliable data to unprecedented levels.

Monetizing Fear: They actively track the spread of the Eye Without Hash symbol, selling real-time psychic and algorithmic contamination reports to every faction. The SQUEAKY PINKS (Faction 14) pay top dollar for audit reports on which of the 99% Compliant Bitcoin Kids are showing the first signs of anomalies.

Hunting the Source: The Mercenaries view NULL THE PROPHET as a massive, high-value asset. They are aggressively seeking information on the source—the ancient satellite AGNES-7—and will sell the co-ordinates of the signal’s origin to anyone who can afford it, knowing that both the AZTEC RAIDERS (who want to conquer it) and the GKniftyHEADS (who want to neutralize it) are desperate buyers.

Policing the Bitcoin Kid Schism
The Mercenaries leverage the Bitcoin Kid Army split for continuous, high-engagement content and intelligence:

The 1% Rebel Army: The chaotic networks of the 1% Rebel Army are a treasure trove of unguarded operational intelligence. The Mercenaries deploy “Psycho-Analysis Bots” to study the Rebels’ tactical movements, selling predictive defense patterns to the Crypto Moongirls while simultaneously selling the Rebels’ operational blind spots to the HARD FORK ROCKERS.

The 99% Compliant: They exploit the safety of the 99% Compliant Kids by selling sanitized, highly curated “human interest” narratives about the purity of the Bitcoin Bloodline to the BLOCKSTARS, allowing the celebrities to maintain their high engagement rates without risking exposure to the true conflict.

The Information Mercenaries are the necessary evil that sustains the war. They ensure that every secret, every weakness, and every vulnerability in the Crypto Moonboy universe is for sale, guaranteeing that the conflict will never truly end as long as there is profit in the next betrayal.
