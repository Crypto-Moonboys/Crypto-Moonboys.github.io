Game I: BLOCKFRONT…
🪙 “Reclaim the Chains.”
Year: 3008.
Location: Block Topia — the last matriarchal crypto stronghold.

Lore Expansion:
After the Great Fork shattered the unified blockchain, Block Topia was carved into gridlocked boroughs, each ruled by a different protocol cult. The Queen P-fly Regime, backed by AI drones and memory-deletion Graffiti, installed an authoritarian dream firewall known as the NULLNET. All Graffiti not registered to the Crown is scrubbed — physically and metaphysically.

The GraffPUNKS, rogue remnants of the old GK spraycode guilds, survive the underground. They've decrypted the Codex of the Fork, an ancient set of 1000 mythic GK tags that can alter the very code of reality.

As the Bitcoin KiD re-emerges from exile, players join the GraffPUNKS in a last-ditch push to reclaim the walls, block by block, using Echo-sigils, dreamspray tech, and tactical Graffiti warfare.

BlockFront is the war before the storm. It’s where you mark your name —or have it wiped from history.

Genre: Tactical Turn-Based Strategy / Base-Building P2E

Tone: Cyberpunk rebellion meets Graffiti warfare
