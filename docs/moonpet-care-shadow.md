# Public Moonpet daily care

Apply D1 migration 115 after 114, then deploy moonboys-api from the same revision. The existing half-hour schedule automatically covers every Moonpet profile. Existing Telegram login and Arcade XP adoption requirements apply. The public feature uses no beta roster or enable flag. No reset runs on deployment.

Feed, Play and Clean on the same hatched pet each UTC day. Accepted zero-XP actions count. Incomplete eligible days build Care Standing at the account level, capped at 1,540 units. Each full care day repays 44 units. Unhatched pets, selection changes, missing observations and known unavailable days are exempt from additional standing. All 48 half-hour samples and the closing midnight boundary must be present. A wholly unobserved day adds nothing. Register known outages before settlement in moonpet_care_shadow_exclusions with the UTC day and either the affected owner or *.

Settlement allows a full UTC grace day for delayed receipts and incident reporting. Awards use the latest standing settled before their UTC earning day began. At positive standing, the rate starts at 10% and reaches 25% at 770 units. Each new award uses its post-cap gross amount. Integer fractions carry across awards per owner. Previously earned XP remains permanent. The care console shows the actual amount withheld today. Players can report incorrect awards through the GKniftyHEADS link in the care guide.

Migration 115 is additive. Only reward writes from the new Worker opt in to its database triggers. A Worker rollback stops new reductions. Preserve the receipt and standing tables when rolling back. Neither this release nor its rollback calls for reapplying the public-launch player reset.
