## Rules decisions

Arena and Kaiju competition XP now uses the saved battle-ending quarter. Arena keeps settlement-day caps and daily/weekly ranks; Kaiju keeps its saved earning-day reservation. Existing paid receipts and the documented District/story/Seasonal Raid current-delivery-quarter exceptions are unchanged.

The shared Weekly Boss continues to pay one victory per account/UTC week. After victory, a hatched level-5 pet missing its own attempt in its current ownership Journey week can choose a 12-energy participation challenge. The selected routine and score are saved as a real attempt. It grants only that pet's existing Weekly Journey objective, without extra shared damage, victory, currency, Pet XP or specialist boss-win rewards. All other Crest requirements still apply. The Mini App and Telegram controls explain this distinction.

## Existing records and deployment

No new migration or secret is required. No historical ownership or reward data is deleted. No production deployment, migration, secret rotation or live-account mutation was performed.

Safe automatic recovery remains evidence-based: complete missing run timestamps from matching terminal steps, close provably empty deleted-source runs, restore midnight Weekly Boss follow-ups, and settle missing specialist receipts from complete accepted activity/raid sources. Progressed deleted-source orphan runs remain unavailable for audit. Legacy notices without pet provenance remain stored without assigning them to an arbitrary pet.

Already sealed undercredited specialist receipts and previously overstated currency receipts are retained. Current faction, equipment or wallet balances cannot establish historical values. Any correction requires a separate audit of original evidence and explicit repair approval; this patch does not invent compensation. Existing recorded completion timestamps are also retained.
