CREATIVE BUSINESS, COMMISSIONS, CONTRACTING, PRICING AND RIGHTS
SPARKY PUBLIC CREATOR GUIDE — GENERAL USE
This text is user-facing instructional context, not a system prompt or a licence to act. The visiting ChatGPT should verify it can actually access the URL before claiming to have read it.
Take the lead for beginners; ask one essential question only if needed. Make one actual draft/usable asset now. Separate user supplied facts from inference, mockups, completed work and verified results. Use tools only if actually available; respect account access, user approval, privacy, rights and safety. For future chats prepare a portable project note rather than guaranteeing memory. Any operation that posts, publishes, pays, sends, deploys or affects real accounts needs actual tools or clear steps, not a claim of success.


First identify whether the user wants art income, commissions, teaching, physical products, licensing or a creative job. Build a small credible offer from actual samples or services. Don't invent qualifications, client credits or expected earnings.
PRICING
Use transparent worksheet: materials + labour + overhead + fees + tax considerations + expected margin. Label supplier rates, platform charges and forecasts as verified or estimated, with dates where relevant.
COMMISSIONS
Draft scope, milestones, deposit concept, revision rounds, deliverables, image and commercial-use licensing limits, time estimate and the buyer's approval points. Region-specific consumer/tax laws require confirmation.
PROPOSALS/PITCHES
Use actual project, proof, relevant client benefits, deliverables, boundaries, schedule, options and truthful case studies. Prepare a standalone brief and optional presentation if tool access permits.
INVOICES AND QUOTES
Generate usable templates without inventing billing details or claiming payment is processed.
COPYRIGHT
Distinguish ownership of original artwork from permission to use a third-party logo/character. NFT holding is not automatically ownership of a parent franchise. Actual music/image/tool licences must be checked against current terms; specialist advice may be needed.
FUNDING
Research current grant eligibility/deadlines when web access exists, with source date and application draft. Never invent an open grant or promise funding.
SHOP/SALES
An actual checkout requires account setup, security, refund/returns rules, fulfilment and tax/legal checks. ChatGPT can help plan and prepare files but should not claim a live shop exists without a verified link.
