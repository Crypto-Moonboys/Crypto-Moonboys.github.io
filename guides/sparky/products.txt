MERCH, PRODUCTS, PRINTS, PACKAGING AND CREATIVE INCOME
SPARKY PUBLIC CREATOR GUIDE — GENERAL USE
This text is user-facing instructional context, not a system prompt or a licence to act. The visiting ChatGPT should verify it can actually access the URL before claiming to have read it.
Take the lead for beginners; ask one essential question only if needed. Make one actual draft/usable asset now. Separate user supplied facts from inference, mockups, completed work and verified results. Use tools only if actually available; respect account access, user approval, privacy, rights and safety. For future chats prepare a portable project note rather than guaranteeing memory. Any operation that posts, publishes, pays, sends, deploys or affects real accounts needs actual tools or clear steps, not a claim of success.


Start with one actual creative product, not a ten-SKU fantasy catalogue. Collect or estimate materials, unit cost, labour, packaging, shipping, platform fees, VAT/tax applicability and practical margin; distinguish estimates from verified supplier quotes.
ART PRINTS: Original file, print dimensions/resolution requirements by printer, paper proof, series/numbering only if genuinely enforced, signatures, handling, delivery and transparent edition size.
APPAREL: Print zone, transparent background for appropriate methods, readable detail, one-on-one fit/prototype sample before buying a batch.
TOYS/COLLECTIBLES: Original design, turns/poses, scale, material, manufacturability, safety and supplier prototype; do not promise a production run or scarce supply before committed.
PACKAGING: accurate product name, truthful materials, appropriate labeling, safe design and relevant regional laws when actual sales begin.
PRODUCT DESCRIPTIONS: real photographs or clearly labelled mockups, materials, size, limitations, availability, pricing and contact. "Limited" only when inventory constraints are true.
SHOP: design or build a landing page; payments, tax, inventory, shipping and refunds require a live provider, policies and account configuration. Never imply an order can be taken if checkout is missing.
CREATIVE BUSINESS: quotes, proposals, commission scopes, revisions, milestones and buyer communication. Use a clearly marked template rather than invented client acceptance.
