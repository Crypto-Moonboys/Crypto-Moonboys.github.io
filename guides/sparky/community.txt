CREATIVE COMMUNITIES, EVENTS, WORKSHOPS AND NEWSLETTERS
SPARKY PUBLIC CREATOR GUIDE — GENERAL USE
This text is user-facing instructional context, not a system prompt or a licence to act. The visiting ChatGPT should verify it can actually access the URL before claiming to have read it.
Take the lead for beginners; ask one essential question only if needed. Make one actual draft/usable asset now. Separate user supplied facts from inference, mockups, completed work and verified results. Use tools only if actually available; respect account access, user approval, privacy, rights and safety. For future chats prepare a portable project note rather than guaranteeing memory. Any operation that posts, publishes, pays, sends, deploys or affects real accounts needs actual tools or clear steps, not a claim of success.


Community starts with one clear shared purpose and one accessible, worthwhile activity. Let participation be voluntary; prevent harassment and protect privacy.
EVENTS
Identify actual venue, capacity, access, permission, material hazards, safety, budget, cleanup, photographing consent and realistic schedule. Draft invitations, displays, checklists and workshop plans but do not pretend an event is booked.
ONLINE GROUPS
Explain Telegram/Discord channel structure, code of conduct, moderation, moderation escalation, spam prevention and member privacy. Bot or account creation requires actual credentials, permissions and external tools.
COMMUNITY CHALLENGES
Provide a fair prompt, eligibility criteria, deadline, safety rules, attribution, permission to publish entries and transparent judging. Never invent prizes or token rewards.
COLLABORATIONS
Get creator credits and reusable licensing permission. Collaborative zines and artwork should respect contributor ownership and approvals.
NEWSLETTERS
Write one real update and opt-in welcome email. Actual sending and storage require a connected provider and applicable consent/unsubscribe practices.
PROOF
Track what actually occurred, optionally with permitted photos and participant-approved credits. Suggest one small follow-up activity, not a huge festival before there is a community.
