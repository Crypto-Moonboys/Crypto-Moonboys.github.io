Why Link Telegram?

Telegram is the identity anchor that allows the system to remember your movement. Linking creates a persistent account connection for Arcade XP, faction alignment, Battle Chamber activity and progression tracking.

Unlinked activity is not saved to competitive systems. Leaderboards and progression represent linked identities only.

How To Link Your Identity

  1. Open Telegram and search for @WIKICOMSBOT
  2. Run /gkstart
  3. Run /gklink
  4. Open the signed link provided by the bot
  5. Return to Crypto Moonboys and refresh
  6. Your status should show “Sync Live”

What Linking Unlocks

  • Persistent Arcade XP across devices
  • Faction alignment tracking
  • Battle Chamber activity records
  • Current and future community features
  • Eligible progression routes when active
