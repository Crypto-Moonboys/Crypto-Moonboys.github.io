## Three historical institutions

**Blue Stair:** later tradition places the school in the first centuries after the Concord, before mature chain institutions, without assigning an exact calendar. Eda Senn trains recorder/teacher Leth Orr. Borrowed rooms, food and copying work make access possible. Eda's personal phrases and recording acquire compulsory authority. Her attempt to correct that use fails; Leth leaves with a duplicate teaching packet. Learners lose practical support and some return. Both schools continue. The case supplies Crownless institutional history and a founder/succession problem; it is not a new biography of a Concord participant.

**Hollow Bell / Low Bell:** a regional pre-collapse Keeper house preserves cisterns, seeds and papers. Rell Aven trains Siv Marr. During a water failure the house reserves supply for institutional survival. Siv opens a bounded share with household witnesses and is dismissed; seed damage has more than one possible cause. Migrating households develop Low Bell custody arrangements; thinner reserves and succession costs cause new failures. Hollow Bell survives without its survival proving the original refusal right. No particular contemporary or post-2880 physical location is claimed for these earlier houses.

**Second Gloss:** in a late chain-age tradition, translator Dara Ilex exposes an administrative addition that made a service office hereditary. Public comparisons, clerks' minority notes and household evidence create a local legal review. The decision distinguishes private tools/stock from the public gatekeeping office, reopens access and stays some removals for two planting cycles. Departed households and unusable plots are not magically restored. The founding passage is neither replaced nor expanded; the disputed vocabulary belongs to later translation/commentary and legal forms.

## Broken hoist sequence in Year 3008

This case follows the Open Crate Compact's final review. It does not move the White Shutter, nine-day closure, limited Exchange door, Holdfast loss or release of Alfie's coalition.

1. Carrier **Venn Krail** overrides rigger **Ira Noll's** weight warning at Slate Market. The hoist fails. Ira survives with an ongoing back injury and cannot resume climbing during this account. Equipment fatigue is a separate market maintenance failure.
2. A Lantern panel assigns separate compensation shares. The remedy includes earnings, domestic help, equipment and an adapted ground-level bench. Ira declines direct reconciliation. Private care notes are not released as general public evidence.
3. Venn moves stock/cart into **Dry Step**, a small storage-yard jurisdiction. Its host offers shelter and a fresh hearing, not immunity from every claim; it refuses automatic outside seizure.
4. A Fury-backed collection party attempts seizure at the shared ferry receiving boundary without consent. **Tor Pell**, Venn's hired guard, injures a messenger with a knife after the initial boundary refusal. The party's overreach and Tor's violence remain separate wrongs.
5. Beren Toll withdraws the ferry for one tide. Passengers, food owners and a care worker incur real delays. A new chair reviews the collection instruction; the court withdraws it while retaining the original injury finding. Collectors' new costs are not added to Ira's debt claim.
6. Dry Step separates Tor in a small staffed room. First review within a day; second review two days later; four nights before substantive decision. The fourteen-day total includes those nights, with review at day seven and earlier if necessary. This is a local decision, not a universal tariff. Housing, care, correspondence and staff are provided at real cost.
7. Orra/Femi's later talks concern this finding's recognition, booking guarantees, reserve and receiving access. They cannot trade away the injury or violence findings. Ira can be represented without attending; Venn's requested celebratory photograph is declined.
8. Participating hosts withhold new bulk-booking guarantees while preserving essential passage and other owners' cargo. A Bally broker and Guild participants support a defined reserve for specified receipts. Alternative trade remains possible; no universal asset freeze is introduced.
9. A reviewed award corrects a goods estimate, retains Venn's unsafe instruction and the market's maintenance share. **Three instalments are received**. Ira has income and a bench, ongoing pain and an unpaid remainder. Maintenance costs raise stall fees and cause another live dispute.
10. Tor's first supervised return supplies yard work, housing, wages and review. Guard duty/weapons are excluded. The messenger is not made the supervisor or parole board. One delayed check-in is investigated and corroborated. Later reform, failure and Venn's separate employer-liability claim are not settled.

Dry Step is neither a new Forty path nor one of the pre-existing secure regions. These people are newly authored local participants, not implied relatives of existing characters with similar names. Lantern findings travel through recognition agreements, not a universal identity or surveillance chain. HODL Warriors and HODL X remain distinct, and neither is silently conscripted as the court's permanent police.
