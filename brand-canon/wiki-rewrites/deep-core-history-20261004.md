## Locked continuity

- 2030: Great Concord authors the Covenant; Bitcoin is its provenance witness. Later proper names are not inserted into founding scripture.
- Unknown later date: Sacred Fork transmission. Sacred/Aether memory layer, World Chain and True Bitcoin Fork stay distinct.
- 2880: catastrophic World Chain Triple Fork. Chainfire describes the spreading process; Great Unravelling describes the wider period.
- By 2930: fewer than a dozen secure regions; Queens stronghold becomes the basis of Block Topia. The 2765 and “subsequent 2789” contradictions remain identified rather than silently corrected.
- 3008: principal saga. Bitcoin X Kids remain the inside engineered generation; Alfie's Bitcoin Kids remain escaped/outside rebels. HODL Warriors and HODL X Warriors stay distinct.
- Kernel origin, NULL's dated origin, universal upload metaphysics, complete Nexus inventory and Final Fork outcome remain unresolved. Local procedures and new lived histories do not claim to settle them.
- The 480 entrants and emissary colour classification belong to the specialised Games account. Arena and optimisation accounts are preserved as competing traditions. No universal live tournament bracket is introduced.
- Jodie, Aleema and the unnamed third rejected recruit retain their existing roles. No invented name or supposed W81 biography is assigned to the third recruit. Clone Harvest does not become every Moongirl's origin.

## New institutions and events to preserve

These entries are authored in this expansion and become the prose proposed for GK's final merge approval. They are not newly recovered archive evidence. No unsupported exact founding years are assigned.

**Sacred Chain:** Grey Pump Hearing; Borrowed Name Dispute; Open Margin Compact; Three-Custody Rule; Hollow Seal scandal; Etta Reed's patched coat; threshold table; return hearings and failed-effort shelves. Three-Custody requires independent failure arrangements, not merely three devices in one room. Privacy does not erase public accountability, and custody does not confer ownership of a person.

**Chainfire reconstruction:** Red Ledger House; Three Heirs case; Night of Open Shelves; Crossing House Three; provisional receipts; Unsettled Register; Common Kitchen agreements; later Continuation Oaths. None is a second global chain or a corrected date for the Triple Fork. The Continuation Oath is later political custom, not 2030 scripture.

**Kernel recovery:** Workshop Nine and the Unanswered Voice; Red Thread Fragment; No-Second-Copy Hearing; Empty Chair convention; Glassweather; Chorus problem; Anchor Cards; Orin Silt's refused reconstruction. Working forms describe present handling and do not establish a complete original Kernel architecture. A readable copy does not authorise destruction of another consciousness.

**Nexus custody:** Receiving Room, Ink Hall, Repair Bench, Listening Room and Warm Shelf are functions, not a published floor plan or an omniscient location claim. Quiet Table; Unfinished Wall; False Mother Piece; Blue Cup Register; Copy Ledger; Three Empty Frames; late-night print-room attribution dispute. Secrecy is protective and remains answerable. Sanctuary does not make a carrier's conduct immune from a hearing.

**Games aftermath:** departure, arrival, trial, placement and return accounts; Withdrawal Ledger; Returned Lists; waiting-hall contribution; disputed service records. These are local witness practices rather than promises that Block Topia honours a universal withdrawal right. Final Hardfork duel is a trial name, distinct from the future Final Fork.
