# Crypto Moonboys — main story entrance audit

16 November 2026. GK requests this existing route as the heart of the lore: an
extended back-cover opening, then a substantial connected world narrative,
mythology, realms and chronology. Implements the direct instruction recorded
as `GK-20261116-STORY-ENTRANCE`. Continues open PR #1800; no release authorised.

## Scope and method

Catalogue pass: all 471 HTML routes, recording titles, descriptions, article
headings, lengths, internal links and redirect status. That inventory contains
421 non-redirect routes; 179 match the broad lore/context screen. A catalogue
pass is not a claim to have semantically reconciled every paragraph of all 471
routes. Detailed synthesis focuses on the owning passages linked below and
on GK's three current command/army clarifications. Real token guides, NFT
metadata and biographies do not establish fictional physics or ancestry.

The result adds approximately 5,600 words of original connective narration
from existing lore, with 48 distinct story links. No new scene is presented as
an original W81 passage. The nine witnessed Books are untouched.

## Page treatment

The opening is an extended narrative pitch, followed by six long movements,
not a grid of character cards or dozens of short dictionary entries. Existing
styles, scripts, comments, citation components and canonical ownership markers
remain. The previous creator/reference paragraphs, exact identity/rights
statements, native contents and anchors remain readable in a closed native
`details` at the end. They are secondary reading, not the story's opening.

The existing SWARMSY clarification script inserts beside its existing anchor
inside that optional disclosure. Its browser contract is updated to open the
reference first and verify the panel there. Runtime code and product terms
are unchanged. Related-card source descriptions stop calling HODL Warriors a
faction and describe HODL WARS as the fictional war.
