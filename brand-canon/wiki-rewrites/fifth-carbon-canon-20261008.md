## New connecting story: The Fifth Carbon

A late Year 3008 copyist brings an inherited fragment to the Croydon account-keepers. Its fifth ink-on-carbon impression includes a witness name, a household's old request for passage and two mutually impossible time labels associated with the old Agent SAM story. Nobody demonstrates that the material is Samuel Blake's own original, that a 2030 Reset was the 2030 Concord, or that a surviving bit-pattern equals the originating person.

The duplicate is useful anyway. A later Block Topia service identity can be suspended by a machine that mistakes a copied **historical personhood claim** for a **present ownership assertion**. Members of the Crypto Moongirls bureaucracy, Squeaky Pinks, Games staff, Chain Scribes and Street Kingdoms must choose who is allowed to correct the administrative error. Their incentives conflict; none has total power.

**New recurring character: Bex Orren**, an adult Crypto Moongirls maintenance clerk (aged 34 in this Year 3008 episode). She controls a local record-queue workflow, **not** Queen Sarah, the Theta Protocols, the entire True Bitcoin Fork, a numbered religious faction or anyone's legal character IP. Her decision not to countersign an invalid transfer exposes both her courage and the dependence of her household on city services. The fact that one Moongirl refuses an order does not erase the movement's coercive history.

**Working narrative mechanism:** The Fifth Carbon travels as a copied physical object and a contested statement, never an infallible supernatural key. The Pinks can hold a disc but cannot certify the original era. A Scribe can make comparison copies but cannot restore a disappeared consciousness. The queen can open a local hearing but cannot retroactively control every archive. The Fifth Carbon establishes **disputed evidence**, not a new universal chain rule.

**New themes:** the institution's ability to harm without lying; a memorial code becoming a property deed; unity that cannot demand permanent enlistment; a ruler who must choose between a perfect-looking ledger and a service that works; old text prophecies sold as guaranteed future history; the difference between being recognised and being owned. Tone: adult, human, material, sometimes vicious; do not substitute empty action spectacle for consequences.
