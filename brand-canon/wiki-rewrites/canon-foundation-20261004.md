## Local geography and capability facts

The observed ground-level circuit is Exchange threshold → low Three Pump branch / raised embankment branch → North Sluice receiving approach → nearby Holdfast Depot. Working travel estimates are about one unladen walking hour to Three Pump and another two to the receiving approach, with access needs, rain, ice and load changing the result. Ferry far-bank trips are separate journeys. This is not a full map of Queens, an answer to every floating-city tradition or a means to travel instantaneously between London and New York.

City manufacture supplies the controlled firing/inspection of ceramic valve seats and a staffed, sealed clinic-concentrate line. Outside recovery supplies selected feedstock and casings. Scrap is not a substitute for every production stage. The medical object is fictional; no real dose, formulation or treatment guidance is added. Costs use work, meal and shift consequences rather than invented global currency rates.

The Exchange door requires an undisturbed source surface, a compared receiving arrangement and somebody able to stop arrivals in this incident. It cannot manufacture floor space, preserve temperature without the keepers' handling, erase obligations or guarantee the next landing. Holdfast copies preserve earlier words and cannot reproduce the lost responsive relation by assertion. Mnemonic, spiritual and cosmological claims remain open where the sources leave them open.
