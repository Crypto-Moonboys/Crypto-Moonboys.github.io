## What this document can and cannot decide

This companion preserves source material **without silently promoting it into settled truth**. In this archive, a witness can be wrong, a character can lie, a proposed game can be cancelled and two documents can describe incompatible endings. The published First Witness provides the convergence account; old posts remain evidence of what different writers or factions believed.

For **real project statements**, use `README.md`, `Crypto_Moonboys_Master_Source_of_Truth_v1.md`, published legal/licensing terms and implemented code. For **fictional continuity**, use GK-approved First Witness decisions and their later authorised expansions. For **raw-only claims**, record the filename, exact claim, and reason it remains open. Do not merge people because names resemble one another, treat every simulated/uploaded entity as a verified soul, or claim a future event has already happened.

## Distinct layers of identity and faction membership

- **Bitcoin Kids / the Bitcoin KiD Army:** Alfie Blaze and the outside/escaped rebellion. They are not the engineered Bitcoin X Kids.
- **Bitcoin X Kids:** the inside-city generation under Block Topia, with a distinct Hard Fork and champion hierarchy.
- **HODL X Warriors:** a separate champion/service tradition related to the Games and its duties. Do not relabel every HODL X champion as a current 1/1 creator-holder or merge them into the emergency muster.
- **HODL Warriors, Year 3008:** an extraordinary **collective defence protocol** when the Forty face an existential threat to memory and survival; not an ordinary selectable slot in the Forty.
- **HODL Warriors, current public brand:** the active creator-builders holding qualifying 1/1s under current published eligibility and IP terms; not the fictional defence protocol.
- **The Forty Paths:** forty is the civilisation model, **not proof of forty individually reconciled named rosters**. The authoritative reading register presently has **34 named cultures/readings and six unassigned positions**. This is a deliberate open register rather than six missing names to guess.

The six later **source-named** readings added to the existing 28 were JPEG Bloodline, Whale Lords, XRP Kids, PU55IES, Slam GiRL$ and Creepto Gals. The factual support is for the names and source relationships; their recent cultural practices were authorised **new fiction**, not rediscovered quotations from 2030 scripture. Candidates such as NoBallGames Legion, Burn Crews, Stencil Witches, Tag Lords and $DUST Protocol are **not** automatic remaining numbered positions. Nor are the Street Kingdoms, the GK Grid, the HODL emergency protocol, or the two distinct HODL title traditions fillers for roster arithmetic.

## Source-specific detailed strands not fully present in the digest

### Agent SAM / Samuel “HyroSAM” Blake — `W12.txt`

The raw **eight-part** narrative runs from a 2036 South Croydon rail-yard scene to a Neural Rack upload, the House of Rackinsats, the first digital raids, a purported “2030 Reset”, the leap toward the future Grid, a capture/vanishing, an underground war and a final cosmic tag. Its main source headings are:

1. London 2036: the night HyroSAM painted his own forever.
2. The Upload: the House feels the spark die and rise again.
3. The first digital raids; Agent SAM as unseen guardian.
4. The “2030 Reset”, hidden society and first whisper from 3008.
5. The leap into the future Grid and the House becoming a seed of Block Topia.
6. Vanishing and capture.
7. Underground war and attacks on a Reset Core.
8. Final cosmic tag and transition toward Block Topia.

**Source problem:** parts set the origin in **2036** but anticipate a **2030** Reset. That cannot become a clean historical sequence without an explicit decision on flashback, displaced chronology or narrator unreliability. **Identity problem:** this fictional person must never be conflated automatically with present-day automated wiki/SAM software, its authors, or the SWARMSY SPARKY operator. The current [Croydon page](../../wiki/croydon-tower-blocks.html) holds only part of this lineage; a dedicated Agent SAM article is a **candidate requiring editorial approval**, not an already-approved new route.
