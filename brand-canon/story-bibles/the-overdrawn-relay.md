# The Overdrawn Relay — new adult connecting fiction

Issue #1458 continuation on PR #1459, 8 October 2026. Primary owning scene:
`wiki/agent-sam.html#overdrawn-relay`; its household consequence is linked from
`wiki/house-of-rackinsats.html#inheritance` and Croydon. This is authored Year
3008 fiction, not a recovered episode in W12 or W13.

Nessa Vale, 42, runs a receiving kitchen using a copied Rackinsats relay mark.
Davit Cole, 51, maintains its equipment. They are new fictional adults. Cole
does not establish descent from Auntie Maeve Cole; shared surnames are not a
genealogy. The unnamed collector is an adult.

A SAM memorial reel attracts more guests than the kitchen can feed. An old
support instruction has acquired authority over the current fuel reserve.
Nessa delays Davit's interruption because her sister owes the collector money.
The transfer empties the reserve. The collector breaks two of Davit's fingers
when he disables the instruction. Nessa keeps the payment and her authorisation
in the daybook. Davit demands missed wages and refuses her request to hide the
assault. His grip remains impaired; debt, hunger and the lost shifts persist.

The kitchen retains the reel, with available portions chalked underneath.
There is no claim that SAM is resurrected or continuously operating in 3008,
that the relay has infinite funds, or that this household survived unchanged
for a millennium. This develops the source's kitchen/guardian promise and its
turn toward coercive authority while preserving the Fifth Carbon, Cut Ledger,
Necessary Monsters and existing chronology.

Keep the causal order: reel and arrivals → unsafe current permission → Nessa's
delay → payout and fuel refusal → Davit's interruption and assault → admitted
authorisation, wage demand and lasting impairment → bounded remembrance.
Later writing must not turn the admission into absolution, the collector into
a misunderstood helper or Davit's injury into an instantly repaired symbol.
