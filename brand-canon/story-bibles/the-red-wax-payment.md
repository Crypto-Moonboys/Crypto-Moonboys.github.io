# The Red Wax Payment — story bible

Newly authored adult connecting fiction for Issue #1458, 8 October 2026.
Owning scene: [Elder Codex-7](../../wiki/elder-codex-7.html#red-wax-payment).
This is authorised draft lore, not a recovered w25 passage or release approval.

## People, place and sequence

An adult carrier brings an incomplete local return-and-wage account to a paid
private reading in Year 3008. The unnamed adult host wants Codex-7's universal
peace ending. The Elder accepts a postponed reading of the carrier's sheet,
then seals a clean account as complete. His attraction to the host's private
invitation helps him minimise what his public signature permits.

The clerk withholds the carrier's promised fee because the extra sheet lacks
the completed delivery seal. Codex-7 offers his own fee while asking to keep the
explanation private. The carrier refuses the clean wall and leaves. Two lost
paid collection shifts remain lost. The original sheet remains with the carrier;
copies of the flattering account have already travelled.

Next morning the Elder records his fee, signature and the refused charge. His
replacement payment remains uncollected during the story. Copying and carriage
consume the remaining fee and leave an uncovered cost beside his name. Tarn
asks that the correction travel. The host's future invitation is declined for
mixed reasons; the missing worker remains unaccounted for.

## Consequences and boundaries

The Elder's reputation and appetite help a bad account travel. Recording his
part does not undo the withheld wages, lost shifts or the carrier's anger.
Neither an offer nor a correction establishes restitution. No sexual encounter
is asserted; the attraction and invitation concern adults. No named real
person, new numbered faction, world-wide institution or magical seal is added.

The seal is a local delivery/account object. It does not redefine the Chain
Scribes' Uncorrupted Seal, authenticate all claims or cancel missing people.
The carrier's original remains separate from the public clean copy. Their
refusal must remain a refusal in subsequent revisions.
