# Black Dividend — Last Loading

Story development reference for the Year 3008 local continuation. Prepared 5 October 2026 from the GK-authorised sandbox batch based on `360a7041727b4c342f1f5d3cb67c3a1b90d857c4`. GK approved publication to main on 5 October 2026. This is a development bible, not a finished novel or screenplay.

## The story

A permanently injured carrier and her wife refuse a freight patron's paid version of their recovery. To get an ordinary replacement load through a receiving yard, they must work with a former guard who wants his late testimony to purchase a clean future. The private protection hire has four working days left on its local booking. Its captain intends to make refusing the next term more frightening than paying it.

The yard opens, the load arrives and owed wages are paid. The guard kills the captain after stopping an attack, when he has a chance to stop instead. The women get no repaired bodies, the patron retains business elsewhere, and a successful delivery has a deliberate killing in its history. The story closes the particular extortion arrangement rather than ending the world's war.

## Tone and point of view

Adult crime drama, bodily fatigue, desired and resented intimacy, money that arrives too late, obscene humour and attractive people doing ugly work. Every participant in the new scenes is over 21. Sex remains private and non-graphic in the current wiki prose. Violence is consequential; the final killing is deliberate and does not acquire a supernatural excuse.

Use three central viewpoints: Etta, Sella and Sevrin. Etta should not become a wound viewed through everybody else's conscience. Sella must have paid work, private desire and choices beyond being a wife who can always help. Sevrin knows the guard occupation from inside and wants an answer other people cannot honestly give him: that speaking late has cancelled being paid to frighten people earlier.

Avoid following every supporting character home in the first adaptation. Their perspectives remain available in the linked wiki references for later stories. The first film or novel can carry the financial pressure through Tala, the receiving choices through Imani, the material work through Suri Clamp and the public version through Lio. Keeping those roles distinct protects the actual chain of decisions.

## The selected continuity

The original Black Dividend sequence follows the corrected Rack Three news and Red Ledger Night. Night Cover genuinely stops an unrelated robbery. Its imposed fee then makes refusal expensive. Cinder Shed burns on Rovan's decision after Kesh demands that refusals stop. Etta is badly injured; Teren is killed on Rovan's order; Sevrin gives his later account. Aster ends her affair and suspends the future reserve extension. Those events are already established.

Split Current Night and its outside receiving delays also precede Last Loading. Beren's earlier three-week exclusion has elapsed. The terminal story's four working days are the remaining interval on a specific Nine Rung booking, not the length of Black Dividend's whole history or a new universal local calendar.

The opening phase's statements that Rovan remains dangerous concern a time when he was alive. Last Loading advances that history. It does not erase the earlier incomplete outcome. The final death is a newly authored continuation, not a recovered archive answer. No mass HODL activation, city escape, new faction, complete mind reconstruction or Final Fork resolution occurs.

## The material rules

1. A guard's past useful work can be genuine while later charges are coercive. Removing the lie must not erase the delivery that made it persuasive.
2. Nine Rung can release booked freight and refuse a future receiving hire. It cannot abolish every armed crew outside its premises.
3. Etta's adapted task is bounded. Another worker must finish the comparison when fatigue makes the rest unreliable.
4. The alternative is one modest load funded by actual customer advances and stated contributions. It cannot absorb all the larger yard's trade.
5. Kesh's particular receiving advance is within the hosts' and office's reach. He authorises the supported wage offset to close that freight account. The earlier reserve payment and private dividends do not magically return.
6. A recording carries observed statements and correction. No recording reveals unwitnessed final thoughts or makes every listener agree.
7. The ending cannot use the Sacred Chain to restore Etta's hand, Teren, Sena or a complete consciousness. No NULL excuse converts human choices into innocence.
8. Drinking water remains separate from commercial exclusion. The local refusal is not a siege starving all people in guard coats.

## What this story conclusively closes

Rovan's life and the current Night Cover hire at Nine Rung. The local forced non-use fee. The particular replacement delivery. The supported remaining earned wages. Sevrin's attempt to become a clean witness through somebody else's preferred version. The women’s decision about the proposed settlement and their right to keep their private household outside the case.
