# The Cut Ledger — authored adult fiction

8 October 2026. Author: Codex, under GK's complete reconciliation task and adult
tone instruction. Sources supply institutions, not these new events. This is a
local Year 3008 continuation; it does not replace Fifth Carbon, Necessary
Monsters, Black Dividend, scripture or any earlier life.

## Cast and fixed consequences

Dara Venn, 38: a former local Crypto Moongirls recovery-detail leader. She wants
escape and leverage, not absolution. She assaults an adult returned woman and
falsifies the injury record; that lie supports her household and later justifies
targeting the victim's adult partner. Dara steals transfer tallies and removes
the authorisation strip naming her. She defects with real evidence and an
incriminating omission. Her fingers are broken during the Cold Choir escape.
Her victim remains unnamed and refuses the court's demand for gratitude.

Lysa Rook, 44: Cold Choir host, bereaved believer in the Great Consensus and
fuel skimmer. Her genuine devotion coexists with patronage and coercion. She
wants the sanctuary to survive and her own influence to survive with it. She
trades Dara a bed for an exhibition of the scar, then funds an armed escort
toward the district Dara fled. Her guards injure Dara. The public tallies cost
the shrine its patron; worship continues amid a fuel shortage and internal
scrutiny. No implied automatic conversion, supernatural punishment or new sect
rank follows.

An unnamed adult guest opens the door and loses his bed. He does not become a
rebel recruit. Dara gives him part of the stolen offerings and refuses the
comfortable claim that this pays the debt. Keep the injury and loss alive in
future scenes; do not make the escape costless or turn Dara into a clean hero.

Sarah receives an incomplete ledger, permits suspension of a local supervisor,
and leaves the recovery detail operating under another signature. This is a
limited political choice, not a regime-wide reform, new royal power, wholesale
abolition of the Hard Fork programme or rewrite of Necessary Monsters.

## Objects and institutions

Cut Ledger: copied local transfer tallies with Dara's strip cut away. No magical
key, infallible testimony or global access. A damaged custody trail can contain
real evidence; the omission remains a reason to challenge the carrier.

Cold Choir: a small receiving house nicknamed for its austere rites. A local
arrangement inside the existing Great Consensus tradition, not a forty-first
faction, universal headquarters or a new witnessed 2030 law.

The exact relative placement to Fifth Carbon and other 3008 local episodes is
open. Do not invent an annual Games date, a new catastrophe or a Final Fork
outcome to place it. Real people and deployed products acquire no biography,
rights, rewards or new capabilities through this story.
