What Is Crypto Moonboys?

Crypto Moonboys is the Graffiti Kings public creator umbrella. The Wiki is one public knowledge layer inside a wider identity, community and creation system.

The Core Truth

Crypto Moonboys is the creator umbrella. It is not a game, NFT project, token project, chatbot or a wiki-only brand. The Wiki is one public knowledge layer. The Arcade, Battle Chamber, Block Topia and SWARMSY are supporting layers in the same public creator system.

There are two valid routes:

  • Path 1: build a Moonboy character identity.
  • Path 2: keep an existing identity, business, product, brand or project and use SWARMSY.

Status-sensitive rewards, burns, evolution and 1/1 forging must only be described as live when the current runtime proves it.

Where It Comes From

Crypto Moonboys grows out of Graffiti Kings, a long-running street culture and creator network. The public story sits on top of that heritage, but the current positioning is about identity, visibility, ownership, community, proof and sustained creative momentum.

What The Wiki Does

  • Explains the public canon and cultural context.
  • Helps readers understand the two paths clearly.
  • Documents the historical lore without turning lore into live runtime truth.
  • Supports discovery across the broader creator umbrella.

The Supporting Layers
