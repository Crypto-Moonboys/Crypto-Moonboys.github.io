Two Valid Routes

There are two ways into the Crypto Moonboys creator umbrella:

  • Path 1: build a Moonboy character identity.
  • Path 2: keep an existing identity, business, product, brand or project and use SWARMSY.

Path 1 is for creators who want to build a fresh identity around a Moonboy character. Path 2 is for people who already have something established and want to keep it while building momentum.

What Joining Means

Joining is about identity, visibility, ownership, community, proof and sustained creative momentum. It is not a promise of live rewards, live forging or automatic role assignment.

Historical HODL lore may appear on the site, but it should not be read as current membership logic.
