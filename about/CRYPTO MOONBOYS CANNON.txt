Score is game performance.

## The Triple Fork Event

In 2880, the World Chain suffered the catastrophic Triple Fork Event.

This is the defining rupture of the Crypto Moonboys timeline.

A normal blockchain fork can create competing versions of a chain.

The Triple Fork did something worse.

It created three conflicting versions of reality.

Each fork claimed to be the true chain.

Each fork carried different histories, different ownership records, different identities, different balances, different citizenship proofs, different governance outcomes, and different versions of who had done what.

Suddenly, truth was no longer singular.

A person could exist on one chain and be erased on another.

A fortune could exist in one fork and vanish in the next.

A criminal could be guilty in one record and innocent in another.

A government could win an election in one version and lose in another.

Property could belong to three different people depending on which fork a city recognised.

Memories were duplicated, falsified, or deleted.

The World Chain had promised one reality.

The Triple Fork created three.

That is the horror of the event.

It did not just break the economy.

It broke agreement.

The old world entered what historians would later call **The Great Unravelling**.

**The Chainfire.**

# How Factions Work in the Living Web3 Wiki

The factions are not only lore objects.

They should connect to the product stack.

## Wiki

Each faction needs a clean wiki page.

That page should define:

Origin.
Role.
Ideology.
Visual identity.
Known allies.
Known enemies.
Key characters.
Related locations.
Game relevance.
Current live mechanics, if any.
Planned future mechanics, clearly labelled.

## Arcade

Faction effects can exist in arcade games where wired.

But the project must not invent final mechanics unless they are documented and live.

A faction page can say:

“This faction is associated with speed, stealth, repair, defence, corruption, or territory.”

But it should not claim exact bonuses unless they exist in the current implementation.

## XP

Faction XP is separate from Arcade XP.

That distinction matters.

Arcade XP gates Block Topia.

Faction XP should relate to faction alignment, status, or future faction progress where wired.

Do not blur them.

## Battle Chamber

The Battle Chamber is where faction movement can become visible.

It can show:

Faction activity.
Faction standings.
Faction signals.
Faction missions.
Faction conflicts.
Faction-related XP/status.
Faction season events where live.

This makes the factions feel alive.

## Block Topia

Future Block Topia / City Block Topia layers can turn factions into territory mechanics.

Zones can be controlled, tagged, contested, corrupted, liberated, or locked.

Factions can influence areas.

Missions can be faction-specific.

Wiki pages can unlock from faction actions.

This is where the faction system can become playable.

## NFTs and IP

NFTs can act as faction identity objects, access points, character anchors, artefacts, or future creator/IP routes.

But keep the wording controlled.

No guaranteed financial claims.

No passive income framing.

Use:

Identity.
Access.
Artefact.
Future utility.
Creator path.
Faction belonging.
Possible IP expansion.

# How to Write Future Faction Pages

Every faction page should follow a consistent structure.

## 1. Faction Name

Example:

**The GraffPUNKS**

## 2. Short Identity Line

Example:

Digital renegades of the Grid. Memory fighters. Wall-code rebels.

## 3. One-Paragraph Summary

Explain who they are in plain English.

## 4. Core Belief

Every faction needs a belief.

Example:

The GraffPUNKS believe the wall remembers what the chain deletes.

## 5. Role in the 3008 Conflict

Explain how they fit into Block Topia vs Street Kingdoms vs Null Prophet.

## 6. Strengths

List practical and symbolic strengths.

## 7. Weaknesses

Give them limits.

A faction without weakness becomes boring.

## 8. Allies

Likely allies.

## 9. Enemies

Likely enemies.

## 10. Game / Wiki Role

Explain current live role if verified.

If planned, label as planned.

## 11. Key Quote

Give each faction a strong line.

Example:

“When the chain forgets, the wall speaks.”

This structure will keep the wiki clean.

# Faction Writing Rules

To stop drift, use these rules:

Do not make every faction heroic.

Do not make every faction evil.

Do not make every faction sound like GraffPUNKS.

Do not invent mechanics unless they are wired.

Do not overclaim live faction wars if not live.

Do not blur Faction XP with Arcade XP.

Do not make Null Prophet just another team.

Do not write factions as only NFT traits.

Do not make faction pages pure manifesto.

Give each faction:

A job.
A belief.
A weakness.
A conflict role.
A future game direction.

That will make the world feel real.
