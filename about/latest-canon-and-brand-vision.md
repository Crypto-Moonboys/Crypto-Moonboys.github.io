# Crypto Moonboys Latest Canon And Brand Vision

Status: working public canon and brand vision brief  
Updated: 2026-10-08 (October lore reconciliation cross-reference; brand model unchanged)  
Sources: current repository README, Master Source of Truth, current SWARMSY positioning, protected First Witness corpus and historical W81 source provenance

This file condenses the current brand direction into one practical source for writers, agents, developers and community builders.

It does not replace published legal terms, live runtime code, community approval, or SAM canon promotion rules. It defines the current public direction: what Crypto Moonboys is, who it helps, what users do now, how the system works, why it matters, and how the lore/IP model should be explained.

## One-Line Truth

Crypto Moonboys is the Graffiti Kings public creator umbrella and shared cultural world for helping people build identity, momentum, community and owned creative presence.

The shortest operating line remains:

**One umbrella. Two paths. Build identity. Build momentum. Turn the spark into culture.**

Crypto Moonboys is not just a game, NFT collection, wiki, token project, chatbot, metaverse or lore archive. Those are layers inside the wider system.

## The Brand Vision

Crypto Moonboys takes the Graffiti Kings street-culture legacy and turns it into a public creator universe where users are not only spectators.

The goal is to give people a route to build with the IP, not just look at it. The direction is closer to a community character-IP movement than a normal collection: users can build identities, stories, products, media, campaigns and creator worlds around assigned or approved Moonboy identities under the exact published terms.

The useful reference points are:

- Bored Ape Yacht Club as an example of character ownership becoming a user-built cultural layer.
- Gorillaz as an example of fictional character identity becoming music, media, merch, story and public culture.
- Graffiti Kings as the real-world root: street culture, public signal, repetition, crew identity, and turning raw visibility into reputation.

Crypto Moonboys should not copy those models blindly. The point is to make a Graffiti Kings version: rougher, more open, more creator-led, more rooted in street art, Web3 ownership, public proof, lore, gaming, AI tools and community momentum.

The simplest user-facing promise is:

**Build a Moonboy identity, or bring the identity you already have. Use the system to make it recognisable, active and hard to forget.**

## Who It Is For

Crypto Moonboys has two valid entry paths.

| Path | Who it is for | User outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Path 1: Build a Moonboy identity | People who want a new character, avatar, alter ego, mascot, faction identity or creator-world anchor | A Moonboy becomes a recognisable identity that can become content, products, stories, games, merch, community and a creator world |
| Path 2: Keep your existing identity | Artists, graffiti writers, musicians, brands, product creators, businesses, organisations, public personalities and unfinished projects | The user keeps their own name, project or brand and uses SWARMSY/SPARKY to define, plan, publish, prove and grow it |

Path 1 is not mandatory. A user does not need a Moonboy, NFT, faction or arcade score to use the wider creator system.

Path 2 is not a side note. It is a full route for people who already have something real and need help turning it into public momentum.

## Why It Exists

The internet gave everyone a voice, but not everyone has an identity.

People can post every day and still be forgotten. They can launch an NFT, song, business, artwork, product or campaign and still fail to create a recognisable signal. AI makes this more intense: content becomes easier to generate, but distinct identity becomes harder to protect.

Crypto Moonboys exists because users need more than another feed, game or collectible. They need:

- a memorable public identity;
- a repeatable story and visual signal;
- a route from raw idea to real action;
- community amplification;
- proof that they did the work;
- ownership where the published terms support it;
- tools that help them stay active after the first burst of excitement;
- a way to build with culture rather than only consume it.

The old graffiti lesson still applies: a tag becomes powerful through identity, repetition, location, style, risk, memory and recognition. Crypto Moonboys turns that principle into a legal, digital, public and creator-owned system.

The cultural rule is:

**Break the mould, not the law.**

## The User IP Rule

The intended direction is user-built IP.

Use this language carefully:

- Users can build with assigned or approved Moonboy identities under the published terms.
- Qualifying 1/1 holders keep 100% of royalties generated from approved commercial use of that assigned character under the published scope.
- Crypto Moonboys and Graffiti Kings do not take a royalty percentage from qualifying 1/1 holder royalties under the current public rule.
- Creator ownership and canon approval are separate.
- A rejected canon submission does not remove ownership of the creator's underlying work.

Do not imply:

- anyone owns the entire Crypto Moonboys brand;
- anyone owns Graffiti Kings trademarks;
- anyone owns another holder's character;
- all lore automatically becomes official canon;
- all features are live just because they appear in old archive posts;
- users can use every asset in the parent IP without limits.

The public rule is:

**Your character. Your licence. Your royalties. Official canon is community-governed.**

**See the identity -> understand what it can become -> choose a path -> take action -> earn proof -> build momentum -> expand the universe.**

Weak order:

**XP -> NFT -> burn -> token -> 1/1 -> unexplained empire.**
