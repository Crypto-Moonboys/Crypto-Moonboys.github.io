Our Mission

The Mission

The mission of Crypto Moonboys is to help people build identity, visibility, ownership, community, proof and sustained creative momentum.

That means supporting creators who want a Moonboy identity, and supporting existing artists, businesses, products, brands and projects that want to keep their own identity and use SWARMSY.

What We Protect

  • Clear public truth over hype.
  • Readable pathways for new creators and established creators.
  • Historical lore that stays clearly labelled as lore.
  • Stable public copy that does not overclaim live rewards or current runtime features.
