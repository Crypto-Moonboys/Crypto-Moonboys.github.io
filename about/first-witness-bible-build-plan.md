## Purpose

This project creates the in-universe sacred text that people in the Year 3008 world commonly treat as their Bible.

It is not a replacement for the existing archive. The existing files are treated as surviving records written by people, factions, machines, archivists, propagandists and later historians attempting to reconstruct the past. Their errors, conflicting dates and incompatible explanations are useful evidence.

The First Witness project becomes the reconciliation layer.

It should explain why contradictory records exist, identify which claims are fixed, preserve ambiguity where the evidence is incomplete, and build a coherent route from the real-world/near-future origin in 2030 to the Year 3008 conflict without silently rewriting the archive.

## Core Canon Premise

In 2030, representatives of many world religions, philosophical traditions, secular humanists, artists, scientists, technologists and ordinary communities produce a common covenant.

The covenant does not declare every older religion false and does not create one central church. It defines a shared minimum set of principles humanity agrees must survive disagreement.

The completed text is cryptographically committed to Bitcoin as the First Witness.

Centuries later the authenticated text is carried or forked onto the Sacred Chain. The original Bitcoin witness remains the provenance anchor. The Sacred Chain becomes the living continuity layer.

Across centuries, groups interpret the same covenant differently. Those interpretive traditions later help shape the Forty Paths associated with the 40 GK factions.

The scripture is shared. Praxis is decentralised.

By 3008, the text is ancient, translated, argued over, partially lost, politically weaponised, painted on walls, encoded in faction customs and treated differently by every culture that inherited it.

Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze is not named directly in the 2030 text. Later readers interpret passages concerning a child/fire/signal figure through the events of 3008.

## Archive Rule

Never “fix” an old lore file by pretending it never existed.

Classify archive claims instead:

- **Witnessed / locked** — supported strongly enough to use as the reconstructed spine.
- **Probable** — consistent with several sources but not absolute.
- **Faction account** — credible as that faction’s version of history.
- **Late tradition** — appears much later than the event it describes.
- **Propaganda / controlled history** — may be intentionally shaped by authority or enemies.
- **Mythic memory** — culturally important even if not literal.
- **Disputed** — two or more surviving accounts materially conflict.
- **Unknown** — the Bible project must leave the matter open.

Contradiction becomes part of the world’s history.

## Current Anchors That Must Be Preserved

The build must continue to recognise the major established anchors already present across the repository and project files:

- Year 3008 as the principal future-story era.
- Block Topia as a major controlled city/grid civilisation.
- Street Kingdoms as outside-world territories with faction, black-market and graffiti-memory culture.
- GK Grid as a tactical/cultural network.
- Aether-Chain / Sacred Chain as a mythic memory/proof layer.
- World Chain as the later civilisation-scale dependency whose collapse becomes catastrophic.
- Triple Fork Event as a central rupture in surviving historical accounts.
- NULL / Antichain as erasure and anti-memory forces.
- Queen Sarah P-fly and the Moongirl order as major Block Topia authority traditions.
- Bitcoin X Kids inside Block Topia and Alfie Blaze’s Bitcoin Kids as an escaped/outside rebel lineage.
- HODL Warriors and HODL X Warriors must not be casually collapsed; their exact historical relationship requires a dedicated concordance.
- The 40 GK factions are distinct cultural/worldbuilding traditions, not forty copies of one faction.
- Graffiti, walls, tags and public marks function as memory, identity, proof and cultural continuity.
- Existing faction and character biographies remain source testimony even when chronology later proves imperfect.

## Canon Hierarchy For This Project

When sources disagree, use this working order:

1. Explicit instructions approved during the First Witness build.
2. First Witness pages already locked in earlier build steps.
3. Current `about/latest-canon-and-brand-vision.md`.
4. Current `about/w81-condensed-canon-digest.md`.
5. Current dedicated HODL WARS / character / faction bibles and recent handovers.
6. Original uploaded W81 and related project archive files.
7. Existing wiki articles and specialist bible JSON as surviving public archive.
8. Older promotional copy, speculative posts and obviously hype-led material.

A lower source can still preserve a true detail. It simply cannot override a stronger locked decision without an explicit reconciliation note.

## Individual Faction Commentary Expansion

After the faction-commentary hub exists, create faction pages one at a time.

Do not pre-create forty empty pages.

For every faction:
1. Pull all relevant archive mentions.
2. Compare current wiki page, W81 raw material, condensed canon and recent character/faction work.
3. Separate what the faction claims about itself from what rivals claim about it.
4. Identify its interpretation of the nine Books.
5. Give it at least one doctrine it gets right and one doctrine it arguably distorts.
6. Keep its culture recognisably different from every other faction.
7. Link its commentary back to the same shared scripture.

## Scripture Writing Rules

The nine canonical Books must read as if written in 2030, not as lore exposition written by somebody who already knows 3008.

Therefore the scripture must not directly name:
- Block Topia;
- Queen Sarah P-fly;
- NULL THE PROPHET;
- Alfie Blaze;
- Bitcoin Kids;
- Bitcoin X Kids;
- GraffPUNKS;
- Hard Fork Games;
- Triple Fork Event;
- World Chain;
- named 3008 factions.

Later commentaries may connect those names to the scripture.

Avoid pseudo-biblical filler. Use clear, memorable language that can plausibly survive quotation, translation, graffiti, song, ritual, argument and political misuse for nearly a thousand years.

Crypto vocabulary is used sparingly. The technology should support the philosophy, not replace it.

## Historical Writing Rules

Historical pages can be explicit about later names and events, but must distinguish:
- contemporary record;
- later reconstruction;
- faction tradition;
- myth;
- propaganda;
- present First Witness reconstruction.

When an exact date is unsupported, do not fabricate one.

Use phrases such as:
- “the surviving record places this after…”
- “later Chain Scribe copies date the event differently…”
- “Block Topia records claim…”
- “Street Kingdom oral accounts preserve another version…”
- “the First Witness reconstruction leaves the year open…”

## Linking Rules

Every completed First Witness subpage should link:
- back to `/wiki/the-first-witness.html`;
- to the previous and next completed First Witness page;
- to 2–6 relevant existing wiki pages;
- to the relevant canon/history page when a distinction matters.

The hub must never display a clickable link to a page that has not been created.

## Update Rules For Existing Wiki Pages

Do not mass-rewrite existing lore pages immediately.

As First Witness sections become locked:
- add a small “First Witness concordance” note to affected pages where useful;
- link readers to the reconciled page;
- retain the original account as archive testimony unless it creates a dangerous real-world false claim;
- only replace old prose when it is clearly presented as current canonical fact and directly contradicts a newly locked First Witness decision.

This preserves the feeling that the wiki contains a thousand-year archive rather than one perfectly edited corporate database.

## Build Workflow For Every Section

For each new section:

1. Search project uploads and repository canon for the section’s names, events and contradictions.
2. Build a source matrix: agreement, disagreement, dates, terminology and bias.
3. Decide what is locked, probable, disputed and unknown.
4. Draft the section.
5. Check against all previously locked First Witness pages.
6. Create the wiki subpage on a dedicated branch.
7. Add its slug to `brand-canon/approved-pages.json`.
8. Add the new link to `/wiki/the-first-witness.html`.
9. Add relevant cross-links from existing pages only where the new page improves navigation.
10. Run/review wiki CI and publish-gate outputs.
11. Merge only when the page is coherent and no existing locked First Witness fact is contradicted.
12. Move to the next numbered section.

## Definition Of Done

The project is complete when a reader can move from 2030 to 3008 and understand:

- what the shared scripture says;
- why it was witnessed on Bitcoin;
- how it reached the Sacred Chain;
- why one text produced forty traditions;
- why decentralisation survived as culture rather than uniform politics;
- how humanity later repeated the concentration-of-truth mistake through the World Chain;
- why the Triple Fork destroyed consensus;
- why walls and graffiti became rival memory systems;
- why HODL became larger than its original crypto meaning;
- why Alfie Blaze can be read as the Child of Fire without the 2030 text naming him;
- why NULL is a threat to every faction;
- why conflicting archives are expected rather than embarrassing;
- and why no single faction, ruler, priest, chain or generation is allowed to become the final keeper of human truth.
