HODL Warriors

Current Meaning

HODL Warriors are qualifying unique 1/1 holders and active creator-builders under the published creator terms. It is a boundary-based role, not a general label for every crypto holder.

A person does not receive the HODL Warrior role simply by holding crypto. The role only applies where the published qualifying 1/1 terms are met.

Historical Lore

The original HODL story, diamond-hands culture and WAGMI language are preserved here as historical lore. They help explain the movement's roots, but they do not replace the current role definition.

  • HODL is a long-running crypto meme and cultural signal.
  • Diamond-hands language is historical lore, not a membership shortcut.
  • WAGMI remains a community phrase, but it does not create automatic status.

Boundary

Only a qualifying unique 1/1 under the published creator terms grants the current HODL Warrior role. Anything else should be treated as community participation or historical reference, not live role assignment.
