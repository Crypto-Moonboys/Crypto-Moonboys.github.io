HOW TO PLAY
CRYPTO MOONBOY PETS

Adopt a Moonpet in Telegram, manage care and battle gear, develop six specialist progression tracks, run protected timed activities and jobs, enter Pet Arena and Kaiju battles, push through five-step Pet Runs and build toward the expanded region, room, modifier, relic and boss systems now supported by the roguelite backend.

The Three Progression Layers

Pet XP

Your main pet growth value. It controls pet level, stage, unlocks and pet leaderboard position.

Specialist XP

Six independent tracks record how your Moonpet is played: Care, Training, Adventure, Arena, Job and Bond.

Community XP

Accepted actions can add XP to your linked main Moonboys profile. This remains separate from Pet XP and specialist XP.

Protected rewards: reward-bearing actions now settle through a central authority. Pet XP is capped at 1,200 per UTC day and Community XP at 250 per UTC day, with stable idempotency keys and duplicate protection.

Six Specialist Progression Tracks

Care

Raised mainly through feed, play, clean, sleep and some training. Represents daily pet management.

Training

Raised through instant training and timed training claims.

Adventure

Raised through exploration, successful run steps and extracted runs.

Arena

Built by competitive combat actions and completed battle outcomes as those runtime hooks are used.

Job

Raised by short jobs and timed work claims. Job XP also determines your displayed job rank.

Bond

Raised by positive care actions and daily chest claims. Represents the long-term relationship with your Moonpet.

Each track has its own total, level calculation and daily award allowance. Daily counters reset by UTC day, while total specialist XP remains permanent.

Traits, Prestige And Long-Term Records

Accepted gameplay actions can add hidden trait progress. When a trait reaches its required threshold it appears in /petprogress. Traits are earned by repeated behaviour rather than purchased directly.

The runtime progression state stores prestige count, completed regions, regional mastery and event-chain records. The newer roguelite foundation adds persistent run regions, deterministic seeds, room state, run modifiers, relic ownership, run history and analytics. These are backend foundations for expansion; they do not mean every future region, relic, boss or player-facing control is already live.

Understanding Your Two Loadouts

Care Loadout: Food, Toy and Outfit. These improve care actions, currencies, Pet XP and selected run modifiers.

Battle Loadout: Armor, Weapon and Charm. These primarily affect Pet Arena attack, defense, crit, dodge and luck.

The loadouts are tracked independently. Changing one does not reset the other. Empty battle slots display none; there is no automatic starter Armor or starter Weapon.

Equipment Levels And Mastery

Eligible purchased or currently equipped items are registered in the equipment progression system. Each item can hold:

  • Item level and item XP.
  • Mastery XP earned when that item is relevant to accepted gameplay.
  • Mastery tier representing longer-term use.

Run /petgear to see recorded equipment progression. The command also repairs missing progression rows for items already equipped in your six loadout slots.

Equipment mastery awards are event-key protected. Repeated delivery of the same accepted action does not create duplicate specialist progression.

Pet Economy, Inventory And Crafting Materials

Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens are game currencies. They buy care upgrades and eligible battle gear through /petshop and /petbuy.

telegram_pet_inventory is now the sole item/material balance authority used by /petbag, item use, run choices and banked run items. Event records remain audit history rather than a second inventory ledger.

Consumables include moon_snack, energy_drink, clean_wipe, lucky_charm, style_patch and adventure_map.

Repeatable roguelite material and item rewards have service-level ceilings so later room and boss content cannot become an unlimited faucet. Crafting recipes and a player-facing crafting command remain future gameplay layers.

Timed Activities

Use /petstart sleep, /petstart train, /petstart work or /petstart explore. Check progress with /petactivity, finish with /petclaim, or stop with /petcancel.

The minimum claim time is five minutes. Sleep, work and explore cap at eight hours. Training caps at two hours. Longer valid sessions scale rewards up to their cap.

Timed training progresses Training XP. Timed work progresses Job XP. Exploration progresses Adventure XP. Cancellation awards nothing.

Session claims are hardened against claim/cancel/expiry races. If reward persistence succeeds but the caller sees an interruption, retries recover the original applied rewards, items, currencies and pet state instead of creating another award or replacing the result with zeroes.

Pet Arena

/petarena is separate from Kaiju Sticker Battle. Pet Arena unlocks at level 10 and uses level, Pet XP, condition, care modifiers and Battle Loadout gear.

Armor improves defense, weapons improve attack, and charms improve crit, dodge or luck. Private chats support app battles. Groups use matchmaking, ready-up, locked turns, battle IDs and server-side results.

Arena rewards settle through the same protected reward authority and remain subject to caps and duplicate protection.

Kaiju Sticker Battle

/petkaiju connects the Kaiju Sticker Battle card game to the bot. Private chat plays the app; groups can host, join or queue.

The app rolls one category from Power, Size, Attack, Defence, Speed or Legacy. Highest card stat wins. Kaiju rewards keep the protected daily repeat slots, conditional Energy payment and retry-safe settlement introduced by the anti-farm hardening.

Pet Run Engine And Roguelite Foundation

/petrun starts or resumes the current five-step Telegram run. Every step offers route choices with different rewards, costs and risk. Rewards remain unbanked until extraction or boss completion.

Successful steps progress Adventure XP. Extracting progresses Adventure XP again and can use the run rare-drop table. Failed runs lose unbanked loot but can retain a small Pet XP lesson.

Care gear and authoritative bag items can modify run risk and rewards. A lucky_charm can be consumed when it boosts a run.

The backend now supports region, difficulty, deterministic seed, room progress, run modifiers, relics, run history and analytics. Run room, boss and completion rewards must prove matching persisted state and flow through the central reward authority.

Run Modifiers, Relics And Bosses

Run Modifiers

Temporary run-only effects can be stored for a run and are removed at terminal transitions. Validation rejects XP, cap, currency and permanent-stat mutation effects.

Relics

Permanent relic ownership can be persisted with rarity, unlock time and effect metadata. Future relic reward bonuses must still pass through the protected reward authority.

Bosses

The starter roguelite boss foundation deliberately favors bounded materials, an evolution item and unique relic ownership instead of repeatable Pet XP or Community XP farming.

Daily Caps And Duplicate Protection

The game uses server-owned UTC daily counters for Community XP, Pet XP and specialist progression. Hitting one cap does not necessarily stop every other type of reward.

Progression-bearing actions use stable event/idempotency keys. Event and Kaiju repeat rewards use protected daily slots, activity settlements are recoverable, item awards/consumption use one authoritative inventory balance, and concurrent run terminal callbacks serialize against late room steps.

This means Telegram retries, repeated button delivery and API retries can repair interrupted work without multiplying progression.

Anti-farm rule: gameplay can remain repeatable after progression rewards taper. Unlimited play does not mean unlimited Pet XP, Community XP, materials or items.

Seasons, Missions And Leaderboards

Daily missions reset each UTC day. Weekly windows and automatic 90-day pet seasons roll over independently.

/petleaderboard ranks seasonal Pet XP. Specialist track levels, job rank, traits, equipment mastery, relic ownership and run-history data remain personal progression records rather than the current leaderboard score.
