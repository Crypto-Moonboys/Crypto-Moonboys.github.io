Understanding Your Two Loadouts

Care Loadout: Food, Toy and Outfit. These improve care actions, progression and selected run modifiers.

Battle Loadout: Armor, Weapon and Charm. These are bought through the Pet Arena Gear Shop and primarily affect Arena combat.

The loadouts are tracked independently. Changing one does not reset the other. Empty battle slots display none; there is no automatic starter Armor or starter Weapon.

Pet Economy

Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens are in-game currencies only. They buy Care Loadout upgrades and eligible Battle Loadout gear through /petshop, the Arena Gear Shop and /petbuy.

The pet bag is separate from both equipped loadouts. Items such as moon_snack, energy_drink, clean_wipe, lucky_charm, style_patch and adventure_map are inventory consumables.

Timed Activities

Use /petstart sleep, /petstart train, /petstart work or /petstart explore. Check progress with /petactivity, finish with /petclaim, or stop with /petcancel.

Sleep, work and explore cap at 8 hours. Training caps at 2 hours. Claims are capped and duplicate-safe; cancellation awards nothing.

Pet Arena

/petarena is separate from Kaiju Sticker Battle. Pet Arena unlocks at level 10 and uses level, Pet XP, needs, Care Loadout modifiers and Battle Loadout gear.

Open Gear Shop from the Arena menu. Armor improves defense, weapons improve attack, and charms improve crit, dodge or luck. Empty slots remain none.

Private chats support app battles. Groups match two players per battle ID with ready-up and turn locks. Rank matching, rewards, caps and duplicate protection are server-side.

Kaiju Sticker Battle

/petkaiju connects the Kaiju Sticker Battle card game to the bot. Private chat plays the app; groups can host, join or queue.

Pet Run Engine

/petrun starts or resumes a five-step roguelite run. Rewards remain unbanked until Extract or boss completion. Failed runs lose unbanked loot but can keep a small Pet XP lesson.

Care gear and bag items can modify runs. lucky_charm is consumed when it boosts a run.

Pet XP vs Community XP

Pet XP grows the pet and powers the pet leaderboard. Community XP reaches the linked profile only after server acceptance. Gameplay rewards are community/game progression only.

The Moonboys API decides equipment state, XP, cooldowns, caps and duplicate protection.

Seasons And Missions

Daily missions reset each UTC day. Weekly and automatic 90-day seasonal rankings roll over on their own.
