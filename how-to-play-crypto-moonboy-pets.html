Results And Rewards

Pet Economy

Care actions can drop Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens. These are in-game pet currencies only. They buy better food, toys and clothing through /petshop and /petbuy.

Pet XP unlocks higher shop tiers. Upgrades improve care actions, add pet XP bonuses or change what your pet has equipped. These upgrades stay inside pet progression and do not bypass Community XP caps.

The pet bag is separate from shop gear. Items like moon_snack, energy_drink, clean_wipe, lucky_charm, style_patch and adventure_map live in inventory and are used with /petuse.

Jobs And Events

/petwork gives the pet a short job like street_artist, courier, crystal_miner or vault_guard. Job and adventure rewards stay pet-only and respect the daily pet XP cap.

/petdaily opens a once-per-UTC-day chest, and /petevent handles random crate choices such as open, sell or ignore. Duplicate retries short-circuit safely.

Timed Activities

Crypto Moonboy Pets now support real sessions on top of the instant buttons. Use /petstart sleep, /petstart train, /petstart work or /petstart explore to make one pet busy, then use /petclaim, /petcancel or /petactivity.

Rewards scale with elapsed time: about 5 minutes gives a tiny payout, 30 minutes is normal, 2 hours is strong, and sleep/work/explore cap at 8 hours. Training caps at 2 hours because it gives stronger Pet XP. Claims still respect the daily Pet XP cap and any Community XP cap, and duplicate claims cannot double-award.

Examples: sleep restores energy and health, train grants Pet XP but drains needs, work pays Moon Gold with small Pet XP, and explore can find gold, crystals, an adventure map or small Pet XP. Cancelled sessions award nothing.

Moon Gold Trades

/pettrade is a risk/reward action using Moon Gold only. A winning trade can return more Moon Gold, a Crystal and pet XP; a losing trade spends the wager but still grants a tiny pet XP consolation. It never awards Community XP.

Pet Arena

/petarena is separate from Kaiju Sticker Battle. Pet Arena unlocks at level 10; lower-level pets see “Pet Arena unlocks at level 10. Keep growing your Moonpet.”

Pet Arena uses your Moonpet level, pet XP tier, health, energy, happiness/morale, cleanliness condition, equipped food, toy, outfit and new battle gear. Armor adds defense and damage reduction, weapons add attack, and charms add crit, dodge or luck. Outfit can add style/intimidation and toy can add speed/agility. Low energy reduces battle power; very low health blocks entry or heavily reduces power.

Main buttons are Find Pet Battle, Battle App Pet, My Arena Status, Cancel Queue and Gear Shop. Private chat resolves against an app pet. Telegram groups match two users per short battle_id; many different pairs can battle in the same group at once.

Rank buckets prefer fair matches: rookie level 10-14, scrapper 15-24, enforcer 25-39, cyber beast 40-69 and moon warlord 70+. Same bucket is preferred, nearby buckets are allowed, and Accept Any Rank can widen matchmaking. Underdogs earn bonus rewards on mismatch wins; high-level pets beating lower-level pets receive reduced rewards.

Rewards are server-side and idempotent: winners earn Moon Gold, pet XP and small Community XP; losers earn consolation pet XP; draws share small rewards. Existing daily pet XP and Community XP caps still apply, so retrying callbacks cannot double-award.

Kaiju Sticker Battle

/petkaiju wires the Kaiju Sticker Battle card game into the pet bot. In private chat, it starts a one-player battle against the app. In Telegram groups, it opens a table where another user can join, or the host can start against the app.

Each player chooses one kaiju card. The bot rolls one stat category: Power, Size, Attack, Defence, Speed or Legacy. Highest stat wins. Wins, draws and losses award capped pet rewards, and the small Community XP award goes through the same server cap and leaderboard path as other accepted pet XP events.

If a group table is busy, new players who tap Kaiju or run /petkaiju are added to the waiting queue. When the table clears, the queued players can start or join the next battle.

Pet Run Engine

/petrun starts or resumes a 24/7 roguelite run. Each run has five steps, each step offers three Telegram button choices such as fight, sneak, loot, rest, trade, gamble or boss, and the fifth step is the boss or major event.

Step rewards stay unbanked until you choose Extract or finish the boss. Push Deeper keeps the run going with higher risk and stronger reward multipliers. If the run fails, the pet keeps a small capped pet XP lesson, but unbanked run loot is lost.

Banked run rewards can include Pet XP, Moon Gold, Moon Crystals, Style Tokens and bag items. Pet XP respects the daily pet XP cap. Community XP is only considered on successful extract or completion, and it still goes through the existing capped server acceptance flow.

Gear and bag items matter during runs: hoverboard helps sneak/escape and gold, crown_jacket improves style and boss rewards, crystal_bowl improves survival/rest lines, adventure_map in your bag can improve run odds or rewards, and lucky_charm is consumed when it boosts a run.

Adventures And Alerts

/petadventure remains supported as an alias for /petrun, so older habits and buttons still lead into the current run loop.

/petnotify on lets the Telegram bot DM you when your pet needs food, cleaning, sleep, attention, missions, upgrades or an adventure. Alerts are opt-in and rate-limited. Use /petnotify off any time.

Pet XP vs Community XP

Pet XP grows the pet, unlocks stages and powers the pet-only leaderboard. Community XP is added to your existing Crypto Moonboys profile and appears on the current main XP leaderboard and graph after server acceptance. New pet jobs, events and inventory loops do not award Community XP; Pet Run Engine Community XP only happens after a successful extract or boss completion.

The server decides accepted XP, cooldowns, caps and duplicate protection. Telegram messages are the play surface, but the Moonboys API is the authority.

Seasons And Missions

Crypto Moonboy Pets runs on UTC daily, weekly and automatic 90-day seasons. Daily missions reset every UTC day. Weekly and seasonal ranks continue until their window rolls over automatically.

Rewards And Limits

Players can earn XP, pet stages, leaderboard ranks, titles, badges and seasonal memories. Gameplay rewards are community/game progression only.
