Qualify And Adopt

Earn 1,000 lifetime Arcade XP in the website Arcade using the same linked Telegram account. Community XP, Pet XP and your spendable Arcade balance are different scores. The first pet is free: qualifying keeps your Arcade XP. Existing pet owners keep access.

Open @WIKICOMSBOT and launch Moonpet OS. Telegram signs your session. On HOME, choose Activate EGGYONE to adopt the Secret Bot. If entry is locked, follow the Arcade link and earn the remaining XP shown, then refresh.

Incubate, Hatch And Breakout

In HOME’s Secret Bot Chamber, choose Warm Bot, Talk to Bot, Inspire Bot or Let It Rest. Each accepted action adds 2 signal, up to 12; at most 8 incubation actions count per pet per UTC day. Use at least three different types. These actions cost no currency or energy and have no timed cooldown. Accepted incubation care can earn one Growth Mark for that pet’s day, including after signal reaches 12.

HATCH BOT becomes available after at least 7 full days of incubation with 12 signal and three care types, or after 14 full days even without those signals. Read the live age and readiness in HOME; filling the meter alone does not shorten the wait. At 14 days hatching is available, but you still press HATCH BOT.

Hatching saves the young companion and opens the hatched gameplay routes. BREAKOUT is the EGGYONE hatch animation, followed by the shared WTFBOI presentation. It is not the canonical identity reveal. The identity remains UNKNOWN through Stages 0, 1 and 2, and becomes visible only at Stage 3, Elite Moonpet.

Hatching and formal evolution are separate: the client can show WTFBOI after hatching while PROFILE still lists Stage 0 until you meet and confirm the Stage 1 evolution requirements.

Pet Stages And Realistic Timing

Stage 0 is Secret Bot / EGGYONE. Pet XP raises your level; it does not automatically evolve your pet. In PROFILE, read the next gate and choose Evolve when all requirements are met. Stages must be completed in order.

  • Stage 1: Street Moonpet — level 5, at least 7 days owned, 7 Growth Marks and 1 Weekly Crest; spend 5 Scrap Metal.
  • Stage 2: Cyber Moonpet — level 20, at least 28 days owned, 21 Growth Marks and 3 Weekly Crests; 3 Alley King victories, 2 owned relics; spend 10 Scrap Metal and 3 Evolution Fragments.
  • Stage 3: Elite Moonpet — level 35, at least 84 days owned, 60 Growth Marks and 10 Weekly Crests; 8 Alley King victories, 5 owned relics; spend 20 Scrap Metal and 8 Evolution Fragments.
  • Stage 4: Moon Guardian — level 45, at least 182 days owned, 120 Growth Marks and 22 Weekly Crests; 12 Alley King victories, 8 owned relics; spend 36 Scrap Metal and 20 Evolution Fragments.
  • Stage 5: Legendary Moon Guardian — level 50, at least 365 days owned, 240 Growth Marks and 44 Weekly Crests; 15 Alley King victories, 10 owned relics; spend 60 Scrap Metal and 40 Evolution Fragments.

Age, Marks, Crests, boss victories and relic counts belong to that pet. Materials are consumed at each evolution; the other requirements are thresholds, not payments. Marks count distinct days and Crests count distinct pet Journey weeks. Later stages unlock jobs and events and improve weekly boss damage.

These are minimum ages, not promised completion dates: plan for at least four weeks to Cyber, twelve weeks to the identity reveal, about six months to Guardian and a year to Legendary. Missing objectives, boss wins or materials can take longer. Legendary completion also requires final evolution, 240 distinct-day Marks and 44 distinct-week Crests. The first evolution also needs a Weekly Crest, including two qualifying Daily Cache check-in days in the same pet Journey week; hatching alone does not supply it. There is no competition-season deadline for this lifetime Journey.

Pet Spaces, Switching And Deletion

New purchases allow three owned pet spaces across all seasons: Pet 1 is free after qualification; Pet 2 costs 500 spendable Arcade XP; Pet 3 costs 1,000 spendable Arcade XP. Buy them in order in PROFILE. Existing historical rosters are retained, including rosters above the new-purchase limit.

One pet is selected at a time. In PROFILE, select an owned pet to continue its needs, identity, XP, specialist tracks, Marks, Crests, memories and loadout. Currencies, inventory and owned gear are shared by the account; equipped choices belong to each pet. Switching does not refresh account cooldowns, daily attempts or caps. Finish, claim or cancel pending activities, runs and battles when the switch control asks you to.

Pets persist: no automatic seasonal reset, replacement or retirement. To start over voluntarily, choose Delete for a pet in PROFILE and confirm that pet. Settle saved rewards and finish or cancel blocking sessions first. Deletion permanently archives that pet and its progression. Free slot 1 immediately starts a new Level 1 egg without another Arcade XP charge. Deleting paid slot 2 or 3 locks that slot until you spend another 500 or 1,000 Arcade XP respectively; repurchasing starts a new pet. Deletion itself gives no refund. Previously awarded account balances and reward receipts remain during this beta, but the deleted pet cannot return to play.

Care Actions, Costs And Benefits

After hatching, use HOME’s Care panel. Lower hunger is better; higher energy, happiness and cleanliness are better. Needs change while you are away, so check them before a risky route. Each of Feed, Play, Clean, Sleep and Train has a 45-second account cooldown for that action. There is no currency fee for these buttons.

  • Feed — energy +4, hunger -28, happiness +2, cleanliness -2. Base reward: 6 Pet XP, 2 Community XP, 5 Moon Gold.
  • Play — energy -12, hunger +8, happiness +22, cleanliness -6. Base reward: 10 Pet XP, 3 Community XP, 7 Moon Gold, 1 Style Token.
  • Clean — energy -3, hunger +2, happiness +4, cleanliness +32. Base reward: 6 Pet XP, 2 Community XP, 4 Moon Gold.
  • Sleep — energy +36, hunger +10, happiness +1, cleanliness -2. Base reward: 5 Pet XP, 1 Community XP, 3 Moon Gold.
  • Train — energy -18, hunger +12, happiness +8, cleanliness -4. Base reward: 20 Pet XP, 6 Community XP, 10 Moon Gold, 1 Moon Crystal.

Train requires at least 18 energy. Sleep and Train are blocked while a background activity runs; Feed, Play and Clean remain available. Sleep in Care is instant, separate from timed Sleep in WORK. Gear can improve normal care benefits; stat limits, wallet limits and daily XP caps still apply. Care builds Care/Bond progress; training builds Training progress and eligible equipped gear can gain mastery.

Energy Drink in Care restores up to 28 energy: 10-minute cooldown, 3 uses per account per UTC day. Dance adds up to 18 happiness: 5-minute cooldown, 5 daily uses. Cuddles adds up to 8 happiness: 5-minute cooldown, 5 daily uses. These three work for eggs too, cost no currency or bag item and award no XP, currency, specialist progress or incubation signal. The bag’s Energy Drink is a separate consumable: it consumes one item for up to 22 energy and 6 Pet XP.

Seven Daily Missions And Claims

In MISSIONS, complete Feed; Train once in Care; Feed + Play + Clean; one Moon Gold trade; buy new permanent gear or upgrade owned gear; one adventure; and Bank 50 Moon Gold (hold at least 50 Gold). One Feed also counts toward the Feed + Play + Clean goal. Timed Train does not complete the instant Train mission. Districts, stories, raids and completed/extracted Standard or official Daily Moon Runs can also qualify for the adventure goal. Free gear switching, Market purchases and crafting do not qualify for shopping; a fully upgraded permanent gear collection completes that goal automatically.

Check off all seven, then press Claim 7/7 Bonus for up to 25 Pet XP, 50 Moon Gold and 1 Style Token, once per account per UTC day. Gold carried over from an earlier day counts toward the 50-Gold balance target; you do not need to earn 50 new Gold that day. Reaching 50 Gold stays recorded even if you spend it. Complete unclaimed days survive midnight. XP goes to the selected hatched pet when the claim starts; retries keep that original pet. The always-visible daily status shows your current goal, 7/7 COMPLETE, then BONUS CLAIMED after the server confirms payment. The checklist highlights the current goal; its shortcuts open qualifying routes without playing them for you. Mission ticks are saved progress, not a separate payout for every tick. Achievements unlock automatically; claim completed bounties using their own buttons.

Daily Cache is HOME’s account daily claim and counts as a check-in. Claim it once per UTC day; its panel shows the actual capped payout. This is separate from the 7/7 bonus and Journey awards.

The MISSIONS badge counts saved daily 7/7 bonuses, Contract XP and Finale victory rewards that can be claimed. It counts rewards, not unfinished goals or automatic Journey awards. Open MISSIONS to review a reward; opening a route spends nothing. The destination opens and is marked SELECTED ROUTE; you still choose an action there. Collapse help with its heading, or close How to Play with Close, Escape or the backdrop. A reward locked until hatching is excluded unless it already belongs to an earlier pet.

Daily Journey And Growth Marks

After hatching, MISSIONS shows five official objectives: defeat 3 Daily Run enemies; reach Daily Run depth 7; successfully extract from the official Daily Run; defeat its Alley King boss; and complete 3 accepted Feed, Play, Clean or Sleep actions. Complete any 3 of these 5 to earn a Growth Mark automatically. Only the official Daily Run supplies its run objectives; Contracts and Standard runs do not.

The cap is one Growth Mark per pet per UTC day, shared with incubation’s daily Mark. Completing more objectives cannot add a second Mark that day. Watch the server-recorded progress and awarded status; use the saved recovery option if delivery was interrupted.

Weekly Journey And Crests

Complete all five objectives on the same pet: 5 accepted Feed/Play/Clean/Sleep actions, 3 Train actions, 3 completed or extracted qualifying runs, 1 Weekly Boss attempt and 2 Daily Cache check-ins. Standard Moon Run completion/extraction and official Daily Run finishes count toward runs; Contracts do not. A Weekly Crest is awarded automatically once the full set is proven.

One Crest per pet per Journey week. New pets use seven-day windows starting on their adoption UTC date, rather than the leaderboard’s Monday reset. Older pets can retain a legacy Journey clock with a different boundary. Check the panel’s exact reset time. Switching pets cannot transfer objectives.

Jobs And Timed Activities

In WORK, choose among 14 jobs. Cards show level, evolution and any specialist requirement plus base rewards. Instant jobs have no pet energy debit and share a 45-second account job cooldown. Jobs earn Pet XP, currencies and Job progression.

For background activity, choose Sleep, Train, Work or Explore. One account activity runs at a time. It continues while the app is closed or you play Contracts. Minimum claim time: 5 minutes. Training caps at 2 hours; Sleep, Work and Explore cap at 8 hours. Claim ends the session and applies the displayed elapsed-time rewards and stat costs; Cancel ends it with no reward. Sleep restores needs; Train, Work and Explore advance their matching specialist tracks.

Compare the current claim preview and next checkpoint: energy costs change with elapsed time, rather than being a flat entry charge. Explore pays one crystal from 30 minutes to below 2 hours; at 2 hours an Adventure Map replaces it. Waiting past the cap adds no rewards. An unclaimed active session expires 24 hours after its cap; a saved interrupted reward has its own recovery state.

Exploration, Stories And Raids

In EXPLORE, Adventure routes require at least 18, 26 or 34 energy for their respective entry gates and share a 30-minute account cooldown. That energy gate is separate from the actual outcome’s displayed energy cost. Compare each approach’s odds, normal reward and setback before choosing. Street Events also show their costs and risk; the first 6 rewarded events per account per UTC day use full rewards, the next 4 use half, and later events award no progression rewards.

District missions cost 10 energy and show their level gate and approach risk; each district has one rewarded mission per account per UTC day. Stories allow one rewarded step per chain per UTC day. Saved decisions preserve the original choice and outcome.

Seasonal Raid: one attack per pet per UTC day after its displayed level gate. Conserve Energy costs 12 energy for lower damage; Steady Strike and the boss-specific counter cost 18. Counter cards show success and setback damage; they affect that hit, not an ongoing Arena status. Damage persists through the rotation. Claim saved defeated-boss rewards without another attack.

Standard Moon Run And Official Daily Run

Standard Moon Run in EXPLORE has 100 rooms. Read the room’s opponent, objective, chance, costs and gear effects; energy must be above zero to take a room step and the chosen outcome applies its own displayed costs. Loot stays unbanked until extraction or completion. Choose Extract to secure the bag and Adventure progress; failure loses the bag but may keep a small XP lesson. Elite encounters occur every five rooms and district bosses every ten. A Lucky Charm (lucky_charm) can be consumed when it boosts a run.

Official Daily Moon Run is one attempt per account per UTC day. It uses route health and score, with no pet energy debit and no Standard unbanked XP bag. Its seeded rooms supply Daily Journey evidence. Safe choices improve odds at lower score; bold choices risk more for score. After rooms 3 and 6, choose Guardian, Striker or Scavenger or skip the upgrade. Reach the last room and defeat Alley King to finish; early extraction consumes the attempt. Finish Saved Daily Run repairs an interrupted ending without using another attempt.

Continuing Contracts

After hatching, open MISSIONS or Play Now. Choose a quest, Scout/Bruiser/Scavenger build, difficulty and route length, then press the quest button. Standard routes have 6 rooms and 2 upgrade drafts; Long routes have 10 rooms and 4 drafts. Higher difficulties unlock after 5 and 15 successful contracts. Selecting a route record fills the controls; it does not start a quest.

Clear the final boss tactic and meet the main goal to earn Contract Rank. Scouts, patches, checkpoint paths, field decisions and owned relics modify route odds and resources; compare the live costs before committing. They use the contract’s supplies, health and salvage, not pet energy or account currency. Failed or abandoned contracts earn no clear.

Contracts are saved online, with no pet energy cost or cooldown. Start another immediately after finishing. The first three successes per account per UTC day offer up to 20 Pet XP each, within the normal cap; later play still builds Contract Rank and route records. Rank and collection records add no currency, items, Growth Marks, Weekly Crests or official Daily Run credit.

Arena And Kaiju

Pet Arena requires a hatched active Moonpet at level 10. Choose solo CRT or player matchmaking in EXPLORE. Attack Head is stronger but less accurate and is countered by Block Head; Attack Body is more reliable and countered by Block Body. Attack attempts build charge, Charge Special guarantees one charge, and Special Move needs three. Solo CRT shows its next intent; player intent stays hidden until both lock. Level, condition and Battle Loadout affect resolution. Arena has no pet energy entry or result debit; low energy can still weaken battle power.

Kaiju Sticker Battle requires a hatched active Moonpet. Choose solo CRT or matchmaking, then a card. The server picks Power, Size, Attack, Defence, Speed or Legacy before cards lock. Compare the displayed category values; the higher value wins and equal values draw. Rival cards remain hidden until resolution.

Kaiju result settlement charges 6 energy for a win, 5 for a draw or 4 for a loss, once from the participating pet. Insufficient energy holds the payout for recovery; restore that pet’s energy and retry the saved result. Account reward slots give full rewards for the first 5 results each UTC day, half for the next 5, then no progression rewards. Energy still applies to later results. Switching or retrying cannot change the original participant or charge twice.

  1. Start a match. START SOLO KAIJU plays the CRT rival; FIND KAIJU PLAYER joins the player queue. Solo is a real match with normal settlement costs.
  2. Read the battle category. Only Power [PWR], Size [SIZE], Attack [ATK], Defence [DEF], Speed [SPD] or Legacy [LGCY] counts for that match. Compare that value on your cards, not their other stats.
  3. Lock one card. Tapping a card submits it; you cannot change it after it locks. The rival card stays hidden until resolution. Higher wins; equal values draw. The highest value among your cards is highlighted, but it cannot guarantee a win.

Example only: if the category is Speed [SPD], a card with SPD 9 beats SPD 7 even if the second card has more Power. This example sends no card and gives no reward.

Real match cost: settlement charges 6 energy for a win, 5 for a draw or 4 for a loss. If energy is too low, restore the participating pet’s energy and refresh to recover the saved result; do not start a replacement match.

Weekly Boss And Season Finale

Weekly Boss requires a hatched level-5 pet. Strike, Outsmart and Endure each cost 12 energy; inspect the damage preview and weakness bonus. Endure deals damage rather than healing. All pets share one account attack per UTC day; accumulated damage resets Monday at 00:00 UTC. Recover Weekly Reward collects a saved victory without another attack.

After the shared boss is defeated, a pet missing the attempt for its own Journey week can take a participation challenge once that week for 12 energy. It records that pet’s attempt only: no extra damage, XP, currency or boss-win credit. It does not finish the other Crest objectives.

Season Finale: Signal Sovereign in MISSIONS needs final evolution, 240 distinct-day Marks and 44 distinct-week Crests; already completed pets also qualify. Pick Striker, Guardian or Tactician, read the next attack, Guard to build charge and Surge when the core is exposed. Repair kits restore battle health. This saved-turn fight uses separate battle supplies and health with no pet energy cost; defeat allows an immediate free retry. Claim Finale Reward pays up to 100 Pet XP, 200 Moon Gold and 5 Style Tokens once per pet per competition quarter. Previous-quarter fights and rewards remain recoverable.

Equipment, Currency And Rewards

Pet XP raises the earning pet’s level. Moon Gold, Moon Crystals (labelled Gems in some controls) and Style Tokens are shared game currencies for game-only rewards, gear, upgrades, bundles and cosmetics, with no cash-out path. Energy is a need, not currency.

In ECONOMY, compare level gates, exact price and benefit, then Buy, Upgrade or Equip. Care Loadout uses Food, Toy and Outfit; Battle Loadout uses Armor, Weapon and Charm. Changing one does not reset the other. Empty battle slots display none. Equipping owned gear is free; upgrades spend the displayed currencies/materials. Gear affects gameplay; it is not painted as clothing on animation frames. Style Lab cosmetics and Rare Morph backgrounds change presentation.

Claim four rotating daily Bounties after their accepted-action targets are met. Expeditions share three account attempts per UTC day: Dust Tunnels costs 12 energy at level 1, Crystal Caves 18 at level 10, Guardian Rift 24 at level 25. Market offers rotate daily with one unit of personal stock each. Crafting spends the recipe’s listed materials for its output. Bag items are consumed when used. Check storage limits and quotes before confirming; free navigation and equipment switching award no shopping credit.

Moon Gold trades risk 10–250 Gold with a 5-minute account cooldown: inspect the possible loss as well as the possible reward.

Lifetime Progression And Competitions

Lifetime Pet XP, level, evolution, Care/Training/Adventure/Arena/Job/Bond specialist tracks, identity, memories, Marks and Crests stay with the earning pet. Specialist XP measures how it is played; it is separate from Pet XP. Account currencies and owned items are shared.

Daily and weekly Pet XP ranks measure awards in those periods; weekly competition resets Monday at 00:00 UTC. Seasonal ranks and Account Season XP use UTC calendar quarters (January–March, April–June, July–September, October–December). All-time ranks total lifetime Pet XP per player; Run Depth ranks the deepest authoritative room reached. Contract Rank is separate. Your pet continues through each boundary.

In PROFILE, claim account season tiers at 250, 1,000, 3,000 and 7,500 Account Season XP: respectively 80 Gold + 1 Style; 160 Gold + 3 Crystals + 2 Style; 260 Gold + 6 Crystals + 4 Style; 420 Gold + 10 Crystals + 8 Style, plus the displayed evolution Style bonus. Claims pay once per account, tier and quarter; earlier unlocked unclaimed tiers remain recoverable.

Linked Community XP has its own main profile and competition season; Pet XP and specialist XP are not added to it. Pet rewards share account caps of 1,200 Pet XP and 250 Community XP per UTC day. Other limits apply to specialist progress, items and materials. Capped rewards can be lower than previews, and repeatable play does not guarantee unlimited rewards.

Menu Badges And Saved Status

The six menu buttons show server-confirmed status. A red badge means a claim, unlock or saved state needs review. Its text says claims, ready or review; these are different things. An amber active badge means a saved session is running, waiting or ready to resume. When both exist, the red badge takes priority and the saved-status panel still lists active sessions. Menus waiting for their first data load show no invented count.

HOME highlights Daily Cache or a ready hatch. MISSIONS lists saved 7/7 bonuses, Contract XP, Finale rewards and ongoing Contracts/Finales. EXPLORE lists Weekly Boss/Raid rewards, saved runs, Arena/Kaiju matches, queues and interrupted street/district/story/raid choices. WORK tracks the one timed activity: running, claim ready or saved-claim recovery. ECONOMY counts completed unclaimed daily bounties. PROFILE lists unlocked unclaimed season tiers and ready evolution/morph paths; an evolution is an upgrade, not a reward payout.

Open each menu’s collapsible Saved Status to review its inbox. Recovery opens it automatically unless you have chosen to close it. It puts recovery and claims first, followed by active sessions and progress. Review opens the original panel without spending, starting or claiming anything. Expand More Saved Status for additional entries. Journeys show progress, settlement pending and settled separately; automatic Growth Marks/Crests do not create extra claims. A daily run marked Attempt Used stays finished until the next UTC day; Contracts remain repeatable after an ending.

A count ending in + is a lower bound: Contract, Weekly Boss and Raid inboxes return limited pages, so more saved rewards may remain. Weekly Boss also filters older boss records after its limit, so its counts always carry + when any are shown. A timer reaching zero on your device is not proof a claim is ready; wait for server confirmation or use Refresh. After an unconfirmed action, badges disappear and Saved Status asks you to refresh before another action. Claims keep their original owner and existing caps.

Saved Activities And Interrupted Rewards

Your progress is saved by the server. Reopen with the same Telegram account, choose the source pet in PROFILE and use the amber Refresh control after an interruption. Closing the app does not cancel an activity or abandon a saved run, Contract or Finale.

Follow Play Now or the original panel’s saved control: Recover Saved Reward for a timed claim; Finish Saved Daily Run; Retry Saved XP Bonus for a Contract; Recover Weekly Reward; Claim Saved Raid Reward; or Claim Finale Reward. Resume saved district/story/raid choices with their original choice; already paid energy is not charged again. Standard run endings and saved Arena/Kaiju results also reconcile through refresh and their battle/run panel.

Saved rewards keep the original pet and accounting window, even after a switch or calendar boundary. Retry completes that saved settlement once; it does not promise a new capped payout. If a read fails, retain the last valid view and refresh before another action. Resolve blocking sessions and pending rewards before switching or deleting a pet.

Canvas Controls, Dance And Inspire Bot

The cyan Audio speaker toggles game sound effects and the generated game score; the score pauses while Radio is requested. The purple Radio button plays/stops GraffPUNKS Radio independently; Loading means connecting and Retry means the stream failed. Audio mute does not mute Radio. Browsers can require a fresh tap before audio plays.

Dance plays a short pose independently of Radio. It never starts or stops the station; use the Radio icon to control playback. The animated stereo and upper-left disco ball appear only while Radio is requested. Furniture remains drawn underneath them; switching Radio off removes both radio visuals.

Inspire Bot is the silent incubation option in the Secret Bot Chamber. It adds 2 inspiration signal and rhythm affinity to the egg’s saved incubation history. It never starts Radio or plays a song and cannot reveal the identity early. Warm, Talk and Rest record their own affinity signals. These choices influence the assigned identity. Temperament is assigned separately and does not change with these choices. No button selects a guaranteed named character.

Endings, Claims And Automatic Resets

HOME — Daily Cache pays once per account per UTC day. Care pays only accepted actions within cooldowns and caps; Energy Drink, Dance and Cuddles change stats without reward XP. Hatching and evolution advance your companion. Your companion continues after any competition reset.

MISSIONS — Daily 7/7 completion has one saved bonus; complete unclaimed days remain claimable after midnight. Journey Marks and Crests settle automatically after validated goals. Weekly Journey uses this pet’s own seven-day period; check its separate reset countdown, which can differ from Monday’s boss rotation. Contracts end on clear, defeat or abandon: only clears earn rank, with up to three account/day XP bonuses. Rank remains replayable after bonuses are used. Finale victory needs its own once-per-pet/quarter claim; a defeat allows a free retry. Saved bonuses and unfinished fights retain their original source.

EXPLORE — Standard Runs end by extraction, defeat or the room cap; defeat loses the unbanked bag. Official Daily Runs end by extraction, defeat or room 10: one account attempt per UTC day, including a failed attempt. Adventures, street events, district missions, story steps and expeditions show their settled outcome once. Story endings finish a cycle, then continue under their daily gate. Weekly Boss and Raid victories pay through their original saved reward; another attack is not required to claim. Arena and Kaiju end on a result or deadline, then settle once for the participating pet. Refresh reconciles saved endings.

WORK — A timed activity starts once, runs while you play elsewhere and ends when claimed or cancelled. Claim from five minutes; Training caps at two hours and the other activities at eight hours. Waiting after the cap adds nothing. Claim within 24 hours after the cap or an untouched activity expires. A claim already saved for recovery keeps its frozen reward. Jobs finish immediately with their shown outcome and shared cooldown. Review caps before starting another activity.

ECONOMY — Claim completed Daily Bounties before 00:00 UTC: unclaimed bounty targets expire when the board changes. Paid receipts stay paid.
