Fast Start

Open @WIKICOMSBOT and launch Moonpet OS.

Telegram securely signs the Mini App session; the app never receives or stores the bot token.

Initialise your Moonpet inside the app, then use the six bottom modules for care, tasks, runs, work, gear and identity.

The Recommended Next Move terminal reads the same server-authoritative Coach contract, including exact requirements, costs, rewards and progress.

The old commands remain as launch aliases during rollout; they no longer operate separate gameplay menus when the Mini App feature flag is enabled.

Persistent Identity And Evolution

Each seasonal Moonpet is a persistent virtual companion with a server-owned lifecycle, identity, care history, sleep state, XP and evolution progress. Stage 0 uses EGGYONE as the shared Secret Bot and Stage 1 uses WTFBOI as the shared Street Moonpet. The assigned canonical identity remains exactly UNKNOWN through Stages 0, 1 and 2.

When the server confirms Stage 3, the canonical identity name is revealed and remains visible through Stages 4 and 5. The full sequence is Secret Bot, Street Moonpet, Cyber Moonpet, Elite Moonpet, Moon Guardian and Legendary Moon Guardian. Later stages use identity-specific art and approved fallbacks.

Equipment and inventory remain gameplay state; they are not drawn as runtime clothing overlays. Rare Morph progression changes the approved environment or background while preserving the Moonpet identity and evolution art.

The Three Progression Layers

Pet XP

Your main pet growth value. It controls pet level, stage, unlocks and pet leaderboard position.

Specialist XP

Six independent tracks record how your Moonpet is played: Care, Training, Adventure, Arena, Job and Bond.

Community XP

Accepted actions can add XP to your linked main Moonboys profile. This remains separate from Pet XP and specialist XP.

Protected rewards: reward-bearing actions now settle through a central authority. Pet XP is capped at 1,200 per UTC day and Community XP at 250 per UTC day, with stable idempotency keys and duplicate protection.

Six Specialist Progression Tracks

Care

Raised mainly through feed, play, clean, sleep and some training. Represents daily pet management.

Training

Raised through instant training and timed training claims.

Adventure

Raised through exploration, successful run steps and extracted runs.

Arena

Built by competitive combat actions and completed battle outcomes as those runtime hooks are used.

Job

Raised by short jobs and timed work claims. Job XP also determines your displayed job rank.

Bond

Raised by positive care actions and daily chest claims. Represents the long-term relationship with your Moonpet.

Each track has its own total, level calculation and daily award allowance. Daily counters reset by UTC day, while total specialist XP remains permanent.

Personality, Aptitudes, Memories And Evolution

Personality reactions and learned aptitudes are separate. Street Fighter, Explorer, Loyal and Curious shape dialogue and preferences; Brave, Loyal, Clever, Stylish, Tough and Lucky record how the player develops the Moonpet. Accepted Mini App gameplay actions can surface a Moonpet reaction, and recent-dialogue history prevents immediate repetition.

Moonpets remember firsts, favourite activity, completed runs, boss victories and evolution milestones. The CORE screen exposes this Memory Archive, the unlock directory, current and next evolution requirements, specialist progress, season rewards and milestone alerts.

Evolution moves through one canonical sequence: Secret Bot, Street Moonpet, Cyber Moonpet, Elite Moonpet, Moon Guardian and Legendary Moon Guardian. The identity becomes visible at Elite Moonpet, Stage 3. Advanced Traits are not this system: they remain future roadmap work.

Choose A Crafting Goal

Open Economy → Craft and choose a recipe to track. The workshop shows materials owned, required and missing, plus how many finished items are already in your bag. Your selected goal returns on this device for the same pet and appears in Play Now. Change it or choose No Tracked Goal whenever you want; selecting a goal spends nothing.

Compare available districts that offer a missing material, expeditions with matching possible finds, or today’s affordable market offers. Sometimes buying the finished item is another option. District clears and expedition finds remain uncertain, with their normal energy and daily limits. Opening a route never starts or buys anything. When supplies are ready, press the recipe button to craft. Goals do not add bonus rewards or official daily credit; Contracts remain available while limited routes reset.

Open the Bag to review what consuming each item does. A Lucky Charm or Adventure Map can instead remain in your bag for standard Moon Run benefits. The consumable Energy Drink is separate from the Care button. Style Lab purchases currently add collection records only; they do not apply visible frames, trails, animations or stat boosts. Changing your callsign does not require buying a Rename Badge.

Daily Cache

After hatching, open the Daily Cache in Care for 40 Moon Gold, two Style Tokens and up to 40 Pet XP. The button shows the XP available under your daily allowance. All your pets share one cache per UTC day; after claiming, its saved receipt and reset timer replace the ready state. Use Continue With Contracts to keep playing while you wait.

Season reward tiers stay claimable if payment fails. A collected tier stays collected after a reload, and retrying cannot pay it twice.

Weekly Boss And Seasonal Rewards

The Weekly Boss opens at level 5 after hatching and rotates Monday at 00:00 UTC. Compare Strike, Outsmart and Endure: each costs 12 energy, and the board shows your pet's damage range with weakness and personality bonuses included. Endure deals damage; it does not heal or grant a defensive buff. All pets share one account attempt per UTC day, and damage persists until the weekly reset.

Today's saved attack and the next reset stay visible. If a victory reward is interrupted, use Recover Weekly Reward to collect it once without another attack or energy cost. Saved rewards remain recoverable after the week changes or you switch pets; victory credit stays with the original pet. Continue with Contracts or Practice while waiting for another boss attempt.

CORE exposes one-time reward tiers at 250, 1,000, 3,000 and 7,500 seasonal Pet XP. Claims are unique per player, season and tier and can be safely retried after an interrupted response.

Seasonal Raid Choices And Saved Rewards

EXPLORE also has a separate Seasonal Raid. Choose Conserve Energy for 12 energy and lower damage, Steady Strike for 18 energy and reliable damage, or the boss-specific counter for 18 energy. Each counter shows its success chance, stronger hit and lower-damage setback. The boss weakness determines the counter; it affects this hit without applying ongoing Arena status effects.

You get one attack per pet per UTC day after reaching the displayed level requirement. Damage persists through the raid rotation. A saved interrupted attack keeps its original move and outcome; if its energy was already paid, resuming costs no more energy.

If a defeated boss reward was interrupted, use Claim Saved Raid Reward. Older rotations for the same pet and pet season remain claimable, without another attack or energy payment. Play Now links to outstanding rewards.

District missions and story chains also show saved choices when an interrupted settlement can resume. District approaches use their displayed risk and cost 10 energy. Stories keep one rewarded step per chain per UTC day. For continuous quests after daily limits, use Continuing Contracts; their rank progression continues while daily Pet XP limits remain in place.

Understanding Your Two Loadouts

Care Loadout: Food, Toy and Outfit. These improve care actions, currencies, Pet XP and selected run modifiers.

Battle Loadout: Armor, Weapon and Charm. These primarily affect Pet Arena attack, defense, crit, dodge and luck.

The loadouts are tracked independently. Changing one does not reset the other. Empty battle slots display none; there is no automatic starter Armor or starter Weapon.

Equipment Levels, Mastery And Set Builds

Eligible purchased or currently equipped items are registered in the equipment progression system. Each item can hold:

  • Item level and item XP.
  • Mastery XP earned when that item is relevant to accepted gameplay.
  • Mastery tier representing longer-term use.

The GEAR screen upgrades owned equipment from level 1 through 10 and now shows Street Runner, Crystal Beast and Moon Enforcer set progress. Set effects activate from the pieces currently equipped, not merely owned. Every upgrade button shows the exact Moon Gold and material cost before it can be pressed. Equipment mastery awards and upgrades are event-key protected, so repeated delivery cannot duplicate progression.

Pet Economy: Earn → Craft → Upgrade → Unlock

/peteconomy makes the full loop visible. Existing care, 14 jobs, timed activities, events, Moon Runs, Arena, Kaiju, weekly bosses and seasonal tiers remain earning routes. The Daily Bounty Board adds four rotating, server-verified goals from a ten-bounty library; Crystal Expeditions add three level-scaled, Energy-costing attempts per UTC day.

The Moon Market rotates four offers from a twelve-offer catalogue every UTC day. Each offer has one unit of personal daily stock and exchanges Moon Gold, Moon Crystals or Style for useful consumables, upgrade materials and carefully limited currency conversions. The whole paid bundle must fit: item stacks and currencies cap at 999,999; each material caps at 9,999. Storage Full blocks the purchase without charging or selling the offer. Use items or spend materials first; Bag and Crafting links are on the Market board. The permanent equipment shop remains the main upgrade sink.

Moon Gold is the broad earning and purchasing currency. Moon Crystals are rarer and fund advanced upgrades or selected market packs. Style Tokens buy cosmetic and specialist value. Nine canonical materials now include Scrap Metal, Moon Fabric, Crystal Shards, Battery Cells, Spray Cores, Kaiju Fragments, Arena Tokens, Evolution Fragments and Mastery Tokens.

Economy’s Crafting Workshop converts early- and mid-game materials into Street Rations, Clean Kits, Battery Packs, Style Patches and Route Maps. Every card shows its level requirement, exact cost and output. The server rechecks current balances and performs material debit plus item credit as one idempotent settlement.

Consumable items remain in telegram_pet_inventory; materials use telegram_pet_material_balances everywhere. Migration 051 safely consolidates legacy material rows so balances cannot split across two stores.

The Coach consumes a structured recommendation contract for every economy feature. Each current and future upgrade must supply a title, exact progress or cost, reward/use explanation and direct callback; this keeps the Recommended Next Move blurb complete as the economy grows.

Daily Run choices and tactics

New official Daily Runs show the current condition, each route’s clear chance and its score on success. Safe approaches offer better odds with less score; bold approaches take more risk for more score. A failed room ends the attempt.

After rooms 3 and 6, choose Guardian, Striker or Scavenger for the remaining rooms, or keep going without a tactic. The choice is saved and cannot be changed. Tactics affect run score and clear chance; they do not add XP, currency or item rewards. Existing saved runs finish under their earlier rules. After your one official attempt, use Continuing Contracts or Practice for more play.

Choose a Route for Your Quest

Every unfinished Weekly Journey objective now opens its qualifying activity: care, training, Moon Runs, Weekly Boss or Daily Cache. Standard run completions/extractions and official Daily Run finishes can advance the weekly run target. Contracts and Practice do not. Daily mission targets also offer alternative qualifying routes: use an equipment upgrade for the shopping target, or a district, story step, seasonal raid or Moon Run finish for the adventure target. Open the route to check its current requirements; navigation itself spends nothing. Play Now links directly to Daily Journey objectives, Weekly Journey targets and the seven daily missions.

Continuing Contracts

After your Secret Bot hatches, open MISSIONS → CONTRACTS or choose Continuing Contracts from Play Now. Pick one of six goals: map successful routes, recover salvage, protect the courier’s health, break the blockade with bold routes, trace the signal with search routes, or bring supplies home. Choose Scout, Bruiser or Scavenger, then a Standard Route (six rooms, drafts after rooms two and four) or Long Route (ten rooms, drafts after rooms two, four, six and eight). Every goal shows both targets before you start. Long routes require seven route wins, 120 salvage, 50 health, five bold wins, five search wins, or eight supplies, according to the selected goal. Standard targets are four route wins, 65 salvage, 50 health, three bold wins, three search wins, or five supplies. Finish the whole route alive and meet the main target to earn rank; reaching the target early does not finish it. Later rooms get harder. Both lengths use the same daily XP allowance. In new contracts, choose one of the offered upgrades or Take Supply Cache: trade that upgrade opportunity for two contract supplies. Supplies can heal, fund Scout Ahead or help complete Well Supplied. The choice is saved and grants no pet item, XP or currency by itself.

At each draft in a new contract, you can also choose Redraw Upgrades: spend 15 contract salvage to replace the current perks with different unowned upgrades, once per checkpoint. The button states how many replacements you will get. It appears only when different unowned perks remain; a late long-route draft may have none left to redraw. Then choose one of the new perks or take supplies. Redrawing does not advance a room, and spent salvage reduces both salvage-goal progress and final rank. Keep the original offers if saving salvage matters more.

Cover, bold, search and supply choices show their odds and consequences. Successful contracts earn saved Contract Rank points. Another quest is immediately available after success, failure or abandonment. Tier 2 unlocks after five completions and Tier 3 after fifteen. Rank has no daily cap and is separate from pet level and competitive leaderboards.

Contracts cost no pet energy and have no gameplay cooldown. Their health, supplies and salvage are separate run resources; salvage does not become Moon Gold. The first three successful contracts per account each UTC day qualify for up to 20 Pet XP each, subject to your normal XP cap. Bonus slots are shared across pets. After those bonuses, keep playing for rank and completions. No currency, items, Growth Marks, Weekly Crests or official Daily Run credit are awarded.

New contracts give each room its own route conditions, already included in the displayed odds and rewards. Add an optional All Routes, Daredevil or Well Supplied objective for 60 × tier extra Contract Rank when both goals succeed. Side objectives add no XP or currency; missing one does not fail the main goal. The board records completions and best rank points for each goal. Older saved contracts keep their original rules.

Every choice is saved online. Close the app and resume on another signed-in device; only choosing Abandon ends an unfinished contract. If an XP delivery is interrupted, use Retry Saved XP Bonus; the original bonus cannot be duplicated.

Plan For The Final Boss

New Contracts end with a boss in the last room. Mapping and courier goals face the Shield Warden: flanking is favoured, while breaking its guard risks more damage for salvage. Signal and supply goals face the Signal Hound: jamming its relay improves search odds. Salvage and blockade goals face the Vault Breaker: striking its exposed drive improves bold odds, while raiding the vault is riskier.

Your boss is shown before starting and during the run. Each final tactic shows its actual clear chance, salvage and failure damage, including your build, upgrades, path and preparation. You must clear the boss tactic and meet your main goal. A failed final tactic ends the Contract with no rank or XP. Supply rest cannot skip the boss; consider Scout Ahead or Field Patch before committing. There is no separate boss reward. After either result, another quest is available immediately with no pet energy cost or cooldown. Older saved Contracts keep their original ending rules.

Make A Field Decision

After choosing a checkpoint upgrade or supply cache in a new Contract, you may meet a Roadside Quartermaster, Courier Aid Station or Abandoned Salvage Rig. Each offers two decisions and a free leave option. Buy or sell supplies, pay for repairs, or take a rough detour for salvage. Buttons show exact costs and gains; healing stops at your maximum route health.

Choose once at each checkpoint, leave, or take your next room route to skip. Your offer and decision survive reloads. These decisions use only that Contract’s health, supplies and salvage, with no room advance or pet energy cost. Spending resources can reduce your main goal, side objective or final rank, so check your target first. They award no extra Pet XP, currency or items. Older saved Contracts keep their existing rules.

Pick A Bounty You Can Play Now

Play Now prioritises unfinished bounties with a qualifying action currently available. It can direct you to an Adventure when the Moon Run route is blocked. Cooldowns, energy, activity timers, inventory and battle unlocks affect which route is suggested. The bounty board still shows every target and explains routes that are not ready. When daily options are exhausted, Continuing Contracts and Practice remain available.

Choose A Checkpoint Path

After choosing an upgrade or supplies in a new Contract, you can plan the next two rooms. Quiet Streets adds 8 percentage points to clear chance, reduces salvage per success by 5 and reduces failure damage by 4. Salvage Hotspot removes 8 percentage points, adds 10 salvage per success and adds 4 failure damage. Stay on Course keeps the normal rules. Chances remain between 30% and 98%; the route buttons include every modifier.

Choose once at that checkpoint, or take a route immediately to skip the offer. Selecting a path costs nothing and does not advance a room or award XP. The choice survives reloads and lasts two room advances, including a supply rest. Preparations do not consume its duration. Older saved contracts retain their original rules.

Care While An Activity Runs

Sleep and Train are unavailable while your pet has a background activity running. Use Review Background Activity in Care to open its timer and claim or cancellation options. Feed, Play and Clean remain available with their normal cooldowns, and Contracts can keep going. Train needs at least 18 energy. Your daily care mission shows which of Feed, Play and Clean you have completed; Coach recommends the next missing step.

Prepare Your Route And Compare Outcomes

New Continuing Contracts offer one optional preparation before each room. Scout Ahead spends two contract supplies to improve that room’s route chances by 10 percentage points, up to 98%. Field Patch spends 20 salvage to restore up to 25 route health without advancing a room. Patch only when hurt; spent salvage no longer counts toward your salvage goal or final rank. You can skip preparation and take a route immediately.

Preparations use only resources inside that contract. They do not cost pet energy, award XP by themselves or change your pet’s stats. The choice is saved and resets when you advance to the next room. Existing saved contracts finish under their original rules.

Street Events and Adventures now show both normal and setback probabilities, base reward ranges and costs. Actual rewards remain subject to repeated-play scaling and caps. Hunger costs increase hunger. Adventure entry energy is a requirement, separate from the outcome’s energy cost. Adventure and job buttons show their existing cooldowns; use Contracts or Practice while waiting.

Play Between Cooldowns

The Play Now board on HOME, MISSIONS and EXPLORE links to available stories, runs, district missions, bosses and objectives. Daily mission shortcuts take you to the relevant controls. Daily Journey also lists all five official goals with server-recorded progress; these are separate from the seven daily missions.

For continuous play, open Practice Roguelite from EXPLORE. Choose Scout, Bruiser or Scavenger, pick a local goal, then manage health, supplies and salvage across 12 rooms. Each route shows its odds and stakes. Draft an upgrade every three rooms, or extract early. Try another build immediately after the run.

Practice is available after adoption, even while your Secret Bot is still an egg. It costs no pet energy and has no cooldown. It awards no XP, currency, items, quest progress or leaderboard rewards. Your practice run and personal best stay in this browser, separately for each pet; they do not sync across devices. You can close the app and resume later when browser storage is available.

The official Daily Moon Run remains one attempt per account per UTC day. Its panel shows whether that attempt is used, and its room and objective progress. It does not use the standard Moon Run unbanked XP bag. Stories still allow one rewarded choice per chain per UTC day; practice does not reset those limits.

Build Your Contract Route Collection

Continuing Contracts now show your saved records for all 108 combinations of six goals, three builds, three difficulty tiers and two route lengths. Past completions count automatically toward Standard Route records; Long Route records are separate. Repeat a route to improve its best Contract Rank, or choose an uncleared route to try a different build and goal. The checklist adds no extra XP, currency or official daily credit.

Choosing a record fills the route length, build and tier controls and focuses the matching quest button. It does not start the contract; press that quest button when ready. Locked tiers still require five or fifteen completed contracts. Your last route length, build, tier and side objective return after finishing or reloading, so you can repeat that setup or change it.

Collection records belong to the active pet and season. Contracts remain available with no pet energy cost or cooldown, including after daily XP bonuses run out. Failed or abandoned contracts do not count as clears.

Timed Activities

Use WORK to compare Sleep, Train, Work or Explore by duration, then start one background activity. It continues while you play Contracts, Practice or other routes, or close the app. Legacy activity commands open the same module while Moonpet OS is enabled.

The minimum claim time is five minutes. Sleep, work and explore cap at eight hours. Training caps at two hours. Compare the current claim preview with the next duration checkpoint: rewards and stat costs both change with time, and normal reward caps and stat limits apply. Explore gives one crystal from thirty minutes until two hours; at two hours, an Adventure Map replaces that crystal. Claiming ends the activity. Waiting past its duration cap adds no rewards, and unclaimed sessions expire twenty-four hours after that cap.

Timed training progresses Training XP. Timed work progresses Job XP. Exploration progresses Adventure XP. Cancellation awards nothing. The Work screen links directly to Contracts and Practice so you can choose another route while the timer runs.

If a claim is interrupted, Play Now and Work show Recover Saved Reward. Retry to finish the original saved claim; it cannot be paid twice. New activities become available after recovery. Work and Economy actions that require hatching show their lock while your pet is still an egg.

Pet Arena

Pet Arena is part of the current build, but live access is still gated. Your active Moonpet must be hatched, and Arena itself still needs a level 10 active Moonpet.

It is a counter battle: Attack Head hits harder but is less accurate and is answered by Block Head; Attack Body is more reliable and is answered by Block Body. Attack attempts build Special charge, Charge Special builds one guaranteed charge, and Special Move unlocks at three charges.

Moonpet OS shows accuracy, base damage, counters, charge state and a perspective-correct recap after every round. Solo CRT practice also shows the app opponent's next intent so players can learn the counter game. Player-vs-player intent always stays hidden until both choices lock.

Level, Pet XP, condition and Battle Loadout still affect authoritative resolution. Armor improves defence, weapons improve attack, and charms improve crit, dodge or luck. Arena rewards use protected, retry-safe settlement.

Kaiju Sticker Battle

Kaiju Sticker Battle is also part of the current build, but live entry requires a hatched active Moonpet in the Mini App combat rollout. The server rolls and persists the active category—Power, Size, Attack, Defence, Speed or Legacy—when the match is created, before either card locks.

Each card shows its active-category score and two strongest stats, turning card selection into an informed draft while the rival card remains sealed. Highest active stat wins; the result panel then shows the category and final score. Rewards retain protected repeat slots, conditional Energy payment and retry-safe settlement.

Endless Moon Run

/petrun starts or resumes the 100-room Standard Moon Run. Every room presents an authored brief, opponent, objective and server-derived decision preview covering risk, costs, reward ranges and gear advantages. Elite encounters appear every five rooms, district bosses every ten, and difficulty and score rise through the run.

Rewards remain unbanked until extraction or terminal completion. Extracting secures the current bag and progresses Adventure XP; a failed room loses the unbanked bag but can retain a small Pet XP lesson. The Run Depth leaderboard records the deepest authoritative room reached.

Care gear and authoritative bag items can modify run risk and rewards. A lucky_charm can be consumed when it boosts a run.

The backend now supports region, difficulty, deterministic seed, room progress, run modifiers, relics, run history and analytics. Run room, boss and completion rewards must prove matching persisted state and flow through the central reward authority.

Run Modifiers, Relics And Bosses

Run Modifiers

Temporary run-only effects can be stored for a run and are removed at terminal transitions. Validation rejects XP, cap, currency and permanent-stat mutation effects.

Relics

Permanent relic ownership can be persisted with rarity, unlock time and effect metadata. Future relic reward bonuses must still pass through the protected reward authority.

Bosses

The starter roguelite boss foundation deliberately favors bounded materials, an evolution item and unique relic ownership instead of repeatable Pet XP or Community XP farming.

Daily Caps And Duplicate Protection

The game uses server-owned UTC daily counters for Community XP, Pet XP and specialist progression. Hitting one cap does not necessarily stop every other type of reward.

Progression-bearing actions use stable event/idempotency keys. Event and Kaiju repeat rewards use protected daily slots, activity settlements are recoverable, item awards/consumption use one authoritative inventory balance, and concurrent run terminal callbacks serialize against late room steps.

This means Telegram retries, repeated button delivery and API retries can repair interrupted work without multiplying progression.

Anti-farm rule: gameplay can remain repeatable after progression rewards taper. Unlimited play does not mean unlimited Pet XP, Community XP, materials or items.

Seasons, Missions And Leaderboards

Daily missions reset each UTC day. Weekly windows and automatic 90-day pet seasons roll over independently.

/petleaderboard ranks seasonal Pet XP. Specialist track levels, job rank, traits, equipment mastery, relic ownership and run-history data remain personal progression records rather than the current leaderboard score.

Game economy: Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens are game currencies used only for game-only rewards and progression; there is no cash-out path.
