The Game Loop

1. Choose

Pick a game from the Arcade. Link Telegram through My Moonboy for competitive play.

2. Play

Finish a run and submit a score where the game supports it. Check the game’s confirmation that the server accepted the result.

3. Track

Open the leaderboard. Use its original scoreboards and Arcade Node Map for game records, or Unified Rankings for Grinder Points.

Score and XP are different

Each game uses its own score to rank runs. Eligible Arcade XP is recorded only when the server accepts the run. Grinder Points measure eligible activity across systems, with a cap for each source. Check your own XP and tasks in My Moonboy.

Earn Grinder Points

Points come from recorded activity. A run can show its score and XP in the breakdown while counting once toward Grinder Points. Daily caps reward playing across games and activities after you reach a source’s limit.

Read the ranks and earn bonuses

Open Unified Rankings, pick a view, then choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Seasonal. Days and months use UTC calendar boundaries. Weeks start Monday at 00:00 UTC. Seasonal follows the configured community season dates.

  • Global · Hardest Grinder includes every eligible player, in or out of a faction.
  • Factions adds member points, eligible NPC rounds and weekly leader boosts for each of the nine factions.
  • Games ranks Grinder Points from the selected game alone and shows your Global rank alongside.
  • Clan Players shows points credited to your selected faction. No Clan shows activity without a faction on its record or weekly crew roster. Both views still show the Global rank.
What happens when a chart closes?

Choose Last finalized to see the frozen standings and earned bonuses. First gets 30 points, second 20, third 10. Tied players or factions share the same rank and award. You need eligible activity in the period to earn another bonus. Allow at least 15 minutes after the reset for final results.

A bonus carries into the next matching period. For example, a weekly first place adds 30 to the next week’s matching chart. Global combines individual chart bonuses up to 60 points. Faction bonuses stay on the faction chart. These points affect rankings. Spendable XP and game prizes follow their own rules.

Help your faction climb

Join a faction in the Battle Chamber and enter its weekly crew roster to credit eligible activity without its own faction record. Bank task tiers and complete Daily WTF Signals to build your faction’s total. NPC leaders also earn 12 faction points per eligible completed round.

How the weekly boost level is decided

Once unified weeks begin settling, the finalized weekly Factions chart sets the following week’s Battle Chamber leader boost level. First place gets level 9, second gets level 8, down to level 1 for ninth. The level applies to one perk chosen when the week closes. Crews with fewer than two-thirds of their roster banking get Crew Rally to reward active member-days. Otherwise, crews averaging at least eight banks per active banker get Signal Relay. The rest get Supply Dock.

  • Supply Dock adds the level in points per banked tier.
  • Signal Relay adds three times the level per completed signal.
  • Crew Rally adds twice the level per active member-day with a bank or signal.

For example, level 9 Signal Relay adds 27 faction points per qualifying signal. This is a faction boost. The separate task-banking standings still decide banking collectible rewards.

Why slow a rival leader?

An overlapping pause cancels the rival’s NPC round. If those lost points change the final weekly rank, they can also lower the rival’s next boost level. Look for rival leaders on the Waterfront or through the Battle Chamber’s crew progress controls.

Use one free slowdown question per UTC day for 5, 10 or 20 minutes. Paid options pause a leader for one hour or 24 hours. The controls show the price and availability. A leader who is already paused or recovering cannot be slowed again yet.

Why do the numbers differ between pages?

A bank can record Clan Renown, a collectible and Grinder Points in their respective systems. A live-signal bonus adds Community XP only after its own receipt settles. Arcade XP comes from eligible synced games. Grinder Points and Leader Power affect rankings and perks. Use the source game to confirm a specific reward.

Need help?

My score is missing

Check whether your game confirmed the submission and whether you are in the right chart and period. The Arcade Node Map takes you to each game or its original scoreboard. Unified Rankings show Grinder Points from eligible records.

My XP hasn't updated

XP and score are separate. Check your verified account in My Moonboy and the game's own result.
