Daily Active Opportunities

Daily active opportunities reset every UTC day. Fresh active options appear each day, and Daily WTF signals open every 4 hours at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, and 20:00 UTC. Each timed signal runs for 90 minutes.

DAILY LOOP

Stay active to stay synced with the city.

Daily missions and active opportunities are built to reward players who keep showing up. Check in while a Daily WTF signal is live, complete the required objective, and unlock follow-up roguelite options.

Timed event XP is a visual preview/status layer unless the server explicitly reports an awarded XP event. The game is the fun; XP is bonus progression/status.
