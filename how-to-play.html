The Game Loop

1. Choose

Pick any available game from the Arcade.

2. Play

Finish a run and submit a score where supported.

3. Track

Check the leaderboard for accepted scores and My Moonboy for verified personal tasks.

Score and XP are different

Score determines rank. Eligible Arcade XP is recorded only when the server accepts the run. A high score does not automatically mean the same XP.

Game-specific help

Wiki participation — comments, article likes and citation votes

Engage (comment), Signal (article like) and Cite (citation vote) are separate eligible actions. Only accepted receipts count. My Moonboy currently shows only the total accepted Wiki receipts for today; check the article for individual results. Full Wiki reward rules.

Block Topia

Block Topia is a gated beta. The game's live status controls entry requirements.

MoonPet rules

Read the MoonPet game guide. My Moonboy is read-only; check MoonPet Saved Status for actual claims.

Need help?

My score is missing

Check whether your game confirmed the submission. Only accepted scores appear in competitive rankings.

My XP hasn't updated

XP and score are separate. Check your verified account in My Moonboy and the game's own result.
