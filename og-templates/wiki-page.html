Framework

BUILD THE PAGE IN CLEAR SECTIONS

Each wiki page should open with one strong hero statement, then move through focused content sections using cards, highlights and action links where they improve understanding.

Hero

Title, category signal, summary and one memorable statement that defines the subject immediately.

Sections

Use full-width topic panels for origin, identity, history, systems, relationships and other major subjects.

Cards

Use three-card or two-card grids to explain connected ideas without creating a wall of text.

The page should feel like one connected project record, not a loose stack of unrelated generic cards.

WHAT EVERY STANDARD WIKI PAGE KEEPS

Page content

SEO metadata, canonical URL, hero artwork, overview sections, related wiki paths and cited sources.
