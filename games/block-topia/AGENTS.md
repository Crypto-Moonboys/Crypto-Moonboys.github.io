# Block Topia map baseline

GK approved the placement, camera angles, in-world scale, and close-zoom art for these seven maps on 24 November 2026. The reference is commit `9cc76583a6e1501d935af22e7c9b34cd0f99e6b6` ([PR #2098](https://github.com/Crypto-Moonboys/Crypto-Moonboys.github.io/pull/2098)), which includes the rear tower placement from #2096. Start from current `main` and use that reference to review changes affecting this baseline. Keep the following settings until GK requests a change to them.

| Map entry | Approved view and placement source |
| --- | --- |
| [Block Topia Survival](index.html) | 20 × 20, 2:1 isometric. [main.js](main.js) puts Bitcoin Cash at the rear on blocked tile `(1,4)`, Zcash at `(3,2)`, and WAX at `(7,2)`. The overview includes headroom for the tower. |
| [Lumina’s Waterfront](waterfront/index.html) | Top-down map with overhead landmark art. [harbor-art.js](waterfront/harbor-art.js) puts Bitcoin Cash on the rear harbor lot `(42,11)`, Zcash at `(16,21)`, and WAX at `(21,22)`. Keep the [restored harbor baseline](waterfront/README.md#preserve-the-restored-harbor) and the compact nature island, which uses one quarter of the original extension’s land budget. |
| [HODL Wars Lores](hodl-wars-lores/index.html) | Tilted Moon projection in [game.js](hodl-wars-lores/game.js). [lunar-world.mjs](hodl-wars-lores/lunar-world.mjs) puts Bitcoin Cash at the rear `(24,14)`, Zcash at `(28,20)`, and WAX at `(19,27)`. Keep the Landing Pad clear and all six lore stations reachable. |
| [Original Node City comparison](test-builds/compare/node-city/index.html) | 48 × 48 classic 2:1 isometric map with a lunar floor built from twelve square textures. Keep the 1.25× opening zoom, 2× close-up limit, and upper-left terrain lighting. The textures cover the full grid with a fixed random arrangement of 6 × 6-tile patches. Keep terrain details at patch scale and preserve the shared lighting direction. Use the approved fixture placement in [comparison.mjs](test-builds/compare/comparison.mjs). The rear tower placement starts its lot search at `(18,20)`. |
| [Survival Grid comparison](test-builds/compare/survival/index.html) | 20 × 20, 2:1 isometric with an overview fitted to the tower. Keep Bitcoin Cash on the rear tile `(1,4)` and keep both sets of towers along the upper edge. [Survival mining lots](test-builds/compare/survival/miners.mjs) use blocked tiles and open ground away from roads. The ten alternate mining designs put the doors and logos on the left-facing wall. Keep the animated light layers registered to their stationary jagged grey rock bases. |
| [Waterfront comparison](test-builds/compare/waterfront/index.html) | Top-down. [scenes.mjs](test-builds/compare/scenes.mjs) uses the shared [harbor renderer](waterfront/harbor-art.js) and its three approved landmark lots. |
| [Moon comparison](test-builds/compare/moon/index.html) | Tilted Moon camera. [comparison.mjs](test-builds/compare/comparison.mjs) places Bitcoin Cash at the rear starting at `(24,14)` and WAX at its existing comparison position. |

- Keep the original projections, camera framing and zoom behavior for each map, along with its ground anchors and depth order.
- Keep the tall Bitcoin Cash silhouette and its 1.7× draw scale relative to the standard miners. Its rear placement gives the skyline room while keeping the main routes clear.
- Use the appropriate isometric, overhead, or tilted Moon atlas. On both Moon views, use the corrected WAX Bee sprite with its entry and base aligned to the sloping Moon grid. The approved miner sheets have twelve transparent 672 × 840 frames in four columns by three rows. Keep the 12 fps loop, stationary foundation, reduced-motion first frame, and high-quality sampling for the miners at close zoom.
- For natural Moon rocks around building bases, use neutral regolith grey `#585858`, sampled from the Node City terrain, with charcoal shadows around `#242424` and lit facets around `#8E8E8E`. Retain the stone texture and upper-left light direction. Keep the exposed edges irregular, with individual jagged rocks and gaps around the foundation. Keep pink and purple out of the stone palette, and keep intentional lights and metalwork in their own colours. Use this palette for future foundation sprites at every camera angle.
- When changing relevant assets or rendering, check the affected views at overview and close zoom. Verify readable signage, a stable rock base, the tower’s height and silhouette, and landmark inspection where supported. If an edit affects the shared renderer or an atlas used by multiple routes, check each affected view.
