What is SPARKY?

SPARKY is the creative-guidance approach behind SWARMSY. This page brings the beginner part into ordinary ChatGPT. ChatGPT can suggest, draft, explain, critique and help plan. It does not automatically access your SWARMSY workspace, remember private SWARMSY decisions, print products or carry out real-world actions.

The starter message asks ChatGPT to read our public SPARKY creative guide for the longer workflow. If it cannot open that link, the starter message still includes the essential instructions. To continue in a new chat, paste your saved project notes or images again.

Stickers, posters and public artwork need permission where displayed. A legal wall is only legal when the relevant owner/authority permits your work. Use your own paper, belongings, private surfaces or digital mockups when in doubt. Never mistake a concept image for evidence that something was actually made or posted.
