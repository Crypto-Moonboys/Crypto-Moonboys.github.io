    iMore info about weekly scoring

    iHow faction leaders earn power

    Bank a Clan Operation tier for 1 Leader Power. Each verified Daily WTF completion earns 2 Leader Power for the faction on your locked weekly roster.

    After the final weekly standings are published, first place unlocks level 9 and ninth unlocks level 1. Each ranked faction leader automatically selects one of three perks for the new week. Tied factions earn the same level.

    Leader Power is shown alongside the official weekly score. Official ranks and prizes use existing crew scoring.

    Leaders can earn 12 power in one randomly scheduled five-minute game each hour. A weighted slowdown question or paid pause interrupts their games. Each account gets one free slowdown question per UTC day. Leaders recover before they can be paused again.

      Final clan results and Hall of Fame

      iMore info about final clan results

      Monthly and seasonal results combine roster scores within the period. Crews need three entrants and at least one bank to place. At least two crews must qualify. Ties share first place. Entrants who banked for a winning crew earn the in-game award.
