Why Join a Faction?

Battle Chamber is the public proof layer. Join a faction to make your arcade activity count for something bigger. Your runs build clout. Your faction earns pressure.

  • Unlock faction daily and seasonal missions
  • Unlock faction perks and roguelite options
  • Unlock faction proof — your activity becomes public
  • Appear on public weekly, monthly, and seasonal boards
  • Build weekly, monthly, and seasonal clout
  • Gain access to faction-specific roguelite paths
  • Compete for badges, stickers, titles, and XP prize pool eligibility
  • Contribute to faction war standing and help your faction dominate

Weekly and monthly winners get visibility, badges, stickers, and roguelite advantages. No action, no proof. More action, more clout.
