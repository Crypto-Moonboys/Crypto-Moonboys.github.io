What You Are Building

A Moonboy is not merely an NFT or profile picture. It is a recognisable creator identity. You may build around one character or connect several characters from different factions into a larger world.

Products & MerchandiseDevelop truthful, creator-led applications such as clothing, packaging, prints, stickers or product characters.
Content & StoryBuild videos, comics, animation, music, podcasts, campaigns and creator-owned lore.
