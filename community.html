Crypto Moonboys is a creator identity movement. Before you grind missions or collect XP, start with one question: who do you want to be inside this universe? Two clear paths exist.

Path 1: Build a Moonboy Identity No existing identity required. Forge a character inside the universe — join a faction, participate through games and missions, build reputation and earn proof. Work toward a unique 1/1 Moonboy: your owned character identity, creator licence and royalties.

How You Build Reputation Inside the Movement

Reputation in Crypto Moonboys is earned through consistent participation — not just by signing up. The route below is how identity becomes visible and proof-backed inside the community.

  1. Discover your identity: understand which path fits you — a new Moonboy character or your existing identity.
  2. Join the community: link Telegram so your presence and progress become persistent.
  3. Choose your faction: pick your crew and align your activity to a collective.
  4. Create and participate: missions, games, quests and Battle Chamber activity are how you show up.

Step 5: Forge Your Identity — Join the Builder Army

The 1/1 Moonboy is the destination of the Path 1 identity route. It is your owned character identity — a creator licence with 100% of the royalties from what you build around it. Crypto Moonboys and Graffiti Kings take no royalty share. The 1/1 can anchor your story, your creator world and your place inside the wider movement.

Published creator terms should define the exact licence and royalty position for each 1/1. The public copy should only promise what those terms support.

Every 1/1 holder becomes a HODL Warrior builder: part of the active creator army that helps each other build, promote, prove momentum and keep the movement visible.
