Moongirls Mech

Waterfront Roamers · Civic service carrier · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

The Moongirls Mech runs supplies between service crews on Lumina’s Waterfront. Its bright pink and green panels are easy to identify when it arrives with clean containers or a replacement fitting. Its operator was taught to record who receives a load because a storekeeper cannot replace what a record says is still on the shelf.

The wrong service room

A worker once collected the same spare twice after an early receipt named the wrong service room. The operator had been in a hurry and filled in the destination from memory. The corrected receipt stayed pinned above the bench well after the spare was returned. The operator still carries blank slips and gives anyone time to read their own before signing.

Off duty

She builds small paper models of overlooked public rooms, including the chair behind the clerk who does the evening count. The models go on a shelf that is gradually beginning to resemble the city by the places where people wait.
