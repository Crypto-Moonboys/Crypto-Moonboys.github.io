⚔️ The DUCKY BOYS

⚔️ Lore 📅 Last updated: March 2026 📂 Lore

The Ducky Boys are the fixers, mechanics and maintenance culture of the fractured world. While other factions argue about truth, status or revolution, the Ducky Boys are usually asking a less glamorous question: what has actually broken, and what will keep working tonight?

Repair Is Their Politics

The post-Unravelling world is full of systems no ideology can operate without: power, cooling, pumps, old servers, vehicles, bridges, repeaters and patched code. Ducky Boy culture grows around keeping that infrastructure alive.

They are deliberately practical. A repair does not need to be elegant if the alternative is darkness, lost water or a dead route.

Digital Duct Tape

W81 gives the faction colourful names such as Digital Duct Tape and wrench-bots. Those can remain part of their workshop slang and visual language without implying one magical repair technology solves every problem.

The deeper rule is improvisation: understand enough of an old system to keep it useful even when the original manufacturer, protocol or documentation has vanished.

Utility Across Faction Lines

Ducky Boys work for Block Topia, Street Kingdom crews, traders and rebels because machinery does not care who won the argument. This makes them politically valuable and morally uncomfortable.

Technical neutrality can preserve lives. It can also become an excuse to keep abusive systems functioning. Their recurring First Witness problem is whether fixing a machine means helping whatever the machine is being used to do.

The Games Economy

Hard Fork Games, faction gatherings and large conflicts create intense demand for temporary repair hubs, broadcast maintenance, armour work and route recovery. Ducky Boys often make more during crisis than peace.

That produces a suspicion they cannot entirely escape: the people best at repairing war damage can become financially comfortable with a world that keeps breaking.

The Unfixable Problem

NULL is terrifying to Ducky Boy culture because it is not merely a broken component. It attacks the meaning of the information used to decide what 'working' means.

The faction's response is characteristically stubborn: isolate the fault, test what still behaves consistently, compare independent records and keep enough infrastructure alive for somebody else to solve what they cannot.

The Faction Weakness

The Ducky Boys embody Book III's maintenance ethic: repair gets no statue, but civilisation dies without it.

Their weakness is confusing competence with moral permission. A person who can fix anything still has to decide what should remain broken.
