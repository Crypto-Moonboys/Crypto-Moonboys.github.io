Project association

The project's Community & People category places iangharrison alongside other writers, creators and contributors. That is a direct public project attribution. It supports inclusion in the contributor reference, although it does not by itself establish a job title, contractual position or complete list of work.

The older individual page describes Harrison as a partner to Crypto Moonboys and associates him with GraffPUNKS. It uses GraffPUNK General in connection with game development. Those descriptions are preserved as the project's inherited account, rather than expanded into an independently verified professional history.

A creative title used within a community does not automatically identify a corporate office, employment arrangement, financial responsibility or command over the fictional forty factions. The association can be recorded without inventing those additional meanings.

A later partnership, not an origin claim

The inherited real-world note says Harrison joined after Graffiti Kings had moved into the online, Web3 and WAX era. It explicitly distinguishes that later partnership from membership of the original Graffiti Kings formation. This page retains that distinction as the existing project account.

The available material supplies no reliable joining date, first credited project or dated announcement that would justify a more precise chronology. It also provides no foundation for adding childhood, education, residence, private relationships or earlier employment.

The public writing trail

The Community & People material identifies a writing and contributor context, and the older Harrison Rift page links the iangharrison Medium profile. That address remains a useful source trail for work published under the handle.

A profile link is not a citation for every statement made elsewhere about its owner. A specific authorship claim requires the specific work and its byline. The reviewed material does not identify a reliable title-by-title bibliography for Harrison, so no publication list, dates or readership figures are invented here.

The limits of the earlier technical portrait

The previous entry portrayed Harrison as a technical validator whose methods produced hidden checksums, minting safeguards and a security layer spanning collections and game systems. Its cited source paths were not available in the reviewed repository, and the inspected material supplies no implementation or attributable technical record supporting those detailed claims.

They are therefore not retained as established achievements. The same applies to assertions that one contributor stabilised the Genesis Kernel, governs disputed assets or determines the behaviour of every new NFT release. Some of those phrases refer to fictional concepts; others imply real deployed responsibilities that require separate evidence.

This correction does not assert that Harrison performed no technical work. It removes a false level of precision from the reference. Actual engineering contributions can be described when linked to inspectable work, an approved project statement or another suitable public source.

The Harrison Rift is a separate fictional entry

The Harrison Rift is a legacy fictional schism whose older page says it was named for Harrison. The reviewed record does not establish who coined the title or verify the real person's authorship or endorsement of that story.

Its new Press Room Eight treatment uses explicitly invented characters, Nessa Ferrule and Arlo Kerf. Their disputed decisions are fictional actions, not representations of Harrison or other real collaborators. A real-person reference should not turn the event into a disguised allegation about project relationships.

The W81 Crypto Protocol chronicle is separate again. It tells a time-travel story involving the GKniftyHEADS, the Machine, Thorne and the Order of Man; it does not name Harrison. Its altered 2024 timeline is not evidence of his real career or of a historical event outside the fiction.

The person and the Year 3008 world

Harrison's contributor association belongs to the contemporary project. The First Witness's 2030 Great Concord, 2880 World Chain Triple Fork and principal 3008 saga describe the fictional setting; they do not make present collaborators witnesses of those events or give them a fictional protocol's powers. A shared name, tribute or community title does not establish a character identity.

Credit, permission and present responsibilities

A contributor credit identifies participation; it does not by itself grant authority to license all project artwork, speak for every creator or promise a particular product release. The project's current creator rules and the terms attached to specific work govern those questions.

Likewise, this archive entry is not a current staff directory or contact instruction. The inherited role descriptions are not used to infer availability, a present management position or permission to publish on another person's behalf.
