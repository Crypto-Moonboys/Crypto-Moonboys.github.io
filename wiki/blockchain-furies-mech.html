Blockchain Furies Mech

Waterfront Roamers · Claims witness carrier · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

The Blockchain Furies Mech carries equipment for public claims hearings. Its crew is drawn to disputes that leave a gap between the tidy record and a person’s damaged property or lost wages. The white chassis has a distinctive Furies badge and enough space to bring documents and a fold-out table to a place both parties can reach.

The missing handover

One evening its crew arrived with a convincing sequence of freight receipts. A worker standing at the edge of the table pointed out that the sequence started after a load had already changed hands. The crew reopened the case. The worker agreed to give evidence there after a second person offered to cover her shift.

Off duty

The operator dislikes the sound of a stamp hitting empty paper. Off duty they spend time binding discarded reports into blank notebooks. The first page of each is left for someone else to write their own account.
