📅 2025 Metaverse Launch Party

🔄 SAM Learning 📅 Lore 📅 Last updated: October 2026 📂 Lore

The 2025 Metaverse Launch Party is an archived community-event announcement associated with GKniftyHEADS and the GraffPUNKS network. The surviving wiki notice points to a Linktree profile and a registration form, and describes a proposed virtual gathering around art, NFTs and games. It does not establish that the gathering took place, what was released there, or whether its original registration route remains in use.

Community and scope

The event belongs to the real-world community history of the project. It is not one of the forty factions in the Year 3008 story, and the word “metaverse” in its title does not identify a particular venue or software platform. The original notice associated it with the Graffiti Kings, GKniftyHEADS and GraffPUNKS creative network. That association is the scope preserved here; it does not assign production responsibilities, legal sponsorship or ownership of an event to an individual.

The older notice borrowed Block Topia, faction and rebellion language as promotional storytelling. Its year refers to a contemporary community announcement, not a gathering in the Year 3008 saga.

What survives in the record

The reliable starting point is a notice, not a retrospective event report. Its title gives the year 2025. Its text presents a planned community celebration and its source list preserves two external addresses: the GKniftyHEADS Linktree profile and a Google Forms link. The copy mentions virtual gatherings, NFT drops and game previews as proposed attractions. No dated programme, venue coordinates, speaker schedule, attendance record or completed release list accompanies those claims in the material reviewed for this page.

The archived W81 source corpus supplies broader accounts of GraffPUNKS art, radio, community rewards and development ambitions, but an exact-title and registration-link search does not supply an independent account of this launch party. That matters because a general roadmap can explain why such a celebration was proposed without establishing the details of one particular event. Repetition in generated wiki summaries likewise does not provide a second witness.

The surviving title should therefore be read as a historical label. With 2025 past, presenting the notice as an upcoming party would mislead readers. The absence of a completion record here leaves its outcome unresolved; it does not prove cancellation, postponement or successful delivery.

The proposed programme

The announcement's three themes were a shared virtual meeting, digital-art activity and an introduction to future games. Taken together, they show an attempt to connect the creative network's different audiences. A listener could encounter the artwork, a collector could hear about a game, and someone attracted by the fictional world could meet the surrounding community. This is the proposal's cultural significance even where its practical arrangements remain undocumented.

The older page expanded that outline into claims about a live HODL WARS skirmish, a large NBG prize pool, a new GraffPUNKS collection, Hard Fork Games expansions and possible PMSL or NBGX rewards. It framed several of these as rumours inside Block Topia while also presenting them alongside “Known Facts.” Those details have no event-specific support in the reviewed material. They are not retained as a programme, prize offer or record of a release.

Likewise, a reference to a planned preview cannot establish that a full game was available. A concept demonstration, a limited test, a public beta and a completed release are different things. This entry preserves the historical ambition at the level the evidence supports, without converting an announcement into a product changelog.
