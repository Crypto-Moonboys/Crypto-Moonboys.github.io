Book II — The Common Blood

First WitnessCanonical scripture · Book IILast updated: October 2026

Book II refuses the idea that shared humanity requires shared culture. Difference is expected; dehumanisation is not.

The text

1 No birth makes a person more human than another.

2 Honour inheritance, but do not confuse inheritance with merit.

3 Your family is your first responsibility, not your permission to treat every stranger as less.

4 Feed the child before you ask which banner the child will carry.

5 A stranger is not innocent because they are strange, nor guilty because they are unknown.

6 Judge an act before an ancestry.

7 Do not make a bloodline answer forever for one ancestor, and do not use ancestry to excuse a present crime.

8 Your enemy remains human while opposing you. This is hardest to remember when it matters most.

9 Mercy is not the denial of consequence. Consequence without mercy becomes appetite.

10 Do not demand sameness as the price of peace.

11 Do not call contempt a tradition merely because it is old.

12 Do not call every tradition contempt merely because it is old.

13 Let communities keep names, songs, dress, rituals and stories so long as those customs do not make another person's body their property.

14 Consent cannot be inherited on behalf of the unborn.

15 Children are not vessels for unfinished wars.

16 The weak require protection from the strong; the strong require protection from becoming what strength permits.

17 A society is measured partly by how it treats the person with nothing useful to offer it.

18 Do not turn poverty, unbelief, belief, origin, disability or defeat into proof that a life is disposable.

19 Where two communities cannot agree, draw a boundary before drawing blood.

20 Where a boundary becomes a prison, reopen the argument.

21 Do not force love. Do not command belief. Do not punish a private conscience merely for being private.

22 Protect the right to leave a group. A belonging that cannot be left is possession.

23 Protect the right to remain with a group. A world that permits only solitary people is another kind of control.

24 You are not required to trust everyone. You are required to remember that distrust does not erase their humanity.

25 Common blood does not mean all are one. It means none are outside the moral account.

Reading rule

This Book belongs to the 2030 Covenant layer. Later pages may interpret it through Year 3008 events, but those later names are not retroactively part of the original text.

The wording is kept direct because the tradition had to survive translation, quotation, song, argument and wall transmission rather than depend on one ornate liturgical language.
