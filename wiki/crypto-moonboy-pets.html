Crypto Moonboy Pets Telegram Roguelite

Crypto Moonboy Pets is the Telegram-first roguelite pet game inside the Crypto Moonboys ecosystem. Players adopt a living Moonboys companion, keep it healthy, train it, complete daily and seasonal missions, and build pet progression over time.

The game is played in Telegram through the Moonboys bot. The website records the public proof layer: combined Community XP, pet-specific leaderboards, how-to-play rules, and the searchable wiki home for the pet system.

How It Works

  • Use /adopt in Telegram to create your Crypto Moonboy Pet.
  • Use /pet to see health, hunger, happiness, cleanliness, energy, stage, level and missions.
  • Use /feed, /play, /clean, /sleep and /train to grow the pet.
  • Care actions can earn Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens.
  • Use /petshop and /petbuy to equip better food, toys and clothing.
  • Use /pettrade to risk in-game Moon Gold for game-only rewards.
  • Use /petadventure for energy-gated runs with cooldowns and pet-only rewards.
  • Use /petnotify on to opt into rate-limited Telegram needs alerts.
  • Pet XP grows the companion and powers the pet-only leaderboard.
  • Accepted pet actions also award Community XP into the existing main XP leaderboard and graph.

24/7 Pet Loop

Pet notifications are opt-in. When enabled, the bot can DM a player if their pet needs food, cleaning, sleep or play, if a daily mission is still open, if an upgrade is affordable, or if an adventure is ready.

Adventures add roguelite runs on top of care actions. They cost energy, use cooldowns, award pet XP and in-game currencies, and stay separate from Community XP.

In-Game Economy

Crypto Moonboy Pets uses three non-transferable pet currencies: Moon Gold for common upgrades and trades, Moon Crystals for rarer upgrades, and Style Tokens for clothing. These currencies are gameplay counters only and cannot be cashed out.

Pet XP unlocks higher shop tiers. Shop upgrades make the daily care loop stronger, but Community XP remains separately capped and server-accepted.

XP Separation

Crypto Moonboy Pets has its own pet progression, but it is not a separate identity universe. Accepted pet actions write to the existing Telegram XP system, so pet-earned Community XP is included in the current main XP leaderboard and graph.

Detailed pet stats stay on Crypto Moonboy Pets pages. Current community pages only show a compact pet summary or call to action.

Seasons

The pet game runs forever through automatic UTC daily, weekly and 90-day seasonal windows. New seasons do not require manual reset. When a player returns after a rollover, the server creates the next season state automatically.
