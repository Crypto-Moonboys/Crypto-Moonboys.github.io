Crypto Moonboy Pets Telegram Pet RPG / 24-7 Roguelite

Crypto Moonboy Pets is an HTML5 Telegram Mini App RPG and roguelite inside the Crypto Moonboys ecosystem. The bot launches Moonpet OS; the current build focuses on care, Daily Journey, Weekly Journey, jobs, runs, equipment, Arena, Kaiju and long-term progression instead of old chat-button menus.

Open Moonpet OS in Telegram

Moonpet OS inside Telegram is the main gameplay surface. The bot is the authenticated launcher and notification bridge; the website provides the public rules, leaderboard, linked-player summaries, Community XP context and searchable wiki record.

Season 1 Rules And Status

Community-only: Crypto Moonboy Pets has no cash purchases, crypto purchases, token cashout, paid recovery path or financial rewards. Arcade XP is earned community progression with no cash value.

LIVE — Season Length

Seasons target a 90-day progression cycle. The current runtime calendar resets at UTC year boundaries, so a year-end partial season may be shorter.

LIVE — Three Pets

Each player can have at most three seasonal Moonpets. Pet 1 is free each season; Pet 2 unlocks for 500 spendable Arcade XP and Pet 3 for 1,000 spendable Arcade XP. These are the current Season 1 earned community XP unlocks—not money or crypto purchases.

LIVE — Active Pet

One seasonal Moonpet is active at a time. Pet-instance actions resolve against the active or explicitly participating pet_id. Switching preserves that pet’s identity, stats, lifecycle and pet-specific progression. Seasonal XP, seasonal tiers, leaderboard progression and other explicitly account-level systems remain shared account seasonal state.

COMING SOON — Future Systems

Advanced Traits, Breeding, Lineage, Fusion, Sanctuary and Prestige remain roadmap systems. They should be presented as future work, not disguised as current Moonpet OS gameplay.

Legendary target — IN DEVELOPMENT: 60 Growth Marks, 10 Weekly Crests, and the required Pet XP and trials. Growth Marks are limited to one per pet per day and Weekly Crests to one per pet per week. In a full target 90-day cycle, the requirements allow roughly 30 missed daily Mark opportunities and roughly 3 missed weekly Crest windows—approximately three to four weeks of scheduling flexibility before additional balancing or catch-up is required. Partial year-end seasons require separate balancing because their progression window may be shorter.

Catch-up — FUTURE: Rested XP, behind-pace bonuses and one difficult seasonal Crest recovery challenge per pet are planned. They will never fabricate Growth Marks, Weekly Crests or Arcade XP, and there will be no paid recovery route.

Start Playing

  1. Open @WIKICOMSBOT.
  2. Run /gkstart, then /gklink to connect your Telegram identity.
  3. Open Moonpet OS from the bot menu and initialise your Moonpet inside the app.
  4. Use the PET module to check needs and the Recommended Next Move panel for coaching.
  5. Use TASKS, GEAR and CORE to inspect missions, achievements, equipment, alerts and specialist progression.

The Live Game Loop

Care

Feed, play, clean, sleep and train your Moonpet. Accepted care actions can affect needs, Pet XP, currencies, specialist XP and traits.

Prepare

Buy care upgrades, equip battle gear, manage authoritative bag items and check daily missions before committing to jobs, Arena battles or runs.

Risk

Choose from 14 stage- and specialist-gated jobs, 10 three-choice random encounters, timed activities, authored district missions, persistent story choices and a 100-room Standard Moon Run. Later evolutions reveal harder work and stranger encounters.

Compete

Fight one personal weekly boss attempt per UTC day and compare seasonal Pet XP. Arena and Kaiju are current-build systems. Kaiju requires a hatched active Moonpet, and Arena uses that same hatch gate plus level 10 for the active battler.

Follow The Coach

The Moonpet Coach prioritises urgent care, active runs, ready claims, evolution requirements, verified bounties, missions, weekly bosses, expeditions and affordable upgrades. Every economy recommendation carries exact progress, cost or remaining attempts, its reward/use and a direct action button. One-time alerts announce newly available traits, achievements, jobs, equipment, economy features and season rewards without repeating forever.

Current build focus: Pet, Care, Daily Journey, Weekly Journey, Jobs, Runs, Equipment, Arena, Kaiju and Progression are the active gameplay priorities. The next phase is to deepen and clarify that loop rather than pretend roadmap panels are already live.

Personality, Memories, Achievements And Evolution

Personality

Combat, exploration, care and event behaviour can unlock Street Fighter, Explorer, Loyal and Curious traits. Those traits now change Moonpet reaction copy during jobs, events, status checks and weekly bosses.

Memories

Your Moonpet remembers its first run, first extraction, first boss, completed runs, boss victories, favourite activity, biggest reward and evolution milestones. These records influence what it says.

Achievements

The TASKS module displays 12 permanent goals covering care, jobs, event variety, runs, bosses, personality and evolution. Progress is rebuilt from authoritative game history rather than trusted client claims.

Evolution

Moonpet OS follows one canonical sequence: Moon Egg, Street Moonpet, Cyber Moonpet, Elite Moonpet and Legendary Moon Guardian. Requirements can include level, Alley King victories, relics and canonical material balances.

Evolution is no longer cosmetic-only. Each later stage unlocks its own jobs and encounters, adds bounded weekly-boss power, changes reactions, and adds one extra Style Token per claimed seasonal tier. Stages cannot be skipped, and Advanced Traits remain a separate future expansion rather than a synonym for current personality or aptitude systems.

Three Separate Progression Layers

Pet XP

Controls the Moonpet’s level, growth stage, unlocks and pet leaderboard position.

Specialist XP

Records how the pet is played through Care, Training, Adventure, Arena, Job and Bond progression.

Community XP

Accepted linked actions can contribute to the player’s existing Crypto Moonboys profile. It remains separate from Pet XP and specialist XP.

Server authority — LIVE: reward-bearing actions finalize through the Cloudflare Worker/server with source allowlisting, stable idempotency keys and duplicate protection. The client cannot self-award progression. IN DEVELOPMENT: Pet XP will use a daily target followed by diminishing returns rather than a hard stop, while protected Growth Mark and Weekly Crest limits preserve seasonal fairness.

Six Specialist Tracks

  • Care: feed, play, clean, sleep and selected training actions.
  • Training: instant training and completed timed training.
  • Adventure: exploration, successful run steps and extracted runs.
  • Arena: competitive combat actions and completed Arena outcomes when the server-owned hooks apply.
  • Job: short jobs and completed timed work; Job XP also determines job rank.
  • Bond: positive care behaviour and daily chest claims.

Daily specialist counters reset by UTC day. Permanent track totals remain. Repeated behaviour also contributes to hidden trait thresholds that become visible in CORE once unlocked.

Care And Battle Loadouts

Care Loadout: Food, Toy and Outfit. These affect care actions, selected currencies, Pet XP and some run modifiers.

Battle Loadout: armor, weapon and charm. Armor supports defense, weapons support attack, and charms support crit, dodge or luck.

The two loadouts are independent. Changing one does not reset the other. Empty battle slots display none; the game does not invent starter armor or starter weapons.

The GEAR module opens care upgrades and Pet Arena equipment, buys and equips eligible items, and displays registered equipment levels, item XP, mastery XP and mastery tiers. The server repairs missing progression records for equipment already fitted to the pet.

Expanded Moonpet Economy

GEAR is the earn → spend → upgrade → unlock dashboard. Bounties select four goals from ten each UTC day and only pay goals proven by accepted server events. Expeditions provide three daily attempts across Dust Tunnels, Crystal Caves and Guardian Rift, with higher zones unlocked by level. The Market rotates four offers from twelve, each with one unit of personal daily stock.

The larger loop connects care, 14 standard and elite jobs, activities, events, runs, Arena, Kaiju, bosses and seasons to Moon Gold, Moon Crystals, Style Tokens, consumables and nine canonical upgrade materials. Consumable items and materials have separate, explicit balance authorities. The Workshop turns collected materials into five useful consumable recipes with level gates, exact cost previews and protected one-time settlement. Daily caps, Energy costs, wallet checks, stable settlement keys and one-stock offers prevent unlimited faucets.

Current build status: the Mini App already exposes jobs, bounties, shops, markets, expeditions, equipment upgrades, loadout-set synergies, story-facing progression and season rewards. Prestige does not belong in the live-now list; keep it marked as coming soon until a later reviewed build makes it truly playable.

Timed Activities, Jobs And Events

The WORK module starts timed Sleep, Train, Work and Explore sessions; shows the active timer; and exposes Claim or Cancel when applicable. It also lists all 14 jobs with their level, evolution and specialist-track gates. PET contains the UTC daily cache, while RUN contains the ten stage-gated random encounters.

The minimum timed claim is five minutes. Training caps at two hours. Sleep, work and exploration cap at eight hours. Valid longer sessions scale rewards up to their cap; cancellation awards nothing.

Activity claims are protected against claim/cancel/expiry races. If a reward settlement is interrupted after persistence, retry recovery returns the original authoritative reward instead of creating a second award or losing the completed session.

Weekly Boss

The RUN module opens the current personal weekly boss. The boss rotates deterministically each UTC week between Neon Titan, Rugpull Colossus, Kaiju Zero and Citadel Overlord. Choose Strike, Outsmart or Endure; matching the boss weakness, relevant personality and a later evolution stage improve damage.

Each player gets one attempt per UTC day, enforced by a D1 unique constraint. An attempt costs 12 Energy. Damage persists for the week, the boss reward settles once through the protected reward authority, and delivery retries recover the same result rather than creating extra attempts or rewards.

Pet Run Engine And Roguelite Foundation

The RUN module starts or resumes the 100-room Standard Moon Run. Every room presents an authored location, objective and opponent plus server-derived previews for risk, costs, reward ranges and equipped-gear advantage. Elite encounters appear every five rooms and district bosses every ten.

Rewards remain unbanked until extraction or terminal completion. Extracting banks the current bag and contributes to Adventure progression; a failed room loses unbanked loot but may retain a small Pet XP lesson. The separate Daily Moon Run provides one authoritative seeded run per UTC day.

The backend persists region, deterministic seed, room state, temporary modifiers, permanent relic ownership, terminal run history and balancing analytics without creating new uncapped XP paths.

Run completion, room and boss reward requests are state-checked and idempotent. Terminal run transitions serialize against competing step callbacks so a late room action cannot mutate a reward snapshot after extraction or completion begins.

Roguelite Rewards, Modifiers And Relics

Run Modifiers

Temporary run-scoped effects can be persisted and are removed at terminal run states. Validation rejects XP, cap, currency and permanent-stat mutations.

Relics

Permanent relic ownership now has a protected persistence layer with rarity, unlock time and effect metadata. Future relic bonuses must still request rewards through the central reward authority.

Boss Rewards

The starter roguelite boss path is deliberately not an XP farm. Repeatable boss rewards favor bounded materials, an evolution item and idempotent relic ownership rather than repeatable Pet XP or Community XP.

Analytics

Run start, room, modifier, boss and end events can be recorded for future balancing, difficulty tuning and content expansion.

Pet Arena

Moonpet OS exposes the current-build turn-based Moonpet combat system, but live Arena entry is gated. Your active Moonpet must be hatched, and Arena still needs a level 10+ active Moonpet. Pet Arena is separate from Kaiju Sticker Battle.

Battle power is calculated server-side using pet level, Pet XP, health, energy, morale, cleanliness, care modifiers and Battle Loadout equipment. The Mini App supports global player matchmaking, same-rank preference, any-rank widening, ready-up, locked turns, polling and solo CRT practice.

Move cards disclose accuracy, base damage, role, counters and Special charge requirements. Solo CRT intent is telegraphed before commitment; player-versus-player intent remains sealed until both choices lock. Perspective-correct round recaps explain what each side did.

Round-scoped controls reject stale moves, duplicate taps and replayed callbacks. Winners, losers and draws receive capped game rewards through the protected reward authority.

Kaiju Sticker Battle

Kaiju Sticker Battle is also part of the current build, and live entry requires a hatched active Moonpet in the Mini App rollout.

Moonpet OS contains the separate Kaiju card-battle system with global player matchmaking and solo CRT practice. Both players choose their cards inside the Mini App and receive an oriented result.

The game selects one category from Power, Size, Attack, Defence, Speed or Legacy before card commitment. Every card shows its value in the active category plus its strongest stats, while the rival card remains sealed. The higher active value wins. Kaiju progression retains protected repeat slots, Energy reservations, original accounting windows and retry-recovery safeguards.

Economy, Inventory And Materials

Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens are non-transferable game currencies. They support care upgrades, battle equipment, trades and other game-only rewards.

telegram_pet_inventory is the authoritative consumable-item balance used by the bag, item use, run choices and banked run items. Canonical crafting materials use telegram_pet_material_balances. Audit events record what happened but are not a second balance authority.

The pet bag is separate from equipped loadouts. Consumables include moon_snack, energy_drink, clean_wipe, lucky_charm, style_patch and adventure_map.

Repeatable roguelite material and item rewards are service-capped so new run content cannot turn into an unlimited inventory faucet. The GEAR screen's Crafting Workshop offers Street Rations, Clean Kits, Battery Packs, Style Patches and Route Maps. Craft requests recheck live material balances, consume costs and award the item in one protected settlement, so retries cannot mint twice.

Missions, Seasons And Leaderboards

Daily missions reset by UTC day. Weekly windows and automatic pet seasons roll over independently without a manual reset. Seasons target 90 days, but the runtime resets its season calendar at the UTC year boundary, which can shorten the final season of a year.

/petseason shows four one-time tiers at 250, 1,000, 3,000 and 7,500 seasonal Pet XP. Each tier is unique per player and season in D1. Claims use stable settlement keys, and the Moonpet’s evolution stage adds a small bounded Style bonus without increasing XP caps.

/petleaderboard and the website leaderboard rank Pet XP across daily, weekly, seasonal and all-time views. Specialist track levels, traits, job rank, equipment records, relic ownership and run-history data remain personal development systems rather than the current public leaderboard score.

IN DEVELOPMENT: Pet XP may beat its daily target at reduced rates so players with concentrated play time can still make progress. FUTURE: after a heavy-grind threshold, continued activity will provide only tiny prestige or cosmetic value. Marks and Crests—not a paid shortcut—protect the intended seasonal pace.

Security, Caps And Duplicate Protection

The Moonboys API owns cooldowns, caps, equipment state, currencies, inventory and accepted progression. Progression-bearing actions use stable server-controlled event or idempotency keys.

Event and Kaiju repeat rewards use protected daily slots; LIVE: global Pet XP and Community XP caps remain enforced today. Duplicate or concurrent callbacks settle once. Timed activity claims, run terminal transitions, item awards and item consumption also use protected state transitions so retries repair interrupted work instead of multiplying progression.

Planned anti-runaway direction — IN DEVELOPMENT: unlimited gameplay should not mean unlimited full-rate progression. The broader balancing direction uses diminishing returns, protected reward tiers, per-pet Mark/Crest limits and idempotency to prevent runaway XP farming; it has not replaced today’s live global XP caps.
