Crypto Moonboy Pets Telegram Roguelite

Crypto Moonboy Pets is the Telegram-first roguelite pet game inside the Crypto Moonboys ecosystem. Players adopt a living Moonboys companion, keep it healthy, train it, manage a pet bag, work jobs, open daily chests, handle random crate events, battle directly in Pet Arena, battle Kaiju sticker cards, and build pet progression over time.

The game is played in Telegram through the Moonboys bot. The website records the public proof layer: combined Community XP, pet-specific leaderboards, how-to-play rules, and the searchable wiki home for the pet system.

How It Works

  • Use /adopt in Telegram to create your Crypto Moonboy Pet.
  • Use /pet to see health, hunger, happiness, cleanliness, energy, stage, level and missions.
  • Use /feed, /play, /clean, /sleep and /train to grow the pet.
  • Care actions can earn Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens.
  • Use /petbag and /petuse to manage inventory from Telegram pet events.
  • Use /petwork to run pet jobs like courier or crystal miner.
  • Use /petdaily once per UTC day to claim the chest.
  • Use /petevent to open, sell or ignore random crate events.
  • Use /petshop and /petbuy to equip better food, toys and clothing.
  • Use /pettrade to risk in-game Moon Gold for game-only rewards.
  • Use /petadventure for energy-gated runs with cooldowns and pet-only rewards.
  • Use /petarena for level 10+ Pet Arena battles using Moonpet stats and gear.
  • Use /kaiju in the Telegram group to enter Kaiju Sticker Battle.
  • Use /petnotify on to opt into rate-limited Telegram needs alerts.
  • Pet XP grows the companion and powers the pet-only leaderboard.
  • Accepted pet actions also award Community XP into the existing main XP leaderboard and graph, but inventory, jobs, daily chests and random events stay pet-only.

Pet Arena In Telegram

Pet Arena is the Moonpet-vs-Moonpet battle mode and is not Kaiju Sticker Battle. It unlocks at level 10; the bot blocks lower-level pets with “Pet Arena unlocks at level 10. Keep growing your Moonpet.”

Turn-based combat stats are calculated server-side from pet level, pet XP tier, health, energy, happiness/morale, cleanliness condition, equipped food, toy, outfit and battle gear. New battle gear uses armor, weapon and charm slots: armor improves defense/damage reduction, weapons improve attack, and charms improve crit, dodge or luck. Low energy lowers battle power and very low health can block entry or heavily reduce power.

Private chats can battle an app pet turn by turn instead of instantly rolling a result. Each round shows HP, special meter and buttons for Attack Head, Attack Body, Block Head, Block Body, Charge Special, Special Move and Forfeit; after you choose, the app AI chooses server-side and the bot posts the next round.

Matchmaking prefers rank buckets: rookie level 10-14, scrapper 15-24, enforcer 25-39, cyber beast 40-69 and moon warlord 70+. It tries same bucket first, then nearby buckets, then Accept Any Rank. Mismatch wins can grant underdog bonuses while high-level wins over low-level pets reduce rewards.

Rewards are capped and idempotent: winners receive Moon Gold, pet XP and small Community XP, losers receive consolation pet XP and draws share smaller rewards. Existing daily pet XP and Community XP caps apply and duplicate callbacks cannot double-award.

Kaiju Sticker Battle In Telegram

Kaiju Sticker Battle is the group battle mode for the same public Kaiju cards used by the Arcade page. The Telegram bot should use the new Kaiju sticker set as the visual layer and the existing card IDs and stats as the rules layer.

  • /kaiju shows the current group table: active match, champion, challenger, waiting queue and quick buttons.
  • /kaiju solo starts one player against CPU if no two-player match is active.
  • /kaiju join joins the group queue if a match is already active, or becomes challenger if a champion is waiting.
  • /kaiju leave removes the player from the waiting queue.
  • /kaiju deck shows the player's available Kaiju cards and their sticker buttons.
  • /kaiju pick or a sticker button locks the player's card for the next round.

Only one live Kaiju match should run per Telegram group at a time. The bot stores that group match in D1 using the chat ID, so the match survives Worker restarts and cannot split into duplicate games.

Kaiju Queue Rules

The group uses a simple arcade cabinet rule: winner stays on, loser leaves the table, next queued player becomes challenger. This keeps the chat readable while still letting many users wait their turn.

  • If nobody is playing, the first player becomes champion and can start a CPU match or wait for a challenger.
  • If one player is waiting, the next player to tap Join becomes challenger and the two-player match starts.
  • If two players are active, everyone else is added to a first-in, first-out queue.
  • When a match ends, the winner remains champion.
  • The loser is removed from the table and may rejoin at the back of the queue.
  • The first queued user automatically becomes the next challenger.
  • If the queue is empty, the champion can keep playing CPU until another user joins.
  • Inactive turns should expire after a short timeout so one player cannot block the whole group.

A five-round match is recommended. Each round: both players lock a sticker card, the bot rolls 1-6, the rolled number chooses the category, and the higher stat wins the round. After five rounds, the round winner count decides the match winner. Ties can go to sudden death or stay as draw depending on the bot setting.

Kaiju Bot State Model

The implementation should use D1 tables instead of in-memory queues. Minimum state: chat ID, champion Telegram ID, challenger Telegram ID, queue order, current round, locked cards, rolled category, match status, expiry time and final result. This prevents duplicate matches and lets the bot recover safely after a deploy.

The bot should also reject a second active match in the same chat. If a user tries to start another game while one is active, it replies with the current table and a Join Queue button instead of starting a new session.

24/7 Pet Loop

Pet notifications are opt-in. When enabled, the bot can DM a player if their pet needs food, cleaning, sleep or play, if a daily mission is still open, if an upgrade is affordable, or if an adventure, job or crate choice is ready.

Adventures add roguelite runs on top of care actions. They cost energy, use cooldowns, award pet XP and in-game currencies, and stay separate from Community XP.

Timed Activity Sessions

Timed activities layer on top of the existing instant Feed, Clean, Play, Train and Sleep buttons. A pet can have one active session at a time with /petstart sleep, /petstart train, /petstart work or /petstart explore. Players can inspect it with /petactivity, finish it with /petclaim, or stop it with /petcancel.

Activity rewards scale by time spent while staying capped: 5 minutes is tiny, 30 minutes is normal, 2 hours is strong, sleep/work/explore cap at 8 hours, and train caps at 2 hours. Pet XP and Community XP still use the existing daily caps, claims are idempotent, and cancelled or expired sessions do not create infinite farming loops.

Sleep restores energy and health, train gives Pet XP while spending energy and needs, work grants Moon Gold plus small Pet XP, and explore can roll loot such as Moon Gold, Moon Crystals, an adventure map, or small Pet XP.

In-Game Economy

Crypto Moonboy Pets uses three non-transferable pet currencies: Moon Gold for common upgrades and trades, Moon Crystals for rarer upgrades, and Style Tokens for clothing. The pet bag also contains usable items such as moon_snack, energy_drink, clean_wipe, lucky_charm, style_patch and adventure_map. These currencies and items are gameplay counters only and cannot be cashed out.

Pet XP unlocks higher shop tiers. Shop upgrades make the daily care loop stronger, but Community XP remains separately capped and server-accepted.

XP Separation

Crypto Moonboy Pets has its own pet progression, but it is not a separate identity universe. Accepted care actions and trades can write to the existing Telegram XP system, so pet-earned Community XP is included in the current main XP leaderboard and graph. Inventory uses, jobs, daily chests and random events do not award Community XP.

Detailed pet stats stay on Crypto Moonboy Pets pages. Current community pages only show a compact pet summary or call to action.

Seasons

The pet game runs forever through automatic UTC daily, weekly and 90-day seasonal windows. New seasons do not require manual reset. When a player returns after a rollover, the server creates the next season state automatically.
