Crypto Moonboy Pets Telegram Pet RPG / 24-7 Roguelite

Crypto Moonboy Pets is the live Telegram-first pet RPG and roguelite inside the Crypto Moonboys ecosystem. Players adopt a Moonpet, shape its personality and memories, unlock achievements, evolve through five identity stages, complete a larger pool of jobs and events, fight a changing weekly boss, claim 90-day season rewards, enter Pet Arena, play Kaiju Sticker Battle, and push through bank-or-risk Pet Runs.

The Telegram bot remains the main game surface. The website provides the public information layer: current rules, the pet leaderboard, linked-player summaries, Community XP context and the searchable wiki record for the game.

Start Playing

  1. Open @WIKICOMSBOT.
  2. Run /gkstart, then /gklink to connect your Telegram identity.
  3. Run /adopt to create your Moonpet.
  4. Run /pet to see needs, health, currencies, missions, active activity and equipped loadouts.
  5. Run /petcoach whenever you need one recommended next action and a direct button.
  6. Run /petprogress and /petgear to inspect specialist progression and equipment records.

The Live Game Loop

Care

Feed, play, clean, sleep and train your Moonpet. Accepted care actions can affect needs, Pet XP, currencies, specialist XP and traits.

Prepare

Buy care upgrades, equip battle gear, manage authoritative bag items and check daily missions before committing to jobs, Arena battles or runs.

Risk

Choose from 12 stage-gated jobs, 10 three-choice random encounters, timed activities and five-step Pet Runs. Later evolutions reveal harder work and stranger encounters.

Compete

Fight one personal weekly boss attempt per UTC day, enter level 10+ Pet Arena, play Kaiju Sticker Battle and compare seasonal Pet XP.

Follow The Coach

The Moonpet Coach prioritises urgent care, active runs, ready claims, evolution requirements, verified bounties, missions, weekly bosses, expeditions and affordable upgrades. Every economy recommendation carries exact progress, cost or remaining attempts, its reward/use and a direct action button. One-time alerts announce newly available traits, achievements, jobs, equipment, economy features and season rewards without repeating forever.

Personality, Memories, Achievements And Evolution

Personality

Combat, exploration, care and event behaviour can unlock Street Fighter, Explorer, Loyal and Curious traits. Those traits now change Moonpet reaction copy during jobs, events, status checks and weekly bosses.

Memories

Your Moonpet remembers its first run, first extraction, first boss, completed runs, boss victories, favourite activity, biggest reward and evolution milestones. These records influence what it says.

Achievements

/petachievements displays 12 permanent goals covering care, jobs, event variety, runs, bosses, personality and evolution. Progress is rebuilt from authoritative game history rather than trusted client claims.

Evolution

/petevolve attempts the next stage in order: Moon Egg, Street Moonpet, Cyber Moonpet, Elite Moonpet and Legendary Moon Guardian. Requirements can include level, boss victories, relics and inventory materials.

Evolution is no longer cosmetic-only. Each later stage unlocks its own jobs and encounters, adds bounded weekly-boss power, changes reactions, and adds one extra Style Token per claimed seasonal tier. Stages cannot be skipped.

Three Separate Progression Layers

Pet XP

Controls the Moonpet’s level, growth stage, unlocks and pet leaderboard position.

Specialist XP

Records how the pet is played through Care, Training, Adventure, Arena, Job and Bond progression.

Community XP

Accepted linked actions can contribute to the player’s existing Crypto Moonboys profile. It remains separate from Pet XP and specialist XP.

Server authority: reward-bearing actions now finalize through a central protected reward authority. Pet XP remains capped at 1,200 per UTC day and Community XP at 250 per UTC day, with source allowlisting, stable idempotency keys and duplicate protection.

Six Specialist Tracks

  • Care: feed, play, clean, sleep and selected training actions.
  • Training: instant training and completed timed training.
  • Adventure: exploration, successful run steps and extracted runs.
  • Arena: competitive combat actions and completed Arena outcomes when the server-owned hooks apply.
  • Job: short jobs and completed timed work; Job XP also determines job rank.
  • Bond: positive care behaviour and daily chest claims.

Daily specialist counters reset by UTC day. Permanent track totals remain. Repeated behaviour also contributes to hidden trait thresholds that become visible through /petprogress once unlocked.

Care And Battle Loadouts

Care Loadout: Food, Toy and Outfit. These affect care actions, selected currencies, Pet XP and some run modifiers.

Battle Loadout: armor, weapon and charm. Armor supports defense, weapons support attack, and charms support crit, dodge or luck.

The two loadouts are independent. Changing one does not reset the other. Empty battle slots display none; the game does not invent starter armor or starter weapons.

/petshop opens care upgrades and Pet Arena equipment. /petbuy item_key buys and equips eligible items. /petgear displays registered equipment levels, item XP, mastery XP and mastery tiers, and repairs missing records for equipment already fitted to the pet.

Expanded Moonpet Economy

/peteconomy is the earn → spend → upgrade → unlock dashboard. /petbounties selects four goals from ten each UTC day and only pays goals proven by accepted server events. /petexpedition provides three daily attempts across Dust Tunnels, Crystal Caves and Guardian Rift, with higher zones unlocked by level. /petmarket rotates four offers from twelve, each with one unit of personal daily stock.

The larger loop connects existing care, jobs, activities, events, runs, Arena, Kaiju, bosses and seasons to Moon Gold, Moon Crystals, Style Tokens, consumables and upgrade materials. Daily caps, Energy costs, wallet checks, stable settlement keys and one-stock offers prevent the new routes from becoming unlimited faucets.

Current boundary: equipment progression records and display are live. The backend persists run rooms, modifiers, relic ownership, region data, run history and analytics, but not every defined future region or relic effect is a player-facing control yet.

Timed Activities, Jobs And Events

  • /petstart sleep
    Start a timed rest session.
  • /petstart train
    Build timed Training progression.
  • /petstart work
    Complete longer Job progression.
  • /petstart explore
    Build Adventure progression and possible loot.
  • /petactivity
    Inspect the active session.
  • /petclaim
    Claim a valid timed session through the protected reward path.
  • /petcancel
    Cancel without rewards.
  • /petwork
    View all 12 jobs and their level/evolution gates.
  • /petdaily
    Claim the UTC daily chest.
  • /petevent
    Generate one of 10 stage-gated encounters with three choices.

The minimum timed claim is five minutes. Training caps at two hours. Sleep, work and exploration cap at eight hours. Valid longer sessions scale rewards up to their cap; cancellation awards nothing.

Activity claims are protected against claim/cancel/expiry races. If a reward settlement is interrupted after persistence, retry recovery returns the original authoritative reward instead of creating a second award or losing the completed session.

Weekly Boss

/petboss opens the current personal weekly boss. The boss rotates deterministically each UTC week between Neon Titan, Rugpull Colossus, Kaiju Zero and Citadel Overlord. Choose Strike, Outsmart or Endure; matching the boss weakness, relevant personality and a later evolution stage improve damage.

Each player gets one attempt per UTC day, enforced by a D1 unique constraint. An attempt costs 12 Energy. Damage persists for the week, the boss reward settles once through the protected reward authority, and delivery retries recover the same result rather than creating extra attempts or rewards.

Pet Run Engine And Roguelite Foundation

/petrun still starts or resumes the current five-step Telegram run. Each step offers route choices with different costs, risk and possible rewards. Run rewards remain unbanked until extraction or boss completion.

/petextract banks current unbanked rewards. Successful steps and extraction contribute to Adventure progression. Failed runs lose unbanked loot but may retain a small Pet XP lesson. /petadventure remains an alias for the Pet Run Engine.

The merged roguelite foundation now gives the backend persistent support for regions, deterministic run seeds, room state, temporary run modifiers, permanent relic ownership, terminal run history and balancing analytics. These systems are designed for later content expansion without creating new uncapped XP paths.

Run completion, room and boss reward requests are state-checked and idempotent. Terminal run transitions serialize against competing step callbacks so a late room action cannot mutate a reward snapshot after extraction or completion begins.

Roguelite Rewards, Modifiers And Relics

Run Modifiers

Temporary run-scoped effects can be persisted and are removed at terminal run states. Validation rejects XP, cap, currency and permanent-stat mutations.

Relics

Permanent relic ownership now has a protected persistence layer with rarity, unlock time and effect metadata. Future relic bonuses must still request rewards through the central reward authority.

Boss Rewards

The starter roguelite boss path is deliberately not an XP farm. Repeatable boss rewards favor bounded materials, an evolution item and idempotent relic ownership rather than repeatable Pet XP or Community XP.

Analytics

Run start, room, modifier, boss and end events can be recorded for future balancing, difficulty tuning and content expansion.

Pet Arena

/petarena opens the level 10+ turn-based Moonpet combat system. Pet Arena is separate from Kaiju Sticker Battle.

Battle power is calculated server-side using pet level, Pet XP, health, energy, morale, cleanliness, care modifiers and Battle Loadout equipment. Private chats support app-controlled battles. Telegram groups support matchmaking, ready-up, locked turns, battle IDs and parallel pairs.

Round-scoped controls reject stale moves, duplicate taps and replayed callbacks. Winners, losers and draws receive capped game rewards through the protected reward authority.

Kaiju Sticker Battle

/petkaiju or /kaiju opens the separate Kaiju card-battle system. Private chats can play the app mode; Telegram groups can host, join or queue.

The game selects one category from Power, Size, Attack, Defence, Speed or Legacy. The selected cards are compared using that category, and the higher stat wins. Kaiju progression retains the protected repeat-slot, Energy reservation, original accounting-window and retry-recovery safeguards introduced by the anti-farm hardening.

Economy, Inventory And Materials

Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens are non-transferable game currencies. They support care upgrades, battle equipment, trades and other game-only rewards.

telegram_pet_inventory is now the authoritative item and material balance layer used by the bag, item use, run choices and banked run items. Audit events remain records of what happened but are no longer a second item-balance authority.

The pet bag is separate from equipped loadouts. Consumables include moon_snack, energy_drink, clean_wipe, lucky_charm, style_patch and adventure_map.

Repeatable roguelite material and item rewards are service-capped so new run content cannot turn into an unlimited inventory faucet. A complete player-facing crafting command and recipe loop remain future gameplay layers.

Missions, Seasons And Leaderboards

Daily missions reset by UTC day. Weekly windows and automatic 90-day pet seasons roll over independently without a manual reset.

/petseason shows four one-time tiers at 250, 1,000, 3,000 and 7,500 seasonal Pet XP. Each tier is unique per player and season in D1. Claims use stable settlement keys, and the Moonpet’s evolution stage adds a small bounded Style bonus without increasing XP caps.

/petleaderboard and the website leaderboard rank Pet XP across daily, weekly, seasonal and all-time views. Specialist track levels, traits, job rank, equipment records, relic ownership and run-history data remain personal development systems rather than the current public leaderboard score.

Security, Caps And Duplicate Protection

The Moonboys API owns cooldowns, caps, equipment state, currencies, inventory and accepted progression. Progression-bearing actions use stable server-controlled event or idempotency keys.

Event and Kaiju repeat rewards use protected daily slots, global Pet XP and Community XP caps remain enforced, and duplicate or concurrent callbacks settle once. Timed activity claims, run terminal transitions, item awards and item consumption also use protected state transitions so retries repair interrupted work instead of multiplying progression.

Anti-farm rule: unlimited gameplay does not mean unlimited progression rewards. Repeatable modes can remain playable after their rewarded allowance while protected reward tiers, daily caps and idempotency prevent endless XP farming.
