Crypto Moonboy Pets Telegram Pet RPG / 24-7 Roguelite

Crypto Moonboy Pets is the live Telegram-first pet RPG and roguelite inside the Crypto Moonboys ecosystem. Players adopt a Moonpet, maintain its needs, build Pet XP, develop six specialist progression tracks, unlock traits, register equipment, complete timed activities and jobs, enter Pet Arena, play Kaiju Sticker Battle, and attempt five-step runs with bank-or-risk decisions.

The Telegram bot is the main game surface. The website provides the public information layer: the full rules, pet leaderboard, compact linked-player summaries, Community XP context and the searchable wiki record for the game.

Start Playing

  1. Open @WIKICOMSBOT.
  2. Run /gkstart, then /gklink to connect your Telegram identity.
  3. Run /adopt to create your Moonpet.
  4. Run /pet to see its needs, health, currencies, missions, active activity and equipped loadouts.
  5. Run /petprogress and /petgear to inspect specialist progression and equipment records.

The Live Game Loop

Care

Feed, play, clean, sleep and train your Moonpet. Accepted care actions can affect needs, Pet XP, currencies, specialist XP and traits.

Prepare

Buy care upgrades, equip battle gear, manage consumables and check daily missions before committing to jobs, Arena battles or runs.

Risk

Take jobs, random encounters, timed activities and five-step roguelite routes. Decide when to continue and when to extract.

Compete

Enter level 10+ Pet Arena, play Kaiju Sticker Battle, improve seasonal Pet XP and compare results on the pet leaderboard.

Three Separate Progression Layers

Pet XP

Controls the Moonpet’s level, growth stage, unlocks and pet leaderboard position.

Specialist XP

Records how the pet is played through Care, Training, Adventure, Arena, Job and Bond progression.

Community XP

Accepted linked actions can contribute to the player’s existing Crypto Moonboys profile. It remains separate from Pet XP and specialist XP.

Server authority: one accepted action may affect several progression systems, but each layer has its own caps, validation and duplicate protection.

Six Specialist Tracks

  • Care: feed, play, clean, sleep and selected training actions.
  • Training: instant training and completed timed training.
  • Adventure: exploration, successful run steps and extracted runs.
  • Arena: competitive combat actions and completed Arena outcomes when the server-owned hooks apply.
  • Job: short jobs and completed timed work; Job XP also determines job rank.
  • Bond: positive care behaviour and daily chest claims.

Daily specialist counters reset by UTC day. Permanent track totals remain. Repeated behaviour also contributes to hidden trait thresholds that become visible through /petprogress once unlocked.

Care And Battle Loadouts

Care Loadout: Food, Toy and Outfit. These affect care actions, selected currencies, Pet XP and some run modifiers.

Battle Loadout: armor, weapon and charm. Armor supports defense, weapons support attack, and charms support crit, dodge or luck.

The two loadouts are independent. Changing one does not reset the other. Empty battle slots display none; the game does not invent starter armor or starter weapons.

/petshop opens care upgrades and Pet Arena equipment. /petbuy item_key buys and equips eligible items. /petgear displays registered equipment levels, item XP, mastery XP and mastery tiers, and repairs missing records for equipment already fitted to the pet.

Current boundary: the equipment progression schema and display system are live. Automatic mastery growth is still a foundation path and should not be described as a fully completed reward loop until its persistence wiring is finished.

Timed Activities, Jobs And Events

  • /petstart sleep
    Start a timed rest session.
  • /petstart train
    Build timed Training progression.
  • /petstart work
    Complete longer Job progression.
  • /petstart explore
    Build Adventure progression and possible loot.
  • /petactivity
    Inspect the active session.
  • /petclaim
    Claim a valid completed session.
  • /petcancel
    Cancel without rewards.
  • /petwork courier
    Run a short job.
  • /petdaily
    Claim the UTC daily chest.
  • /petevent
    Generate a random encounter with three choices.

The minimum timed claim is five minutes. Training caps at two hours. Sleep, work and exploration cap at eight hours. Valid longer sessions scale rewards up to their cap; cancellation awards nothing.

Pet Run Engine

/petrun starts or resumes a five-step roguelite run. Every step offers route choices with different costs, risk and possible rewards. Run rewards remain unbanked until extraction or boss completion.

/petextract banks current unbanked rewards. Successful steps and extraction contribute to Adventure progression. Failed runs lose unbanked loot but may retain a small Pet XP lesson.

Care gear and bag items can influence run survival, route choices and rewards. A lucky_charm can be consumed when it boosts a run. /petadventure remains an alias for the Pet Run Engine.

Pet Arena

/petarena opens the level 10+ turn-based Moonpet combat system. Pet Arena is separate from Kaiju Sticker Battle.

Battle power is calculated server-side using pet level, Pet XP, health, energy, morale, cleanliness, care modifiers and Battle Loadout equipment. Private chats support app-controlled battles. Telegram groups support matchmaking, ready-up, locked turns, battle IDs and parallel pairs.

Round-scoped controls reject stale moves, duplicate taps and replayed callbacks. Winners, losers and draws receive different capped game rewards, with server-side idempotency preventing double awards.

Kaiju Sticker Battle

/petkaiju or /kaiju opens the separate Kaiju card-battle system. Private chats can play the app mode; Telegram groups can host, join or queue.

The game selects one category from Power, Size, Attack, Defence, Speed or Legacy. The selected cards are compared using that category, and the higher stat wins. Group match state is stored in D1 so matches can survive Worker restarts and avoid duplicate sessions.

Economy, Inventory And Materials

Moon Gold, Moon Crystals and Style Tokens are non-transferable game currencies. They support care upgrades, battle equipment, trades and other game-only rewards.

The pet bag is separate from equipped loadouts. Consumables include moon_snack, energy_drink, clean_wipe, lucky_charm, style_patch and adventure_map.

Persistent crafting-material balances and server-owned rare-drop tables exist for eligible jobs, runs and competitive outcomes. Material stacks are capped and credited from the saved D1 balance. A complete player-facing crafting command and recipe loop remain future gameplay layers.

Missions, Seasons And Leaderboards

Daily missions reset by UTC day. Weekly windows and automatic 90-day pet seasons roll over independently without a manual reset.

/petleaderboard and the website leaderboard rank Pet XP across daily, weekly, seasonal and all-time views. Specialist track levels, traits, job rank and equipment records remain personal development systems rather than the current public leaderboard score.

Security And Duplicate Protection

The Moonboys API owns cooldowns, caps, equipment state, currencies and accepted progression. Progression-bearing API actions require stable event keys. Telegram commands derive stable keys from messages, callbacks, sessions and run IDs.

Runtime event claims, specialist XP, traits and eligible material changes are applied transactionally. Repeated Telegram delivery, button retries and API retries can therefore repair interrupted work without multiplying progression.
