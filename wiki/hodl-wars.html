HODL WARS is the central conflict of the Crypto Moonboys Year 3008 universe and the title used for the wider HODL WARS game/lore project. In canon, it is the period when faction rivalry, Block Topia control, Street Kingdom resistance and NULL's attack on memory become one connected war.

Older wiki copy described HODL WARS as a “secret beta game dropping soon.” That product-status wording is now retired from the lore page. Game formats, card-game rules and any digital implementation should be documented from their current dedicated project materials rather than inherited from old promotional drafts.

What the War Is Actually About

Territory matters, but the deeper conflict is over who gets to define identity, ownership and remembered truth. Block Topia believes civilisation requires stable verification. Street cultures believe official verification can become authorised erasure. Economic factions fight over settlement and scarcity. Cultural factions fight over what survives. NULL threatens the possibility that any of those records can still mean anything.

The central conflict is therefore free creator identity versus erasure, control, corruption and fake ownership.

The Main Battlefields

  • Block Topia: the controlled city-state where order, verified identity and authority are strongest.
  • Street Kingdoms: unstable outside territories where local crews, trade, repair and cultural memory survive without one central system.
  • The GK Grid: the tactical/cultural network linking walls, routes, signals and factions.
  • The Sacred Chain: the contested memory/provenance layer every side needs but no side should own completely.
  • The Wall: public memory, identity and proof carried outside official ledgers.

The Forty Paths

HODL WARS takes place across a civilisation understood through forty GK faction paths. The current archive does not yet lock all forty names, and the wiki should not invent the missing places to make a neat roster.

Factions remain genuinely different. Some value order, some mobility, some repair, some profit, some secrecy, some memory, some force. The war works because temporary alliances do not erase those differences.

Bitcoin KiD

Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze becomes one of the central rebel signals of the war. He leads the Bitcoin Kid Army and can move across faction boundaries because his mythology represents a decentralised dream older than any one 3008 faction.

He is not the king of the Forty. The strongest interpretation of his role is the opposite: he reminds the Forty why no one should become their permanent owner.

Queen Sarah P-fly

Queen Sarah P-fly represents the status/order side of the conflict. Her Block Topia has a serious survival argument: after the Triple Fork, functioning records, food systems, identity and law are not luxuries.

Her danger is permanent emergency logic—treating every uncontrolled difference as the beginning of another civilisational collapse.

NULL THE PROPHET

NULL changes HODL WARS from politics into existential defence. NULL does not need to conquer every faction if names, records and relationships can be reduced to meaningless static.

This is the threshold where ordinary enemies may have to defend the same underlying reality together.

HODL Warriors

HODL Warriors are the collective defence response above normal faction rivalry. They rise when the Sacred Chain, cultural memory or shared reality faces a threat no faction can survive alone.

The formation must be temporary. A permanent emergency army claiming authority over all forty paths would reproduce the centralisation the doctrine exists to resist.

What HODL Means Here

HODL expands far beyond holding an asset. It means hold memory, hold culture, hold proof, hold your word, hold the wall, hold enough of the chain together for another block of history to remain possible.

It also contains its own limit: know what should be released. Blind endurance can preserve a bad system as easily as a good one.

HODL WARS as a Game / Physical Project

HODL WARS is also used as the title for the project's game and physical card-game development. The lore provides characters, factions, ranks and conflict; individual game rules are a separate design layer and can change without rewriting history.

Old W81 crowdfunding, P2E, token, NFT-buff and beta-launch proposals remain project archive material. They are not automatically current product promises.

The Core Question

HODL WARS asks what a civilisation should keep when keeping everything is impossible. Every faction answers differently. The war begins when those answers collide and becomes existential when NULL threatens to make the question meaningless.

Hold what must survive us. No single keeper owns the final word.
