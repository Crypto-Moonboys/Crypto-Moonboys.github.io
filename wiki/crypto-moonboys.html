Path 1

BUILD A MOONBOY IDENTITY

This route is for someone who wants to enter the Crypto Moonboys universe through an original character identity. A creator can build around one unique 1/1 Moonboy or develop several characters across the wider Moonboy factions.

How the route works

  1. Join the website, connect identity and choose a faction.
  2. Play the arcade, complete activity and grind server-backed XP.
  3. Receive eligible free NFT drops through the reward route.
  4. Keep the NFTs, flip them, or burn eligible drops.
  5. Use burn routes to evolve into new or rarer NFTs and ultimately forge a unique 1/1 Moonboy.
  6. Build a character, alter ego, brand, story, world, products and community around the 1/1.

What the 1/1 means

The 1/1 Moonboy is not merely an image. It is a creator identity anchor. Under the published creator terms, the holder owns the licence and royalties assigned to that unique character and can build a wider creative world around it.

What you join

All 1/1 Moonboy holders are HODL Warriors. They are not passive holders. They are the creator army inside the umbrella: builders who use SWARMSY, support one another, share momentum, amplify each other's work and expand the larger Crypto Moonboys network.

This path gives you more than a solo identity. It gives you a character you own, a faction position, an army of other builders and a connected universe to build into.

Artists, businesses and product creators who need support with identity, visibility, strategy, campaigns, content, community and long-term momentum — without replacing the identity they already own.

Mission

BUILD AN IDENTITY PEOPLE REMEMBER

Crypto Moonboys exists to turn the street-earned lessons of Graffiti Kings into creator infrastructure for the next generation. The aim is not to make everyone play a game or buy an NFT. The aim is to help people create a recognisable identity, get seen, build community and keep enough momentum to turn an idea into a movement.

Path 1 creates new Moonboy identities and brings their holders into the HODL Warriors. Path 2 strengthens identities that already exist through SWARMSY. Both paths share the same culture, systems, community and belief: identity is built by showing up, telling a clear story, helping others and continuing after the hype disappears.

The wall created identity. Crypto Moonboys carries it forward.
