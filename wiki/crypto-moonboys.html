Graffiti Kings / Public creator umbrella / Two ways to build

CRYPTO MOONBOYSBuild an identity. Bring your own. Make something people remember.

Crypto Moonboys is the Graffiti Kings public creator umbrella: a cultural world for building identity, visibility, community, ownership and lasting creative presence. Moonboy characters, factions, games, optional blockchain artefacts, the wiki and creator tools have different jobs within that wider purpose.

There are two valid routes. A creator can build one or more Moonboy identities, or keep an existing artist, business, product, brand or alter-ego identity and use SWARMSY to develop it. A Moonboy, NFT or Arcade score is not a prerequisite for the second route.

From public marks to a creator umbrella

The project's cultural foundation is Graffiti Kings and its long association with street art, public identity and creative work. The current project describes that foundation as more than four decades of culture. The relevant history includes names built through repeated work, crews that carry reputations together and images that gain meaning through the places and people around them.

Those practices help explain the later digital world without turning every resemblance into a historical first. Graffiti writers used pseudonyms and public signs long before blockchain identity; a digital token introduces another kind of provenance rather than inventing reputation. A wall, a photograph, a collection entry and a signed transaction each preserve different information.

The W81 archive shows the project being described through several stages: NFT ecosystem, digital urban nation, multi-chain universe, game proposals and creator-intelligence manifestos. Those descriptions are evidence of development. The current README and Master Source of Truth place the creator umbrella above its individual products, rather than treating one older pitch as the definition forever.

What the umbrella is for

Crypto Moonboys addresses a practical creator problem: having something to make or say does not automatically produce a recognisable public identity. A character, visual language or clear business story can help an audience recognise continuity between separate pieces of work. Continued publishing, community activity and preserved proof give that identity substance over time.

The promise is a route for building, not a guarantee of attention, income or cultural importance. An attractive avatar does not complete a business; an effective tool does not decide a creator's judgement for them. The creator still chooses what the work means, whom it serves and which opportunities to accept.

The public world brings those efforts into contact through shared characters, cultural references and participation. Its value is not exhausted by collecting or playing. A creator can contribute through a story, a useful guide, a design, a maintained archive or work developed under their own established name.

Two routes into the same wider world

The Moonboy route suits someone who wants a new character identity to develop. The existing-identity route suits someone who already has an artist name, business, product or personal creative direction they intend to keep. They are choices about identity, not a hierarchy in which one group is merely waiting to become the other.

The routes can overlap. A designer may retain a professional practice while separately building a licensed Moonboy character. A small business may use SWARMSY for planning without collecting anything. The overlap does not remove the need to distinguish which name, rights and audience belong to which project.

Current progression and availability still matter. The Moonboy route connects to supported participation and eligible ownership routes; the existing-identity route connects to creator planning and development. Neither route makes every historical reward proposal, external action tool or hosting feature automatically available.

What a Moonboy identity can become

A Moonboy is a character identity: a recognisable combination of name, appearance, attitude and story that can recur across work. The character can anchor illustrations, videos, stories, merchandise concepts, performances or a creator world within the rights actually assigned to it. Recognition comes from coherent use, not merely from rarity or a numbered edition.

The current project calls Moonboys the new generation of Graffiti Kings identities in the Web3 and blockchain world. That is cultural succession rather than a claim that every character is a documented descendant of a real artist. Fictional bloodlines and future biographies belong to the story layer.

A character also needs boundaries. Its identity is not the whole Crypto Moonboys brand, another holder's character or an unrestricted claim over every image carrying a similar style. The work a creator builds around an assigned identity remains subject to its published licence and to the ordinary rights of others.

Keeping the identity a creator already has

The second route begins with the user's existing work. An artist may need clearer presentation of a portfolio; a product maker may need a launch story; a small organisation may need consistent language across public material. The identity is an input to the work, not something the system must replace with a Moonboy.

SWARMSY's role is to help organise direction, planning, content and continued activity around that identity. A useful outcome might be a coherent brief, a reviewed campaign plan or a better record of decisions. These are examples of creator work rather than claims that every connected publishing action is currently automated.

This route protects the breadth of the umbrella. People can participate in its methods and culture without adopting a fictional biography, pursuing a token or entering a competitive game. The shared connection is the effort to make creative work recognisable and sustained.

GKniftyHEADS as the primary foundation

GKniftyHEADS is the primary Moonboy identity architecture alongside other factions and collaborations connected to the Graffiti Kings cultural foundation. Its characters and collection records help connect artwork, identifiable editions, community ownership and the wider mythology.

The name also appears inside the fiction as the Immortal Architects and keepers of fragmented memory. That portrayal must be read in its era and source. It does not turn the present collection into an actual immortal institution or make every real holder responsible for a fictional character's actions.

The GKniftyHEADS reference and collection index serve more specific purposes than this overview. A collection record can identify an artefact; a lore article can explain a tradition; neither on its own expands the legal rights attached to a different asset.

The forty-faction civilisation and public crews

The forty factions give the fictional world different occupations, aesthetics, loyalties and interpretations of its inherited history. Brokers, repairers, archivists, artists, soldiers and opportunists need one another even when their ideologies conflict. The number forty is a locked civilisation model, not permission to manufacture a complete roster from every group name appearing in an old proposal.

In the public project, faction identity helps organise characters and community activity. The live selectable set follows the current supported directory and rules. A mythic faction list, an archived game roster and a present onboarding menu are different records; one should not be used to silently overwrite another.

The distinction makes room for personality. A faction member can disagree with a leader or pursue ordinary work without ceasing to belong. The story is stronger when factions contain households, routines, costs and internal arguments rather than forty interchangeable sets of powers.

GraffPUNKS and culture in circulation

GraffPUNKS carries graffiti, music, broadcasting, writing and resistance imagery through the shared world. W81's Unwritten Wall and Legacy Over Hype material repeatedly connects public marks with sound, audiences and cultural memory. These texts explain the project's vocabulary; their metaphors of guaranteed permanence should not be mistaken for technical guarantees.

In the 3008 fiction, GraffPUNKS use walls and the Paint Path to preserve records beyond official control. In the public project, artwork, writing, radio and community publishing connect people across different media. The two layers influence one another without becoming identical events.

Readers can follow the GraffPUNKS article and radio reference for their separate histories. A fictional broadcast capable of changing a city's memory is a story device; a real broadcast's existence and availability need their own current evidence.

Tokens, assigned rights and creator royalties

NFTs can record a particular token, edition and transfer history. The project has used WAX collections as a significant home for artwork and character artefacts. That record does not by itself define every copyright, commercial permission, game effect or canon status associated with the image.

The current qualifying unique 1/1 creator rule is specific: the holder receives the assigned character identity and creator licence within the published scope, keeps 100% of royalties from approved commercial use of that character, and owes no royalty percentage to Crypto Moonboys or Graffiti Kings under that rule. The exact qualifying terms govern the assignment; generic earlier drops do not automatically receive the same rights.

Creator ownership and official canon remain separate. A story can belong to its creator without becoming official shared history. A rejected canon submission does not remove the creator's underlying ownership. Conversely, publication in a wiki does not grant rights over the parent brands or other people's work.

Participation, rewards and irreversible choices

The Moonboy progression route connects Telegram identity, supported faction activity and Arcade play with server-backed XP and proof. Where active and eligible, later stages can include reward drops, supported keep, transfer or burn choices, evolution and a qualifying 1/1 route. The conditional wording matters because those stages do not all share one permanent release state.

A score measures performance in a run; Arcade XP records accepted persistent activity. Current competitive participation requires the supported authenticated identity. A reader should not infer an anonymous competitive leaderboard or retained unlinked progression from an old screenshot or campaign story.

A burn permanently removes the specified inputs under its active recipe. It should be understood through the current requirements and output, not a promise of profit or a lore description of sacrifice. No fixed XP threshold is repeated here: the current progression service and configuration determine eligibility, and a historical article must not freeze a changing gate into universal policy.

HODL Warriors in the project and the fiction

In present brand usage, every qualifying 1/1 holder is a HODL Warrior: part of the collective creator-builder role. The name describes active contribution through making, collaborating, promoting and sustaining a creator world. It is not a normal selectable live faction, a passive collector tier or a promise of financial reward.

In the First Witness reconstruction of the 3008 story, HODL Warriors primarily name emergency collective defence when memory and shared reality are threatened. Rival cultures cooperate to preserve the conditions under which they can continue to exist. Their authority is temporary; the defence must not become permanent ownership of the factions it protects.

HODL X Warriors remain a distinct archive tradition. The Games, champion and Seeding accounts involving them do not justify making all HODL Warriors graduates of one trial system. The dedicated HODL Warriors chapter preserves the contexts and the unresolved relationship.

SWARMSY, SPARKY and maintained direction

SWARMSY is the private creator operating environment connected to both identity routes. The current project describes an AnythingLLM-based foundation for working with knowledge, project context and AI assistance. SPARKY is the visible guide and operator that helps keep the user's direction coherent across work.

Memory Locks preserve approved decisions. Proof Review separates ideas, proposals, completed work and claims supported by evidence. Their purpose is practical: a creator should not have to rediscover the project's identity every time a tool, collaborator or model changes. Saved decisions can be revised deliberately rather than drifting through repeated drafts.

Availability still depends on the actual product and connected capabilities. This page does not certify a public hosted release, universal offline operation, every model provider or unrestricted external actions. It explains why the system belongs within the umbrella: the public world carries identity and community, while private project work needs its own place to develop before publication.

Arcade and the Block Topia beta

Games provide a participation layer within Crypto Moonboys. The current repository documents the public Arcade, identity-linked progression and a Block Topia multiplayer survival and mission beta. Its documented gameplay includes phases, combat, respawning, extraction, upgrades and ready/start/restart behaviour behind the supported entry gate.

Those concrete systems should be distinguished from the much larger proposed campaigns in W81. The archive imagines DreamWars, Forklords, branching boss stories, huge raids and artefact rewards. They remain useful story and design sources without becoming evidence that every encounter or reward is deployed.

HODL WARS is first a named fictional conflict in this reference. Older promotional descriptions of a separate secret game or an imminent launch are not a current release verification. Likewise, the fictional Hard Fork Games institution is not automatically a public tournament product. Readers can use the current Arcade routes for supported participation and the lore pages for their different narrative roles.

The Year 3008 world

The future story centres on societies trying to keep identity, evidence and ordinary life intact after the failure of a civilisation-scale system. Block Topia offers security and infrastructure while tightly governing recognition and movement. The Street Kingdoms contain changing outside communities, trade, conflict and alternative practices of memory.

Alfie Blaze leads the escaped or outside Bitcoin Kids. The Bitcoin X Kids are the engineered generation inside the city. Queen Sarah P-fly and the Squeaky Pinks belong to the city's ruling and enforcement traditions, while GraffPUNKS preserve competing testimony. NULL threatens the connection between records and meaning that all of them ultimately need.

Some archive accounts describe universal uploads, extinct bodies or a total simulation. Others preserve physical infrastructure and different forms of consciousness. The current reconstruction does not make one total explanation compulsory. The disputed Genesis Kernel, dream layers and Antichain origins leave room for uncertainty without removing the concrete costs of food, repairs, work, grief and war.

The shared chronology

The First Witness provides the stable historical framework. In 2030 the Great Concord produces the Covenant, with Bitcoin as its provenance witness. A later, undated Sacred Fork carries the authenticated tradition into Sacred/Aether memory. Those events do not already contain the named characters and faction titles that appear centuries later.

The World Chain becomes a civilisation-scale dependency for money, identity, governance and public record. In 2880 its Triple Fork produces conflicting realities and a spreading collapse remembered as Chainfire within the wider Great Unravelling. By 2930 fewer than a dozen secure regions remain; the Queens stronghold becomes the basis of Block Topia.

The principal saga is 3008, encompassing the Games, outside resistance, inside life and HODL WARS escalation. The Final Fork remains unresolved. Older dates and completed-victory transmissions survive as attributed traditions rather than silently replacing these anchors.

Different chains do different work

The Sacred Chain or Aether-Chain is the later mythic memory and proof tradition carrying scripture, identity and cultural records. The World Chain is the later global dependency that fails in 2880. The True Bitcoin Fork is Block Topia's protected governing and identity anchor. Similar language in old posts does not make the three interchangeable.

The GK Grid is the 3008 tactical and cultural network of routes, walls, signals and faction contact. It provides a way to describe how people coordinate, not a shortcut that establishes every piece of fictional technology as one currently operating product.

This separation keeps the conflicts meaningful. A city can defend its administrative records while suppressing a mural; a wall can preserve an omitted witness while carrying an inaccurate date. The story asks how different forms of evidence can challenge one another, rather than granting automatic truth to whichever surface appears most permanent.

A world maintained by ordinary work

The expanded history makes the universe larger through specific lives rather than only larger battles. Block Topia's Air Ledger and Glass Kitchens concern the work of keeping people alive inside a controlled city. The outside-world accounts describe lodging, markets, routes and negotiations that continue while their political futures remain uncertain.

The Army's Load Table, HODL's Release Table and the Games' Returned Lists each address different consequences of organised action. They are established local practices in their dedicated chapters, not one universal authority with several interchangeable names. A returned candidate, a repair worker and a commander do not experience the same event in the same way.

These stories were authored as fictional expansion and recorded as such in the canon history. They are not recovered real-world biographies. Their practical detail helps preserve a central feature of the setting: people need places to sleep, material to repair and records they can dispute even while famous figures argue over the future of reality.

The public wiki and its evidence

The wiki joins real history, public project records, practical guidance, fictional worlds, collection references and documented systems. Those materials can support one another only when their status remains legible. A campaign pitch, an implemented feature and a character's claim about themselves answer different questions.

The First Witness corpus supplies a fixed reference for the current lore reconstruction, while the archive preserves contradictions and older ambitions. A reconciliation can prefer one source without pretending the discarded version never existed. New authorship can extend a world without being mislabelled as an ancient discovery.

The public wiki is also different from private SWARMSY memory. A creator can explore an unfinished idea privately before deciding what to publish. Public indexing, community review and canon acceptance are separate steps; none should be used as a hidden condition on rights already granted under a qualifying licence.

How the layers connect

Graffiti Kings supplies the cultural foundation. Crypto Moonboys is the public umbrella. Moonboy identities and GKniftyHEADS provide a major character architecture. GraffPUNKS carries cultural and narrative expression. Factions organise shared styles and activity; HODL Warriors name the qualifying creator-builder role in the present and emergency defence in the fiction.

SWARMSY supports private creator work through SPARKY and maintained context. The website, wiki, graph and public activity surfaces help people encounter work and understand its connections. Arcade and Block Topia offer supported participation. Collections and qualifying terms supply particular artefact and ownership relationships.

The map is connected without being compulsory. Someone using one route does not automatically need every layer, and a problem in one product does not redefine the entire cultural project. The useful starting point is the identity and work a person intends to develop, followed by the relevant supported tools and terms.
