Graffiti Kings legacy / creator umbrella / two paths

CRYPTO MOONBOYSONE UMBRELLA. TWO WAYS TO JOIN.

Crypto Moonboys carries more than 40 years of Graffiti Kings culture into the Web3 and blockchain world. The Moonboys are the new generation of Graffiti Kings identities: characters, factions and creator worlds led through GKniftyHEADS and other Moonboy factions connected to OG Graffiti Kings artists.

Crypto Moonboys is not a game. It is the public creator umbrella, community and shared record. Games, XP, eligible NFT rewards, burn routes, the wiki, factions, Battle Chamber and Block Topia are systems inside that umbrella, not the mission itself.

The umbrella

WHAT CRYPTO MOONBOYS ACTUALLY IS

Culture first

The foundation is Graffiti Kings: identity, public signal, reputation, crews, collaboration, storytelling and the discipline of staying visible.

Creator onboarding

The project gives people a route into a larger creator world, whether they need a new character identity or already have something real to build around.

Shared momentum

SWARMSY, the wiki, community, factions and qualifying 1/1 HODL Warriors connect creators into a movement that helps members keep building.

The internet gave everyone a voice, but not everyone has an identity. Crypto Moonboys exists to help people become recognisable, build a story around what they do, get seen and keep moving after the first burst of attention.

Path 1

BUILD A MOONBOY IDENTITY

This route is for someone who wants to enter the Crypto Moonboys universe through an original character identity. A creator may build around one Moonboy or connect several characters across the wider Moonboy factions.

How the route works

  1. See what a Moonboy can become: a character identity, alter ego, mascot, product face, content signal, story world or community anchor.
  2. Connect identity, link Telegram where required and choose a current live faction.
  3. Complete current missions, play accepted Arcade runs and build server-backed XP and public proof.
  4. Use eligible NFT reward routes only where the current runtime labels them live, limited or active for eligible users.
  5. Keep, flip, burn or evolve assets only where the relevant route is supported and currently active.
  6. Build toward a qualifying unique 1/1 Moonboy only where the published route is active or correctly labelled as planned, limited, beta or coming soon.

What the qualifying 1/1 means

A qualifying unique 1/1 Moonboy is not merely an image. Under the published creator terms, it anchors an assigned character identity and creator licence. The holder keeps 100% of approved commercial royalties from that character; Crypto Moonboys and Graffiti Kings take no royalty share.

HODL Warrior role

Every qualifying unique 1/1 holder becomes a HODL Warrior: a creator-builder who uses SWARMSY, supports other builders, shares work, amplifies launches and helps connect creator worlds. HODL Warriors is a role for qualifying 1/1 holders, not a selectable live faction.

This path is not an NFT reward farm. The Arcade, XP, drops and burns are progression tools that support the identity route; they do not define the umbrella.

Path 2

KEEP YOUR IDENTITY. USE SWARMSY.

This route is for people who already have something real to build around. You may already be an artist, graffiti or street artist, musician, writer, designer, filmmaker, photographer, tattoo artist, business owner, product creator, brand, organisation or public personality.

No Moonboy required

You do not need to adopt a Moonboy character, grind for a 1/1, enter a faction, play the Arcade or understand blockchain. Keep your existing name, face, business, product, art or alter ego. SWARMSY is the private creator operating environment that helps it stand out.

What SWARMSY helps with

  • Identity and positioning: define what you represent and why people should remember it.
  • Storytelling and creative direction: shape voice, visual language and public narrative.
  • Planning and publishing: turn ideas into a repeatable content and campaign system.
  • Approved SPARKY Truths and Proof Review: keep rough ideas separate from confirmed decisions and separate claims from evidence.
  • Momentum: keep current, remain visible and continue building after launch day.

You already have the subject. SWARMSY helps you make it recognisable, organise the work, get it seen and keep the momentum alive.

Current SWARMSY-Ai implementation

WHAT IS IMPLEMENTED NOW

SWARMSY is a customised AnythingLLM fork. AnythingLLM supplies the workspace, document, retrieval, model-provider, agent and API foundation. SPARKY is the permanent visible operator for the guided creator journey.

Fixed protected SPARKY workspace

The canonical SPARKY workspace is created automatically when missing, shown as a fixed “Continue with SPARKY” entry and protected from normal rename, update and deletion routes.

Starter journey

New users receive three starter routes: Help me shape my project idea, Build my project identity, and Turn this idea into an action plan.

Approved SPARKY Truths

Confirmed facts and decisions are stored separately from rough ideas, scoped by workspace and user, injected into later SPARKY prompts and available for archiving.

Normal thread controls

The fixed SPARKY sidebar entry keeps normal AnythingLLM thread history and New Thread controls instead of creating a separate chat system.

Eight core packs

OG SPARKY Contract, Project Manager Protocol, Identity Questionnaire, Do It For Me Prompts, Approved Decisions, Action Confirmation, Tasks and Schedule, and Proof Review.

The packs are present under server/sparky/packs/core. The canonical SPARKY system prompt is loaded directly, but the wider pack catalogue is not yet fully auto-ingested as workspace documents. Hosted releases, finished installers and external actions must not be described as live unless the current runtime proves them.

Creative modes and hooks

SPARKY can use SAFE and WTF as creative intensity settings. It may also frame ideas through MESSAGE, DOODAD and PLACEMENT: what people should remember, the recognisable symbol or character, and the context that makes the idea hit harder.

Ownership / licence / canon

OWNERSHIP AND CANON ARE SEPARATE

Your character. Your licence. Your royalties. applies to qualifying unique 1/1 Moonboys under the published creator terms. It does not automatically apply to every NFT, generic progression asset, earlier drop, faction item or game reward.

Ownership of a qualifying 1/1 character is not dependent on wiki inclusion, SAM indexing or official-canon approval. A creator may own the character and still have a lore submission rejected from the shared official universe.

Creator-owned lore

Lore the holder creates for their own character or world.

Community-approved lore

Lore accepted through the published community process.

Official canon

Approved lore indexed into the shared Crypto Moonboys record.

Mission

BUILD AN IDENTITY PEOPLE REMEMBER

Crypto Moonboys exists to turn the street-earned lessons of Graffiti Kings into creator infrastructure for the next generation. The aim is not to make everyone play a game or buy an NFT. The aim is to help people create a recognisable identity, get seen, build community and keep enough momentum to turn an idea into a movement.

Path 1 creates Moonboy identities and may lead to qualifying unique 1/1 ownership where the route is active. Path 2 strengthens identities that already exist through SWARMSY. Both paths share the same culture, systems, community and belief: identity is built by showing up, telling a clear story, helping others and continuing after the hype disappears.

The wall created identity. Crypto Moonboys carries it forward.

Lore and worldbuilding

THE CREATOR WORLD BEHIND THE MISSION

The 3008 lore, Block Topia, GraffPUNKS, Crypto Moongirls, Bitcoin Kids, factions and the wider GK universe give creators a shared world to explore and build into. The lore is a cultural and creative layer, not the definition of the project.

Moonboy characters can become websites, stories, artwork, videos, merchandise, games, communities and connected creator worlds. Approved creator links can be organised through the living wiki so individual worlds do not disappear as isolated posts. SAM and the website agents support that memory system.

GK provides the floor. Moonboy holders, artists, businesses and creators build on top.
