🪙 NBGX Token

🪙 Cryptocurrencies 📅 Last updated: March 2026 📂 Cryptocurrencies

NBGX is the fractional companion-token concept associated with NoBallGames’ $NBG. W81 describes its purpose as deeper liquidity and broader player accessibility. The archive’s short entry establishes that design aim, not a verified current market, conversion contract or game reward.

Project identity

NBGX belongs with NoBallGames’ token proposals. It is not an unnamed Block Topia faction currency, nor a second name for NBG. The established NoBallGames reference treats NBGX as a separate proposed branch whose rules would need their own published record.

Fractionalisation and accessibility

W81’s numbered NBGX entry calls it a “fractionalised version” of NBG. In that context, the intended benefit is easier access and more flexible liquidity. The phrase does not supply an exchange ratio, redemption guarantee, decimal precision, maximum supply or issuer account.

A larger nominal unit count would not alone make an asset cheaper in economic terms or guarantee a liquid market. Accessibility depends on the actual conversion design, market depth and supported uses. Those implementation details remain unestablished in this source.

Correction of the earlier faction story

The older page repeatedly imagined NBGX unlocking territories during rebellions, rewarding a holder’s faction loyalty and revealing experimental nodes. None of those mechanics follows from the fractional-token entry. They are removed as assertions about the asset rather than being promoted into Year 3008 history.

No ownership of NBGX grants a standing in the HODL WARS, an automatic claim on NBG or a right to another project’s artwork. Any such connection would require a specific published programme.

Status of the proposal

The consolidated NoBallGames reference places NBGX among proposed specialist branches. This companion page retains the original token URL and explains that narrow concept; it does not certify a live issuance or integration.

Source record

W81 W2.txt, entry 147, supplies the fractionalisation and accessibility description. The current NoBallGames reference supplies the distinction between the main token and proposed branches. The former broad GraffPUNKS lore link did not establish an issuer or conversion mechanism.
