🐱 Tabby

Tabby is an XRP meme-token entry in the Crypto Moonboys wiki. Its cat-style identity gives it a simple community hook: easy to remember, easy to meme, and naturally suited to social posts, holder jokes and collectible-style branding.

XRPL context

Tabby is not XRP itself. It should be understood as a separate XRPL-related token reference. The correct research path is to verify the issuer account, token code, trust-line settings and liquidity before relying on any market listing.

Community identity

Animal memes are one of crypto’s most durable formats. Dog coins, frog coins and cat coins all work because the branding is instantly readable. Tabby fits that pattern inside the XRP meme sector, but community branding does not replace ledger-level checks.

DEX and trust-line checks

Before interacting with Tabby, users should confirm the official issuer, trust-line details, market route, available liquidity, slippage and source links. If more than one token uses the same or similar name, the issuer account is the deciding detail.

Risk note

This page is informational and not financial advice. XRP meme tokens can be volatile, thinly traded and community-dependent.
