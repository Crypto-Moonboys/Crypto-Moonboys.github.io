How the Forty Practise the Same Covenant Differently

First WitnessFaction practice · Section 52Last updated: October 2026

The easiest way to understand the Forty is to watch them perform the same ritual differently. Shared scripture produces recognisable structure; culture changes everything else.

Passing the Key

Chain Scribes transfer encrypted archives with a public checksum and an attached list of unresolved disputes.

Ducky Boys pass an actual tool or access key and require the outgoing keeper to explain what is most likely to break next.

GraffPUNKS may pass a location, symbol sequence or wall route rather than an object. The successor proves receipt by altering the route without destroying its meaning.

Finance Guild closes books, reconciles obligations and requires both parties to sign the unsettled liabilities before authority changes hands.

The Empty Chair

Nice & Easy Bois use it in negotiations to name the side not present.

Crypto Moongirls use a formal data representation of affected populations rather than a literal chair.

Street factions sometimes leave a tagged crate, helmet or jacket instead of furniture.

The Day of Repair

Salvagers turn it into a city-scale recovery operation.

Code Alchemists audit neglected software and old contracts.

OG Pixel Saints restore obsolete media, displays and early digital works.

High Hats often fund repair publicly, which rivals accuse them of converting maintenance into reputation laundering.

Concord Day

Blockstars make the meal a major performance event.

Nomad Bears prefer small roadside gatherings with whoever happens to be present.

Information Mercenaries have been accused of using the event to gather intelligence from unusually mixed crowds.

Funerals

GraffPUNKS favour Final Marks. Chain Scribes favour the Three Records. AllCity Bulls may repeat the dead person's name across multiple territories. Gasless Ghosts sometimes remove rather than add a mark, leaving an intentional absence.

What the differences prove

No central liturgy controls these practices. The common structure survives precisely because local culture is allowed to carry it differently.

The argument is not whether variation exists. The argument is when variation stops being interpretation and becomes contradiction of the Covenant itself.
