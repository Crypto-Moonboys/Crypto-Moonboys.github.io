Camo Bear

Waterfront Roamers · Trail hand and seed swap organiser · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

Camo Bear wears green and blue because clothes that already look busy hide a surprising amount of soil. He earns his keep tending the edges of paths on the nature side of the Waterfront. Some days he counts how many people carry something through a gate before he decides which growth to cut back.

The turn by the growing bed

A footpath near his first growing plot kept widening. He blamed impatient walkers until a carrier showed him how little room the old turn gave a person with a laden cart. They moved one bed and planted it up again. He still feels a twinge when he passes the replacement plot and sees how well it gets the sun.

Off duty

He trades seedlings in old paper cups and puts the name of the grower on each one. He loses at dominoes regularly and claims the game punishes a person for thinking about the ground when he should be looking at the table.
