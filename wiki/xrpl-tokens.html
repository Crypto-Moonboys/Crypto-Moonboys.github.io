🪙 XRPL Tokens

XRPL tokens are assets issued on the XRP Ledger that are separate from XRP itself. XRPL supports issued tokens through trust-line mechanics, as well as newer token and NFT features. This page explains the basic research model needed before covering individual XRPL projects such as PMSL, EXYBOY, TAGZcoin and XRP Army / ARMY.

Issued tokens and trust lines

On XRPL, XRP is native. Other fungible assets are issued by accounts and usually require a trust line before a user can hold them. That makes issuer verification central. A ticker name is not enough. The issuer account, supply controls, freeze settings, liquidity, project links and market data must all be checked.

DEX and liquidity

XRPL includes decentralized exchange functionality for issued assets. That helps project tokens and meme coins form markets, but it also means users can encounter thin liquidity, copied names and fast-moving speculative tokens.

How this wiki handles XRPL projects

For strong source-backed projects, pages can include token mechanics, issuer details, marketplace links and community context. For thin-source tokens, pages should stay conservative and avoid unsupported claims. This is especially important for meme coins because speculation can outrun documentation.
