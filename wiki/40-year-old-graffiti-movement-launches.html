What four decades can carry into another medium

“Legacy over hype” asks an audience to value a history of making before the promise of a future market. The street-art connection matters because a name acquires substance through repeated work, public encounters and the people willing to recognise it. Moving that name into Web3 changes its distribution without automatically carrying every earlier relationship with it.

The source joins a cultural account to ambitious promotional claims. Read those claims in their original period, then follow the connected artist and project records for the work itself. The useful continuity is a practice people can inspect: who made an image, what it draws on and how another creator can participate while retaining a clear account of their own contribution.
