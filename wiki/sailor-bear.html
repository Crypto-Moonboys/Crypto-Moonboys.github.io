Sailor Bear

Waterfront Roamers · Harbor lookout · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

Sailor Bear worked on broad harbor decks before taking a route on foot. He still wears his captain’s hat and red goggles when the wind drives spray over the rail. He knows which stairs stay open at a high tide and which dock approach backs up when two foremen expect the same truck.

The carpenter’s crossing

He once gave a crossing estimate using a set of timings kept in good weather. A carpenter missed a paid shift because she trusted him. He now keeps the range of possible arrival times in his pocket and marks the one he would give a person with a wage riding on it. The longer answer frustrates people in a hurry.

Off duty

In his spare time he carves little hulls from the wood that washes up in corners of the dock. He tests each one in a dish of rainwater and gives the best floaters away before he starts to feel possessive about them.
