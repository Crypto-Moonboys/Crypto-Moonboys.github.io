The Six Pillars

The Six Pillars are an old military and sociological reading of the GK Grid. They sort twenty-eight source-era factions by the work and power they exercise. The diagram is influential because it is useful; it is dangerous because commanders can mistake a useful diagram for the whole human world.

Six functions, competing interests

Rulers and Enforcers: Crypto Moongirls, Squeaky Pinks and Finance Guild. Their source classification prioritises order, surveillance and stable authority. It puts a governing movement, an enforcement culture and financial power beside each other without making them one command. Their alliances are agreements with costs, not a shared personality.

Resource and Utility Core: Ducky Boys, Salvagers and Nice & Easy Bois. They maintain supply, repair and passage. Calling them amoral necessities conceals the choices a repairer makes about which district receives a part and the price a broker charges a trapped traveller.

Economic Predators: High Hats, Rugpull Miners and Moonlords. Their source grouping concerns extraction, dependence and manufactured fear. The Moonlords’ narrative power remains distinct from the Whale Lords’ reserve tradition. Wealth imagery alone does not merge them.

Military and Resistance: Aztec Raiders, Tuskon OGs and Blockchain Furies. Acquisition, endurance and retaliation can bring them to the same battle while giving them different desired outcomes. An enemy shared with Alfie does not enlist every outside fighter in his Army.

Ideological Observers: GKniftyHEADS, Crypto Stoned Boys and GraffPUNKS. They preserve, interpret and contest cultural memory. Calling them observers understates the damage a public account can do and the labour needed to make a wall outlast its first painter.

Logic and Information Specialists: Chain Scribes and Gasless Ghosts. The old taxonomy emphasises access, hidden movement and control of records. Scribe custody and Ghost escape routes solve different problems. Neither makes the faction the owner of everyone it helps.

The twelve names in the arsenal

Theta-Shield and Neural Freeze: the source imagines revelation and immobilisation as Authority weapons, even assigning a range and a sixty-second lock. Those specifications describe its tactical fiction. They are not universal capabilities of every Pink, and no passage licenses claims that a live wallet can be remotely frozen by a player.

Grid-Patch and Overclock Cables: repair and redirected power become Utility weapons. The source promises instant reconstruction and enemy energy extraction. Bounded modern accounts require compatible equipment, power and somebody doing the work. A repaired image is not automatically repaired concrete.

FUD-Grenade and Liquidity Siphon: Predator imagery joins induced panic to extraction. The first promises a false-rug hallucination; the second treats value as a drainable reserve. Their practical political equivalent is a frightened debtor accepting a ruinous bargain. Neither is an executable instruction or a real asset-transfer feature.

Data-Spear and Chain-Blade: Resistance weapons combine material assault with damaged access and armour. The source gives the spear a unique ability to pierce Theta defence. A narrator’s superlative does not remove power limits, wounds or the possibility that a fighter loses.

Echo Ink and Memory-Sigils: cultural tools are narrated as broadcasts and traps that overload hostile interpretation with graffiti history. The names preserve the idea that memory can interfere with domination. They do not establish that every painted mark paralyses any machine that sees it.

Zero-Trace and Null-Cipher: specialist claims offer unrecorded movement and deletion from a local Grid instance. Their absolute wording is precisely what makes the doctrine seductive. The names do not prove perfect anonymity, universal physical death, or that the Gasless Ghosts are NULL THE PROPHET.

Twenty-eight, Forty and the emergency muster

The source’s faction ranks belong to its own twenty-eight-active framework. Six Pillars groups functions across the forty named cultures; it adds no factions. The nine live Battle Chamber options remain a separate operational roster.

Bitcoin Kids and Bitcoin X Kids remain different generations and political lives. HODL Warriors’ Year 3008 emergency convergence does not make HODL X champions redundant or create a permanent central army. No pillar grants a delegate the consent of every household sharing a faction name.

Who is missing from the diagram

The Fifth Carbon rehearsal makes each desk look necessary and each choice look measurable. It omits the neighbouring block whose valves are taken to make the winning repair. Tessa Coil asks about that cost and the officer tells her it lies outside the evaluated zone.

The episode exposes the doctrine’s weakness without making it useless. A six-function diagram can show where a coalition lacks repair or intelligence. It cannot decide whose hunger is acceptable, whose injury counts, or when an escort must let a household leave. The arsenal’s grand names survive because people want power that looks as clean as the classification. The people paying for it usually know better.
