The rhetoric of an uprising

The Blockchain Uprising addresses its audience as though a decisive moment has already arrived. That voice can gather dispersed frustrations into a common call, turning a technical change into a cultural story of agency. It also raises the expectation that participation will lead somewhere a reader can recognise.

Read the manifesto through the work that follows the declaration. A movement needs continuing publication, credited making and ways for people to contribute without surrendering their own judgment. Its source-era language of scale and imminent change belongs to that rhetorical moment; its ongoing cultural argument can be assessed through the concrete creative network around it.
