The Forty Paths

First WitnessCivilisation map · Section 16Last updated: October 2026

The Forty are not forty religions. They are forty survival cultures reading the same inherited Covenant through different work, wounds and political experience.

Why forty

The archive repeatedly uses a 40/40 structure but does not consistently preserve forty fully defined faction names. The reconciled register now names forty enduring cultures. The names come from surviving sources; the six completed membership accounts are later commentary, not additions to the 2030 Books.

The number later became institutional through gatherings, representation systems and the Hard Fork Games, but the Paths began as cultural interpretations before they became gameable identities.

Shared text, different praxis

A builder reads the Keeper as maintenance. A trader reads it as settlement. A graffiti crew reads the Wall as public memory. An authority reads the Chain as stable verification. A rebel reads the Fork as the right to leave. A healer reads Common Blood as care. A spy reads Witness as control of information.

The same passage can produce opposite institutions because the dispute is often not about the words but which danger a community fears most.

The anti-centralisation function

No faction is allowed to become the official interpreter of the whole Bible. Even when one faction dominates territory, its commentary remains commentary. This is the cultural equivalent of distributed validation.

That rule is why the coming of a unifying figure creates danger as well as hope. A person capable of crossing all Forty can become a defence signal or a new centre.

Forty does not mean moral equality

Decentralisation does not make every path good. Some factions exploit, deceive or dominate. The Covenant allows their claims to be tested against the same shared text rather than granting immunity because they are culturally distinct.

The strongest faction writing gives every group a real virtue and a real corruption of that virtue. That is what keeps the Forty human rather than turning them into colour-coded teams.

Every path is a fear made cultural

The Forty become more believable when read through fear rather than costume. One community fears chaos, so it loves order. Another fears erasure, so it loves walls. Another fears hunger, so it loves reserves. Another fears capture, so it loves escape routes. Another fears lies, so it loves archives.

That is how real traditions split around the same inheritance. People do not only interpret a text with intellect. They interpret it with the wound they most want never repeated.

Real-life reading: find the verse your group loves too much

Every family, company, scene, political tribe and creative crew has a favourite virtue. Loyalty. Freedom. Discipline. Kindness. Hustle. Authenticity. Safety. Winning. None is automatically bad. Any can become destructive when it is the answer to every problem.

Ask what your group praises most, then ask what that virtue becomes when pushed too far. Loyalty becomes silence. Freedom becomes abandonment. Discipline becomes control. Kindness becomes avoidance. Hustle becomes exploitation. Authenticity becomes an excuse not to grow.

The Forty are built from that tension, not from forty random aesthetics. See Life Readings.

What belongs in the Forty-path register

A path is an enduring culture with a name, a practice of belonging and a way of arranging responsibility. It need not hold a fixed district or command an army. A guild, mobile crew or sanctuary lineage can meet that description. A temporary service does not become a path because it acquires a banner. The Open Crate escort, Lantern Courts, Borough Thread and Holdfast Depot therefore remain local arrangements. They may gather several paths and unaffiliated households without increasing the civilisational count.

The register distinguishes cultures, generations, offices and emergency roles. Bitcoin Kids names the escaped and outside lineage; Bitcoin Kid Army names an organised force within that wider life. Their overlap does not make every escaped child enlisted. Bitcoin X Kids remains the engineered generation inside the city's order. HODL Warriors names collective existential defence. HODL X Warriors preserves a separate champion and service tradition whose complete historical relationship to that defence is unresolved. Neither HODL title is inserted as a convenient missing ordinary faction.

GKniftyHEADS and the Immortal Architects are not counted twice merely because the older texts use two descriptions. OG Pixel Saints and Pixel Saints share the relevant reading here. Shard Mothers and Forkborn require attention to context rather than automatic duplication: the Forkborn inheritance can reach beyond a single roster term. Moonlords and Whale Lords remain distinct. Similar wealth imagery does not erase the difference between predatory narrative dependency and the Deep Holder tradition. No name can be collapsed into another solely to make the arithmetic easier.

The reconciled commentary contains forty named cultures. Its earlier thirty-four retain their existing histories, including JPEG Bloodline, Whale Lords, XRP Kids, PU55IES, Slam GiRL$ and Creepto Gals. NoBallGames Legion, Wildstyle Collective, Fractal Taggers, Porch Poets, Resin Relic Keepers and Bone Idol Ink Ritualists complete the register through distinct enduring institutions of belonging. Burn Crews, Stencil Witches and Tag Lords stay within GraffPUNKS; Legion arena extensions stay within the Legion. A numbered archive roll does not turn every service, office or universal family claim into another culture.

Belonging is broader than a delegate

A Games delegate cannot consent for every person sharing a faction name. A sanctuary resident may depend on a lineage without accepting all its interpretations. A repairer can work for a rival without changing her entire belonging. The register therefore describes characteristic institutions and disputes, not an oath attributed to every member. A household can be important to a culture while never sending a fighter to its muster.

The White Shutter bargaining illustrates the difference. The Finance Guild supplies an advance; Bally brokers arrange access; Furies protect a disputed claim; the Army signs a limited compact; households bear consequences none of those representatives can cancel. Cooperation in one sequence does not install a shared government. When the common escort ends, some people continue under an independent agreement. Their departure changes the coalition and does not create a thirty-fifth path.

New commentary belongs to the later margin, not the witnessed text of 2030. The Great Concord remains a common Covenant rather than the abolition of older religions. No current faction name is retroactively placed in its Books. The Faction Commentaries explains the named cultures, their resources and their favourite errors. The HODL Doctrine governs the danger of keeping emergency unity after the purpose for which people agreed to it has ended.

Sources and the later margin

The First Witness chronology and concordance govern this reading. W81 w69 and W2 preserve the female-crew and XRP names; w72 supplies the JPEG Bloodline at the Calling; W5 and W10 preserve the Deep Holder and Whale Lord traditions. The local institutions added here are current fiction, not additional passages in the 2030 Books. The canon foundation evidence ledger records the new register decisions and their limits. The raw archive remains retired from the public tree.

Why a forty-entry list is not a settled forty-path roll

W5's Tome 8 supplies forty numbered labels, but it counts Nomad Bears twice, first as wanderers and later as an expanded explorer division. It describes Burn Crews, Stencil Witches and Tag Lords within GraffPUNKS and then numbers them separately. Four arena entries are expressly extensions of the NoBallGames Legion. Its fortieth entry, Eternal Family, calls itself the sum of all tribes. Reading the full list exposes the membership question its heading conceals.

w68's older directory describes its twenty-eight active cultures as some of forty; HODL collective defence stands outside that numbered list. w69 names the female crews and XRP Kids without supplying a complete roll. M16 calls its compilation complete while leaving most numbered positions TBD. These witnesses support a broad society and several significant missing traditions. Their claims cannot be combined by adding every office, generation and repeated name.

The completed Forty register separates source names from later membership institutions. The six enduring cultures join the earlier thirty-four without replacing their adult customs. A service task alone still does not establish a path, and the nine live Battle Chamber options remain a separate product roster.
