🐸 XRP Meme Coins

XRP meme coins are meme and community tokens associated with the XRP Ledger. Coinranking describes XRP meme coins as tokens on the XRP Ledger inspired by memes, used for trading and community engagement, and listed 151 coins on its XRP meme category page when checked.

Visible examples

Coinranking’s visible list included Phoenix / PHNIX, ARMY, Tabby, TAGZcoin, FUZZY, 666, DROP, BEAR, Opulence and SIGMA among the top visible entries at the time checked. These rankings and values change quickly and should not be treated as permanent.

How to read XRP meme coins

XRP meme coins are not XRP itself. They are separate assets, usually issued tokens or community projects. Research should focus on issuer identity, trust-line setup, liquidity, market source, social source, token controls and whether the project has official documentation.

Risk model

Meme coins are speculative and can be extremely volatile. XRPL token users must verify issuer accounts and trading routes. A token appearing on a list does not prove long-term value, safety or Ripple endorsement.
