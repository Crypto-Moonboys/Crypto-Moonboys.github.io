Lore

From the desolate digital wastelands of Block Topia’s underbelly, GRIT clawed his way into legend as a rugged survivor whose very existence defies the crushing entropy of the metaverse. Hardened by years of struggle against both code and foe, his body bears the marks of survival—glitches turned scars, data corruption turned strength. In events like the Triple Fork Event, GRIT stands as an unmovable force, his raw power a beacon for those lost in the chaos of digital warzones.

Yet, his unknown allegiance keeps factions at bay, uncertain if this stoic warrior will fight alongside them or vanish back into the shadows from whence he came. Some say GRIT seeks redemption for a past lost to corrupted data, while others believe he simply endures because it’s all he knows. Whatever his truth, his presence in the rebellion is a reminder that even in the darkest corners of Block Topia, survival is its own form of defiance.
