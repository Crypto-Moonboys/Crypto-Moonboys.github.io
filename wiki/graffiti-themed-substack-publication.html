A culture with several kinds of record

This register is a map of relationships: artists, fictional figures, publications, tokens, technical projects and places share space because GK’s culture moves between them. That breadth is useful when someone first encounters a name through an artwork and follows it into a story. It also makes the distinctions matter. A character’s allegiance, an artist’s credit and a product’s availability each need their own kind of evidence.

Use the register as a set of doors. GraffPUNKS leads towards music and independent publishing; Graffiti Kings towards working artists and their public history; the First Witness towards a tradition lived differently across cultures. The central Moonboys story supplies the current command relationships. The connected articles carry the detail a compressed name list cannot: what people make, what they believe, and where their versions of the world disagree.
