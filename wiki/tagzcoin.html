🏷️ TAGZcoin

TAGZcoin is named in W81’s XRPL token record and street-art platform proposals. The archive connects it to murals, digital drops and phygital culture. That project history is more specific than the older page’s generic meme-token description, but it does not establish a currently available claim, game or token exchange.

The XRPL archive record

The ecosystem retrospectives list TAGZcoin alongside PMSL and EXYBOY under XRPL. NBG, LFGK and other WAX assets appear in a different group. This chain-specific listing identifies the intended setting of the token reference; a shared cultural network does not make its separate assets interchangeable.

The numbered W81 directory describes TAGZcoin through on-chain murals, digital loot drops, play-to-earn mechanics and phygital street culture. Those are the uses claimed by the directory, not a technical specification or independent confirmation of deployment.

Walls, scans and digital drops

Two promotional street-culture texts put TAGZcoin beside a proposed connection between physical walls and digital objects. They describe scanning a mural to receive NFTs and treating tags as records of ownership. Their wider ambition is to connect graffiti makers across cities through a common platform.

This gives TAGZcoin a particular cultural role in the archive: the wall becomes a point of encounter with an artist’s digital work. It does not follow that every marked wall contains a working claim or that scanning one transfers copyright, property ownership or a licence to paint there. Current project terms and the permission of the relevant maker or property holder remain separate.

Relationship to Crypto Moonboys

Current repository truth places Crypto Moonboys above its individual tools and token experiments as the Graffiti Kings creator umbrella. TAGZcoin is one historical project reference within that wider world. It is not the umbrella’s defining product or a requirement for every participant.

The archive’s language of rebellion and “walls as wallets” belongs to its promotional moment. It should not be converted into a new event in the Year 3008 chronology, or a claim that the Sacred Chain is an XRPL token contract.

Identifying an issued asset

On the XRP Ledger, the issuer and currency identifier distinguish a fungible issued asset. A ticker or recognisable logo is insufficient. The former page cited Coinranking’s XRP meme-token category; a category listing alone does not verify the current official issuer or programme.

This source set does not establish a verified current TAGZcoin issuer, active reward rate, conversion mechanism or live liquidity figure. No trust-line or purchase instruction is inferred from the archive’s cultural description.
