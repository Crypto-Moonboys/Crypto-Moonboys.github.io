🔗 XRPL Trust Lines

Trust lines are a core part of holding issued tokens on the XRP Ledger. They are one of the main reasons XRP meme coins need issuer-level checks before users interact with them.

Plain English

XRP is native to the ledger. Other XRPL tokens generally require users to create a trust line with an issuer. That means the issuer account matters as much as the token name.

Research checklist

Before setting a trust line, verify issuer, token code, supply, freeze settings, official source and liquidity. Avoid copied tickers and unofficial issuer accounts.
