The same name on several stages

Crypto Moonboys works as a name people encounter through art, collecting, creator tools and the fictional saga. The relationship resembles a cast and the world it performs: real people create roles, bring their own histories to them and develop stories with other makers. A newcomer can therefore arrive through a character without having to understand every technical project first.

In the central fiction, the All City Bulls and Nomad Bears give the market a human face. Their members are shadowed around the clock by Alfie’s Bitcoin Kid Army, whose orders come only from GKniftyHEADS. Collective mobilisation produces the HODL Warriors; the distinct HODL X order belongs to Queen Sarah’s Block Topia. Keeping those relationships clear gives all the surrounding myths, images and creator identities a common stage.

It is not limited to one thing — the project explicitly builds it as an overarching franchise that operates on three interconnected levels at once (brand + NFTs + in-universe characters).

1. Brand Name / Franchise / Universe Name

Crypto Moonboys is the official flagship brand and umbrella name for the entire 3008 sci-fi rebellion saga.

Official phrasing on graffpunks.live: “The Crypto Moonboys franchise is the culmination of this journey” and “The Crypto Moonboys universe is our answer to this existential threat. It is not merely a game, an NFT collection, or a token. It is a parallel…”

It encompasses the full multi-chain Web3 empire: play-to-earn game, 24/7 blockchain radio, phygital toys/merch, AR murals, DAO, books, and the “HODL WARS” lore.

Also styled as MIGHTY Crypto Moonboys, GKniftyHEADS Crypto Moonboys & Girls Saga, or Crypto Moonboys Multi-Verse.

The brand reclaims the old crypto slang term “Moonboys” (mocking overly bullish degens) and turns it into a positive battle cry for street-culture rebels who “moon” through holding, creating, and owning the chain.

2. NFT Collections

Crypto Moonboys is also the parent name for multiple specific NFT collections and drops that sit inside the larger universe.

Examples of official collections released under the Crypto Moonboys banner:

The project stresses it is “not merely an NFT collection” — meaning the NFTs are tools/characters within the bigger Crypto Moonboys brand and lore, not the whole thing.

3. The Bald-Headed Bitcoin X Kids from Within the Walls of Blocktopia

In the official 3008 lore, Bitcoin X Kids (also called Bitcoin Kid Army or Bitcoin Kids) are the literal next-generation children born inside the walled megacity of Block Topia.

Exact lore text (graffpunks.live/the-lore): “The children born from these couplings were called the Bitcoin X Kids — genetically secure, ideologically clean, and raised from birth with self-validating chain IDs encoded into their iris patterns.”

They are the genetically-engineered offspring of male Crypto Moonboys (who survive the Hard Fork Games) and Crypto Moongirls. They represent the “pure” future generation inside the sterile, AI-controlled Block Topia empire.

Bald-headed refers to their distinctive visual design in the official artwork, phygital vinyl toys (e.g., “OG Bitcoin Kid” crypto toy), and NFT PFPs — smooth, bald heads with iris-chain ID markings, track-suit armor, and graffiti tags. They are the iconic “kids” faction/character line that appears across toys, game avatars, and lore illustrations.

In short:

Crypto Moonboys = the brand (the whole rebellion saga)

Crypto Moonboys = the NFT collections (the tradeable assets inside it)

Crypto Moonboys = the Bitcoin X Kids (the bald-headed in-universe children who are the literal future of the story).

Everything is designed to interconnect — owning a Moonboys NFT makes you a canon character who can influence the lore, play the game, and even get phygital toys of the Bitcoin X Kids. This triple meaning is intentional and repeated across their official channels.

Darren Cullen (aka SER)

Darren Cullen (aka SER) is a pioneering London-based professional graffiti artist, street art entrepreneur, DJ/music producer, and Web3 innovator from Croydon, UK. He is one of the UK’s earliest graffiti writers (active since 1983) and the founder of Graffiti Kings, a trailblazing commercial graffiti and street art agency that operated for 25+ years before closing in late 2024. Cullen has since pivoted 100% into the GraffPunks / GKniftyHEADS ecosystem—a multi-chain blockchain project blending 40 years of UK hip-hop/graffiti/pirate radio culture with NFTs, play-to-earn gaming metaverses, phygital designer toys, AR street art, and a 24/7 online radio station. His work emphasizes “legacy over hype,” decentralization, community ownership, and “banking the unbanked” through tokenized street culture. He has no connection to any other public figure of the same name.

Background & Biography

Darren James Cullen grew up in Croydon, south London, and began graffiti in 1983 (as a child/early teen) amid London’s BMX, skateboarding, and nascent UK Hip Hop scene. He was part of the original “lil Acorn GraffPUNKS” crew, engaging in classic 80s illegal activities like tagging trains, bombing, and running pirate radio stations from tower blocks. His tag SER started as a crew name (”South East Rockers”) for south London writers; he adopted it permanently when others moved on. Early vandalism led to council interventions, which he turned into paid commissions and youth workshops—marking his shift from “vandal to professional.” By his teens he was already doing legal murals for play centers and train stations. He credits Banksy with helping legitimize graffiti as an art form. Cullen has described himself as a “veteran writer and consummate businessman” who built a global brand from underground roots. He is also a DJ, music producer, digital creator, and blockchain consultant. Current base: London/Kent area. Strong emphasis on mentorship, female empowerment in graffiti, and turning rebellion into positive community impact.

Graffiti Kings (1999–2024)

Cullen founded Graffiti Kings in 1999 as an informal movement of friends teaching graffiti workshops after local councils (Merton, Croydon, Sutton, Lambeth) requested more youth programs to reduce vandalism. It formalized into a limited company around 2007, headquartered at Leake Street (the famous Banksy Tunnel) in London. The agency represented ~30 artists and specialized in commercial graffiti, street art, murals, live painting, branding, event activations, and workshops. It served worldwide clients including major brands (Nike, Adidas, Red Bull, Netflix), Hollywood stars, central banks, royalty, public institutions, and events. Key milestones:

2012 London Olympics: Painted stage sets for the opening ceremony; customized the Olympic cab for BBC London Tonight (described as “artistic royalty”).

Notable public works: Hyperrealist mural of Kate Middleton and the “royal baby” at Brookland Lake, Snodland, Kent; sea-themed classroom murals at Rockmount Infants School (Upper Norwood) with artist Paul Barlow; eco-bus paint job for a green campaigner (chip-fat powered, toured globally).

Workshops & community: Anti-vandalism programs across the UK (e.g., painting Wallington Train Station with 50+ young people involved); corporate executive graffiti sessions; live events at Trinity Leeds and for Sony; features on BBC Young Apprentice.

TV/media: Covered extensively; positioned as the “bridge” between street culture and legacy brands.

The company closed/dissolved in December 2024 so Cullen could focus fully on Web3. His original artworks (spray on board/canvas) have appeared at auction with estimates £800–£1,700+ and have sold through galleries.

Current Focus: GraffPunks / GKniftyHEADS (Full Pivot 2024–Present)

Rebranded as GKniftyHEADS (”born Graffiti Kings, now live as GraffPUNKS”), it launched as a multi-million-dollar Web3 project with NFTs, gaming, phygitals, and metaverse elements. Cullen and team invested personally ($150k–$200k+ reported) with no profits taken—all reinvested. Core ethos: graffiti as “the original blockchain” (decentralized, permissionless); “We don’t sell out, we sell UP”; “legacy over hype.” Key elements:
