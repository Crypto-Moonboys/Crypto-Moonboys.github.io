🌊 XRP Ledger

✅ Source-cited⛓️ XRPL📅 Last updated: June 2026

The XRP Ledger, usually shortened to XRPL, is the public ledger ecosystem behind XRP, issued tokens, trust lines, NFTs, the native decentralized exchange and a fast-growing meme-token culture. For Crypto Moonboys, XRPL matters because it is not only a payment rail: it is also a token and community playground where assets like XRP meme coins, issuer tokens and trading communities can form directly around ledger-native infrastructure.

Quick facts

Native asset: XRP is the native digital asset of the XRP Ledger.
Token model: XRPL supports tokens other than XRP, including fungible trust-line tokens, Multi-Purpose Tokens and NFTs.
Trading layer: Trust-line tokens can be used in cross-currency payments or traded through the XRPL decentralized exchange.

How XRPL differs from generic smart-contract chains

XRPL token culture is built around ledger-native primitives rather than every project needing to deploy a custom smart contract. Tokens often use issuers and trust lines. A user must generally opt in to hold an issued token by setting a trust line, which is different from chains where any token can be sent into a wallet by default.

This structure is central to understanding XRPL meme coins. A ticker name is not enough. The issuer, trust line, supply behaviour, freeze settings, liquidity and community source all matter. Two assets can share a similar name while being completely different issued tokens.

Trust lines and issued tokens

XRPL documentation explains that all assets other than XRP can be represented as tokens, and that trust line tokens are the established fungible token standard on the ledger. Trust lines are relationships between accounts and include limits, balances and settings. This is why proper XRPL token research needs issuer-level verification, not just ticker-level hype.

DEX, liquidity and meme coins

XRPL has native exchange infrastructure where issued assets can trade. That makes it possible for small community tokens and meme coins to form markets without waiting for centralized exchange listings. It also increases risk: liquidity may be thin, issuers may be unknown, and market data can move quickly.

Plain English: XRP is the native asset. XRPL tokens are issued assets. XRP meme coins are usually community-led issued tokens or XRP-branded assets that need careful issuer and liquidity checks.
