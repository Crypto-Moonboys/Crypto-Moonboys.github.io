Evidence labels

Locked means the convergence project treats the point as canonical unless deliberately revised. Probable means multiple strong records align. Faction account means the statement is reliable as evidence of what a group believes. Late tradition appears long after the event. Mythic memory preserves cultural meaning without requiring literal chronology. Disputed means serious alternatives remain. Unknown means the archive does not justify an answer.

A contradiction is not automatically a mistake to delete. It can reveal who controlled a record, what a faction feared, or how an event changed as it moved through centuries of retelling.

Four people, one funeral, four histories

The Concordance is built on a fact families already know: several honest people can remember the same event differently. One remembers the words. One remembers the silence before them. One remembers who was missing. One remembers what happened years earlier that made the words land differently.

Contradiction does not automatically mean one witness is lying. It can mean memory is partial. The historian's job is not to force every account into one smooth story but to ask what each account could see.

Real-life reading: stop cleaning the past

Families clean founders into saints. Brands clean messy launches into strategy. Nations clean accidents into destiny. Artists clean years of copying and failure into one inevitable style journey.

The Concordance treats rough edges as evidence. Keep the embarrassing draft. Keep the old logo. Keep the message showing the plan changed. A truthful history is usually less flattering and far more useful.

This practice is one of the central tools in Life Readings.
