St Patrick’s Day Bear

Waterfront Roamers · Parade route steward · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

St Patrick’s Day Bear keeps his shamrock hat and green goggles ready through the year. He first took responsibility for a procession route after a rehearsal blocked the only convenient way for a bakery shift to get home. The shop crew waited out the rehearsal and gave him their usual finishing times in person the next morning.

The last person in the parade

He still likes a big turnout. He also asks each procession to name the person who will walk last and check that side routes reopen. His least favourite job is telling a late-arriving music group when its slot is gone. He tries to tell them face to face and find out what it cost them to come.

Off duty

His small collection of hand-painted parade boards lives under the table where he grows clover in chipped cups. The board he lends most readily has an arrow that points back towards the harbor. He likes that everyone can understand it after an evening out.
