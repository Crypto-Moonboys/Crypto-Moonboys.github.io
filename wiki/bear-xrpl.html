🐻 BEAR on XRPL

BEAR is an XRP meme-token entry with a theme that crypto traders understand immediately: bear markets, bearish sentiment, survival memes and the humour that appears when prices turn against a community.

Market-cycle identity

BEAR works because it turns a negative market condition into a social identity. In XRP culture, that can mean laughing at red candles, surviving long periods of sideways price action, or using the bear as a badge for holders who stay active when hype cools down.

XRPL token context

BEAR is not XRP itself. It should be researched as a separate XRPL-related issued asset. Users should confirm the issuer account, trust-line setup, market route and liquidity before treating any BEAR token as the correct asset.
