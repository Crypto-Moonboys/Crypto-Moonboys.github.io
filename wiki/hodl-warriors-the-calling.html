The moment a calling reaches the ground

The Calling opens under a sky already wounded by the old world’s systems. Its imagery gives mobilisation an emotional setting: people answer because they inhabit the consequences, not because they have been handed an abstract description of a cause. A summons becomes meaningful when someone must leave an existing duty to meet it.

Read the HODL Warriors as people arriving from many GK factions to form an underworld army. Their shared service has room for different reasons to answer and different ideas of what returning home should mean. The distinct HODL X aspiration remains beyond that mobilisation. The story’s weight lies in what a person agrees to carry before knowing whether the collective can keep its promises.
