Why gaps remain

The chronology is intentionally not filled with invented century markers. A thousand-year history should contain lost periods. New dates can be added when the archive or future approved canon supports them.

The purpose of the master chronology is not to make every old page look correct. It is to make clear which dates the current convergence project uses while preserving old pages as evidence of how history was remembered.

The comfort of exact dates

People like exact dates because dates feel harder than stories. A year printed in a table looks authoritative even when nobody knows where it came from. The First Witness chronology deliberately leaves gaps where the evidence leaves gaps.

This is another quiet challenge to sacred-history habits: precision is not the same as truth. An honest “sometime in this period” can be stronger history than a fabricated day and month repeated for centuries.

Real-life reading: know what you know about your own life

People build personal myths too. “That was the year everything changed.” “I always wanted to do this.” “They never supported me.” Sometimes the broad shape is true and the details have been edited by repetition.

Old calendars, photos, messages and bank records can humble memory. Use them without letting them erase meaning. The date can tell you when you moved house; it cannot fully tell you when the old life stopped feeling like home.

Chronology gives bones. Memory gives flesh. Neither should pretend to be the whole body. See Life Readings.

A local Year 3008 sequence with usable limits

The established local sequence places Three Pump's stoppage before Grey Return, followed by North Sluice and the Quiet Muster's release. Mira Latch's Lower Walk assessment and declined placement belong to the Games-and-return period before Grey Return. This anchors the episode without assigning a fabricated day, a second annual tournament or one bracket to every archive tradition.

The later White Shutter closes the Borough Exchange for nine days. Its reopening leads to the Open Crate Compact's three weekly reviews. The Holdfast response failure occurs after reopening and before the final review; common command ends at that review, with some supporters continuing separately hired escort work. The broken hoist, Dry Step hearings and first recorded restitution payments follow. These relative links preserve events already established in the foundation and institutions batches.

Rack Three's failed and corrected Three Lamps runs, the programme's disputed caption, the Pinkline disc and Cass's limited ground assignment belong to the ensuing late local 3008 account. A corrected passive-record return is observed at ground equipment. No orbital arrival for those applicants is recorded here, and the missing entrant in Nemi Ash's earlier case remains unidentified.

These links give a writer clear before-and-after relationships. They do not claim to order every faction's activity across the world, determine the date of the Sacred Fork or place the broader Final Fork after a scheduled prototype launch. The historical anchors remain 2030, the undated later Sacred Fork, 2880, the by-2930 secure-region convergence and the principal 3008 saga.

Evidence for the new working accounts

The Lower Walk entrant story, Ground Array Office, Rack Three test and connected local developments are newly authored Year 3008 lore. Their source foundations, sequence and limits are recorded in the Games and current operations evidence ledger. They are not newly recovered ZIP passages.

The archive's differing Games tracks remain attributed. A ground data test does not establish orbital consciousness transfer, immunity to NULL or a completed Final Fork. The local account grants no present-day game reward, NFT benefit, access right or creator licence.

Samuel Blake: 2036–2045

The accepted House sequence is 2036 upload at nineteen; late 2039 council; 2040 Stabilisation/Reset; 2041 bridge; early 2042 disappearance of Denise, Tracey and Forge; 2045 climax. The SAM account gives the causal order and its limits. The Great Concord stays in 2030.

W12's contradictory 2029, 2030, 2031, 2032 and 2035 dates survive as damaged or disputed evidence on fictional records. Correct chronology is not permission to rewrite a surviving artifact. The Fifth Carbon keeps its two eras, and the missing household members remain missing. The bridge's extraordinary source claim establishes no general law of resurrection.

One catastrophe, two historical measures

The World Chain Triple Fork is the single 2880 catastrophe. M16's 2198 and w92's 2588 are conflicting archive records of that rupture, not additional identical catastrophes. The loss of over 75% of major cities by 2900 measures urban destruction. Fewer than twelve secure regions by 2930 measures the remaining secure areas; neither number substitutes for the other.

Queens supplies the basis of Block Topia. A source's three decades of construction do not establish an exact inauguration year. The Sacred Fork remains later than the Covenant and undated; Sacred/Aether-Chain, World Chain and True Bitcoin Fork remain distinct.
