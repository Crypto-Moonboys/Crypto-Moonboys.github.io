✕ XRP

XRP is the native digital asset of the XRP Ledger. In this wiki, XRP is covered as both a major cryptocurrency and the cultural centre around XRPL issued tokens, trust-line assets, DEX markets and XRP community identity.

Quick facts

Native ledger: XRP Ledger / XRPL.

Different from: XRPL issued tokens, which use issuer accounts and trust-line mechanics.

Community layer: XRP Army, XRPL builders, meme-token traders, issuer-token collectors and payment-focused crypto users.

XRP versus XRPL tokens

XRP is the ledger-native asset. Tokens such as PMSL, EXYBOY, TAGZcoin, ARMY and other XRPL project assets are separate assets with their own issuer, liquidity and community risk. A ticker alone is not enough to verify an XRPL token.

Why XRP culture matters

XRP culture is unusually community-driven. The XRP Army identity, pro-XRP meme culture and XRPL token experiments all feed into a broader social layer. This is why XRP meme-token pages belong in the Crypto Moonboys wiki beside other chain culture pages.
