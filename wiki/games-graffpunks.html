Overview

The Games route has three parts: the fictional contest inside Block Topia, the GraffPUNKS characters who encounter it, and the project’s separately documented playable activities. A story’s dramatic reward does not establish a current game feature.

Games in the Crypto Moonboys universe

Hard Fork Games is the main account of Queen Sarah P-fly’s selection system, Feast Week, champions and emissaries. Its sources use different elite titles. HODL X Warriors preserves those differences; HODL Warriors covers the separate collective-defence tradition. The contest does not collapse those identities into one title.
