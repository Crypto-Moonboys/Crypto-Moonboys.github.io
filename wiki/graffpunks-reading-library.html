Follow a story into its world, an idea into its consequences, or a project into the people making it. These long-form readings bring the GK publications together with added context and routes into the current Moonboys universe.

The library includes lore, cultural essays, character accounts and historical design proposals. Each reading keeps its original voice while making its relationship to the present clearer. A fictional narrator can have a compelling account without knowing the whole truth; a design paper can be interesting without all its advertised features being live.

For the central story, begin with Crypto Moonboys: the saga, realms and timeline. The master chronology and Concordance explain contested dates and identities. For making your own work, use the creator guide and AI Creator Studio.

Older project quantities, prices, release claims and technical promises remain attached to their publication period. Real creator rights follow published terms. Inside the fiction, the Bitcoin Kid Army protects the market, HODL Warriors unite all factions’ members, and the distinct HODL X order belongs to Queen Sarah’s Block Topia.
