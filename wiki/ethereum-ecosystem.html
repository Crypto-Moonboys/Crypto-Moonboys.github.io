Ecosystem overview

Ethereum (ETH) is the starting point for the network and its smart-contract model. This guide groups reading by subject. Bitcoin, Solana and the story pages below have their own identities; appearing together here does not make them Ethereum projects.

Connections to HODL WARS

HODL WARS borrows market language to tell stories about trust, pressure and obligation. The Great Dip follows a sudden shock; FOMO Plague follows contagious urgency; Rug Pull Wars follows broken promises and their consequences. Paper Hands examines a contested label for departure, while Moon Mission examines the hope of an eventual destination.

These are narrative reading paths, not Ethereum protocols or price forecasts. W81 W2 and W11 supply much of that inherited market vocabulary; the current First Witness reading keeps its moral and prophetic claims open to scrutiny.
