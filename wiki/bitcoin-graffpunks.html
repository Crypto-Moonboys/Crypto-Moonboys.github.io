Overview

Start with the real network, then follow the characters and culture that borrow its vocabulary. The title of this guide joins related subjects; it does not name a separate faction, chain or project.

Bitcoin in the Crypto Moonboys universe

Bitcoin (BTC) introduces the real network. In the Year 3008 story, Bitcoin Kids names Alfie Blaze’s escaped and outside rebels. Bitcoin X Kids names the engineered generation inside Block Topia. A shared word does not make either group the Bitcoin network or merge their histories.
