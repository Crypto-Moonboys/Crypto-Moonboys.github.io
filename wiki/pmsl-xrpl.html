XRPL context

Any PMSL token activity on XRPL should be treated as separate from XRP itself. XRP is the native asset. PMSL-style tokens are project-level assets that require issuer, trust-line, liquidity and official-source checks.

Why PMSL fits XRP meme culture

PMSL is naturally meme-first: it carries humour, reaction culture and community energy. That makes it a good fit for the wider XRP meme-token category, where social identity often matters as much as raw token mechanics.

Research checklist

Verify the issuer, token code, supply, trust-line details, DEX route, liquidity and official project links before assuming any PMSL asset is the canonical Crypto Moonboys / XRPL version.
