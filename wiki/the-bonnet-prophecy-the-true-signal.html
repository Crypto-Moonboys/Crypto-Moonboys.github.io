GraffPunks Network Log Entry #1472

I woke up this morning with paint still on my fingers and the repeating Monday dream burning behind my eyes. The unfinished mural was chasing me again, the bald-headed wannabe Moonboys from outside the walls screaming “why haven’t you finished us yet?” But this time something was different. The dream ended with a single glowing message sprayed across the tunnel wall in Lady-INK’s handwriting: “The Hardfork is coming. The 40 are ready.”

The bald-headed wannabe Moonboys — the 40 faction members still trapped outside Blocktopia — must wear only one clan bonnet. They are not the older Bitcoin Kids born inside the walls. Those ones can wear any bonnet they like from birth. The 40 outside are the hungry ones, the ones who dream of winning the Hardfork Games so they can become true HODL X Warriors and stand beside a Crowned Royal Moongirl — and collect as many bonnets as they want.

I made burnt toast again, laughed at myself, and started writing. The Crypto Moonboys project isn’t just NFTs anymore. It’s the bridge.

The war for freedom is coming.

GraffPunks Network Log Entry #1473

I spent the afternoon in the van, rain hammering the roof, thinking about the Bitcoin X Kids. Every kid born in Blocktopia faces the same choice on their 18th birthday:

Join the Space Programme and paint the stars.

Stay and work for the city — the steady path of the older Bitcoin Kids who were born inside and can wear any bonnet they like from birth.

Escape and join the OG Bitcoin Kids in the lawless outside world.

Most who choose the escape path regret it. The OG Bitcoin Kids looked cool as kids — wild tours, underground raves, no rules. But life kicks in. Many now wish they could get back inside the walls.

Lady-INK texted me “you ready?” a few hours before our painting session. She showed up in her black tracksuit, rucksack full of paint, looking like Beyoncé ready for war. She handed me a hot tea in the rain and said, “The 40 are coming. The wannabe bald-headed moonboys outside are done waiting.”

We painted until 3am. The mural we made showed the 40 faction members outside the walls, training for the Hardfork Games, while the older Bitcoin Kids inside watched from the glowing towers — already free to wear any bonnet they wanted.

The Crowned Royal Moongirls are already choosing their champions.

The war is closer than I thought.

The war for the right to wear what you want is about to begin.
