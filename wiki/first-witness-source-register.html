First Witness Source Register

First WitnessSource register · Section 26Last updated: October 2026

The First Witness is a convergence project built from a deliberately messy archive. This page records what source families informed the reconstruction and how they are weighted.

GK’s current character and command direction

Crypto Moonboys names both the Graffiti Kings creator brand and the characters inhabiting its shared fantasy world. Real people create and play those characters like performers playing roles in a film: their identities, rivalries and stories become part of the world. A fictional role does not turn a character’s actions into a claim about the real person playing them.

Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze leads the Bitcoin Kid Army and takes orders only from the GKniftyHEADS faction. GKniftyHEADS are the underworld good guys: the authority above the Bulls, the Bears and the remaining 37 GK factions that run their city. The Bitcoin Kid Army is the army for the crypto market. It is a military force, not a GK street faction or an extra place in the Forty; Alfie takes its orders only from GKniftyHEADS. The structure is GKniftyHEADS + two main market factions + 37 other GK factions.

When a larger army is needed, all GK factions unite as the HODL Warriors. This collective wartime army is distinct from the HODL X Warriors of Block Topia. Every HODL Warrior aspires to become a HODL X Warrior; aspiration does not itself award the title, erase faction identity or complete the Hard Fork trials.

Queen Sarah P-fly rules Block Topia with her Crypto Moongirls army and the HODL X Warriors within its order. The Bitcoin X Kids are the city’s engineered generation. Some escape and join Alfie’s Bitcoin Kid Army outside; the inside-born generation, the wider outside Bitcoin Kids community and enlisted Army service remain different categories.

GK’s direct instruction of 16 November 2026 supersedes conflicting classifications and the former claim that the HODL relationship was wholly open. The Hard Fork / Rise of the HODL Warriors source remains evidence for Block Topia, the Queen, the Moongirls, the Games and the engineered generation. Its contradictory dates and future outcomes are read through the accepted chronology and the Concordance.

Tier D — existing wiki pages

Pages such as Sacred Chain, Triple Fork Event, Block Topia, HODL Warriors and Alfie Blaze are public archive witnesses.

A page can contain a useful fact even when its surrounding chronology is outdated. The First Witness links back rather than deleting the evidence.

How new evidence changes canon

A new source does not silently overwrite a First Witness page. It should be compared against the Concordance, assigned an evidence label, and then either confirm, refine or explicitly revise the convergence record.

This lets the corpus remain editable without becoming unstable, and lets future writers see why a date or relationship changed instead of discovering a silent retcon.

Resonance is not equivalence

The First Witness deliberately contains echoes of older human wisdom traditions: covenant and witness, hospitality to strangers, stewardship, mercy, non-attachment, service, duty, exile, wilderness, towers, idols, fire, cycles, impermanence, common meals, honest scales and the danger of false prophets.

Those echoes are not claims that Christianity, Judaism, Islam, Hindu traditions, Buddhism, Sikh traditions, Jain traditions, Stoicism or any other living tradition teaches one identical doctrine. They do not. Their theologies, histories and practices differ in important ways. The fictional Concord borrows the human pattern of arguing across those differences, not authority from them.

Some easter eggs are intentionally recognisable to readers who know older texts. They function as cultural sediment: people in 2030 were not born without history, so their new Covenant naturally speaks in images humanity had already used for thousands of years.

Why the references stay indirect

A direct collage of famous scripture lines would make the First Witness feel derivative and would flatten living traditions into props. The better method is conceptual resonance: a tower that warns about universal systems; a wilderness that tests freedom; a common table that tests tribal boundaries; an idol that mistakes the tool for the sacred; a fire that attracts followers and therefore demands suspicion.

Readers who recognise the roots get another layer. Readers who do not still receive a complete idea.

The reader-facing application layer is collected in Life Readings from the First Witness.

The completed source comparison and current decisions

All 94 original W81 source comparisons are complete. W81 is foundational evidence; exact GK decisions reconcile its conflicting records while preserving approved modern fiction. The immutable source inventory retains original filenames, byte counts and hashes. A current interpretation does not rewrite those originals.

The current chronology, Concordance and Forty register carry the accepted historical dates and membership distinctions. Year 3008's public future stays unresolved. Later faction commentary remains separate from all nine original First Witness Books.
