First Witness Source Register

First WitnessSource register · Section 26Last updated: October 2026

The First Witness is a convergence project built from a deliberately messy archive. This page records what source families informed the reconstruction and how they are weighted.

Tier A — current convergence anchors

about/latest-canon-and-brand-vision.md defines the current public brand and canon spine and essential distinctions, including Bitcoin Kids versus Bitcoin X Kids, the 40 factions, Sacred/Aether-Chain and the instruction not to collapse HODL Warriors with HODL X Warriors.

about/w81-condensed-canon-digest.md maps the raw W81 archive into usable canon, cosmology, faction, character and system buckets while explicitly warning that raw posts are not automatically final truth.

Tier B — dedicated modern bibles

The HODL WARS Lore Bible and Master Game Bible support Year 3008 roles, Block Topia, NULL, HODL WARRIORS, walls, the GK Grid and faction-story architecture. Recent faction and character bibles are treated as stronger for their own subjects than older generic promotional posts.

These documents still remain below explicit First Witness decisions because the new project is the place where contradictions are reconciled rather than simply inherited.

Tier C — raw W81 and archive files

The large Crypto Moonboys canon text collections, W81 source posts, gk3 copies and related files preserve names, events, dates, rituals, discarded ideas and contradictions. They are invaluable evidence but not self-validating canon.

Material used in this reconstruction includes the World Chain description, 2880 Triple Fork, Great Unravelling and Chainfire, the 2930 secure-region anchor, 40/40 Hard Fork Games structure, Paint Path disciplines, Block Topia and True Bitcoin Fork conflict, and the surviving faction descriptions.

Tier D — existing wiki pages

Pages such as Sacred Chain, Triple Fork Event, Block Topia, HODL Warriors and Alfie Blaze are public archive witnesses.

A page can contain a useful fact even when its surrounding chronology is outdated. The First Witness links back rather than deleting the evidence.

How new evidence changes canon

A new source does not silently overwrite a First Witness page. It should be compared against the Concordance, assigned an evidence label, and then either confirm, refine or explicitly revise the convergence record.

This lets the corpus remain editable without becoming unstable, and lets future writers see why a date or relationship changed instead of discovering a silent retcon.
