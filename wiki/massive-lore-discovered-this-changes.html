How a cast list becomes a world

A cast register gives a large saga a working memory. Names, generations and allegiances let a reader recognise a person who reappears in a different medium or under another title. The useful unit is a relationship: who taught whom, who commands whom, who escaped which institution and who still has a reason to disagree.

The integrated source document records an earlier arrangement of that cast. Present allegiance follows the current character accounts and GK’s command clarification. Birth among Bitcoin X Kids, life in the outside community and enlistment in Alfie’s Army remain distinct. That distinction gives a character room to make a choice; it prevents an origin label from deciding every later act of loyalty.
