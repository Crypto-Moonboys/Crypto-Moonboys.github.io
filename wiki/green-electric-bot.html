Green Electric Bot

Waterfront Roamers · Garden light and sensor orb · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

The Green Electric Bot was built from a charge shell recovered from a retired greenhouse. Its present circuit follows garden lights and moisture posts where the harbor meets the nature extension. When it hovers around a dark fixture it is usually waiting for the automatic dusk cycle to give it a fair test.

The branch over the sensor

The orb once asked a grounds crew to replace every light that failed on the same evening. Camo Bear walked it back along the trail and pointed out where a newly grown branch covered the light sensor. They freed the sensor and left most of the branch. Green subsequently learned to log shade and weather before it fills out the parts order.

Off duty

It collects the names people use for the same plant and repeats all of them when asked for directions. The habit can double the length of a route announcement. It has earned the orb an invitation to every seed swap on its round.
