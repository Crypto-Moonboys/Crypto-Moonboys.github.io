NoBallGames / WAX / fan token / art and game utility

$NBGThe NoBallGames token built around Charlie Buster's art, graffiti mythology and connected Web3 projects

$NBG is the capped fan and utility token of NoBallGames, the independent art, NFT and game world created around UK street artist Charlie Buster. It was issued on WAX to connect collectable art with community participation, staking, game systems, NFT progression and long-term projects such as Blank Walls.

The token is not the primary currency of Crypto Moonboys, GraffPUNKS or Block Topia. It belongs to the NoBallGames ecosystem. Those worlds may collaborate or connect through specific published integrations, but $NBG should never be presented as a universal token for every Crypto Moonboys faction, game or story.

Collectible fan token — maximum supply 420,000,000 NBG

What NoBallGames Means

NoBallGames began as Charlie Buster's own creative banner: a deliberately blunt name for an artist who combines graffiti, humour, character design, digital collectables and game culture. The phrase carries two meanings. It rejects empty promises and insider games, while also creating a fictional universe in which art, characters and community can be developed without waiting for permission from established institutions.

$NBG extends that identity onto WAX. Rather than existing as an isolated speculative coin, its stated purpose is to move between several creative layers: NFT collecting, artist-led releases, staking programmes, game rewards, graffiti projects and future creator collaborations. The artwork and the community came first; the token was created as infrastructure around them.

“Where Art, Play, and Community Power Their Own Economy.” — published NoBallGames token whitepaper

The Published 2025 Whitepaper

The October 2025 whitepaper established the first full public architecture for $NBG. It described a maximum supply of 420 million tokens and a broad economy built around art, games, staking, liquidity, community claims and specialised future projects.

The document divided the supply into four broad areas: unlocked ecosystem allocations, locked future allocations, reserves and early distribution. Its proposed unlocked pools included liquidity, Blank Walls, play-to-earn gaming, automatic staking, free claims, liquidity farming, treasury development and cross-project liquidity pairs.

Blank Walls and the Future NBG Economy

Blank Walls is the largest long-term utility concept attached to $NBG. The project is designed around ownable graffiti surfaces, original tag art, staking, wall progression, Street Score and rotation into the NBG All City Runners world. The economic goal is to make the token useful because people create, collect, customise and play—not merely because they hold it.

The newer economic framework separates three kinds of value. WAXP acts as the external reserve and liquidity asset. $NBG handles ownership, premium utility, crafting, staking and ecosystem progression. Street Cash is intended as a non-transferable everyday gameplay counter. Keeping these roles separate prevents every minor game action from becoming a speculative token transaction.

Blank Walls, Tag Packs and All City Runners remain subject to their own constitutions, launch status and implemented contracts. Their planned role explains the direction of $NBG utility; it does not make unreleased mechanics live.

Emissions, Decay and Sustainability

The original whitepaper proposed declining emissions rather than unlimited flat rewards. A follow-up ten-year plan described an expansion phase, a growth phase and a later sustainment phase in which real ecosystem income would increasingly replace token emissions.

That model proposed directing income back into the mechanism that generated it, project development, market buybacks, treasury reserves and permanent burns. The principle is regenerative: early token pools help create activity, while later NFT sales, fees, royalties and other real income are intended to support the system without continually increasing supply.

Emission schedules and redistribution percentages can be revised before deployment. Only the rules attached to an active contract, public vault or current constitution should be treated as operational.

Buybacks, Burns and Visible Vaults

NoBallGames has explored automated royalty routing in which part of WAXP income accumulates in transparent vaults. One design uses a token-burn vault that purchases official $NBG after a published threshold is reached and sends the acquired tokens to an irreversible burn destination. A separate NFT vault can purchase eligible NoBallGames NFTs and remove them from circulation.

These mechanisms are intended to make secondary activity support the wider collection rather than disappear into an opaque project wallet. They also introduce risks: low liquidity, slippage, front-running, manipulated NFT floors, unsuitable marketplace permissions and contract mistakes. A proposed burn system must not be described as active until its accounts, permissions, thresholds and transaction history are publicly verifiable.

NBGX and Other Proposed Branches

The whitepaper reserved supply for several possible branches, including NBGX, charity, energy, storage, DeFi, meme and Bitcoin Cash-linked systems. These were expansion concepts rather than a statement that seven complete economies were already operating.

NBGX is the most developed named companion concept: a larger-supply fractional token intended to improve accessibility and support specialised liquidity or utility. It must still be treated as separate from $NBG. Holding one does not automatically grant rights in the other unless an active contract or published programme states the conversion or pairing rules.

NoBallGames and Crypto Moonboys

NoBallGames is Charlie Buster's artist-led project. Crypto Moonboys is the wider Graffiti Kings creator, character, game and lore universe. The two are connected through Charlie's work, shared Web3 history, collaborations and possible game or NFT integrations, but they are not interchangeable brands.

$NBG should therefore appear on Crypto Moonboys pages only where the relationship is specific: a NoBallGames collaboration, a supported NFT route, a named game integration, Blank Walls, NBG All City Runners or a documented reward programme. It should not be inserted into unrelated GraffPUNKS lore or presented as the currency of all forty factions.

Current Status and Source Discipline

The Medium whitepaper records the published economic vision. Charlie Buster's @nftbuster account on X is a public project-update channel for newer announcements. Neither source should be stretched beyond what it actually confirms.

Every $NBG claim should be labelled by status:

  • Live: supported by a current contract, interface, pool, wallet or transaction history.
  • Active programme: has published dates, eligibility, reward rules and an operating distribution route.
  • Historical: previously operated but is no longer open.
  • Planned or proposed: appears in a whitepaper, roadmap or design document but is not yet available.

This distinction protects collectors from confusing creative ambition with deployed functionality.

Risk and Collector Positioning

$NBG is positioned as a collectible fan and utility token, not a share in a company, a claim on project revenue or a promise of profit. Token prices and liquidity can move sharply. Reward pools can finish. Interfaces can close. Smart contracts, wallets, bridges and decentralised exchanges can fail or be used incorrectly.

Collectors should verify the token contract, supported market, pool depth, slippage, wallet permissions and programme status before acting. Never assume that a roadmap allocation guarantees future value or that staking rewards remove market risk.

The useful question is not “How high can it go?” It is “What verified action can this token perform today, and what risk must I accept to use it?”
