🎮 Gang Signs (Card Game)

🎮 Gaming 📅 Last updated: March 2026 📂 Gaming

Gang Signs Card Game is the existing companion reference for Gang Signs, the street-art card-battler concept associated with Bone Idol Ink. It is not a second game. This page records the supported project premise; the fuller article develops its fictional Card Dens, Tag Dealer and inherited tournament stories.

Cultural setting

The concept draws on GraffPUNKS’ visual language of tags, crews and competing loyalties. Faction signs can give a card a recognisable identity, while alliances and disruption provide a strategic premise. That association does not make the card game a faction of its own or grant it control over the forty factions.

What the source establishes

W81’s Bone Idol Ink compilation names a Gang Signs card-battler/GameFi element in connection with the artist’s GraffPunks work. The inherited specialist records also associate the concept with a Medium reference attributed to @boneidolink. The evidence supports the name, cultural relationship and broad format.

It does not supply a complete deck list, turn sequence, victory condition, release build, official tournament structure or current staking mechanism. Those missing parts cannot be inferred from a story about territorial rivalry.

The card table in the fiction

The Gang Signs chapter retains the Card Dens and Tag Dealer as fictional traditions, including contested trophies, invitations and unreadable offers. Their purpose is to bring opposing loyalties to one table, where a legal move can still damage a relationship.

The former companion page repeated its opening description almost in full under Lore, then treated every match as a permanent Sacred Chain transaction and promised encrypted AR advantages. The source does not establish those capabilities. They are removed as facts about a real game rather than copied into another section.

Concept, edition and release

A card concept, a printed edition, an NFT asset and a working game client are separate things. The record available here does not identify a published Gang Signs ruleset or prove that buying an object grants playable effects. Any actual edition must state its own format, credits, rules and access terms.

This narrow project reference complements the longer story chapter without repeating its entire cast and mythology. For the associated creator’s work, see Bone Idol Ink.

Source record

W81 W8.txt, Bone Idol Ink compilation, contains the card-battler mention. The inherited wiki records provide the broader strategic premise and the original Medium citation; repeated copies of that citation are one reference, not independent confirmations.

Inherited media citation

The original page linked a @boneidolink Medium Gang Signs article as a media reference. That link alone cannot verify a release, a card’s behaviour or ownership rights.
