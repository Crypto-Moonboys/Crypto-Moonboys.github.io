A radio culture before a token diagram

The PUNK proposal starts from a cultural activity that requires continuing labour: selecting music, producing a broadcast, supporting DJs and keeping an audience together. A token design tries to describe how some of that participation might circulate as incentives and resources.

The scheme should be read through the broadcast rather than allowing the asset to obscure it. Who makes the programme worth hearing, who keeps the channel available and how is that work supported over time? Listen2Earn and Rave2Earn language records the source’s proposed mechanics. Current programme terms and implementation evidence establish what a listener or organiser can actually use.
