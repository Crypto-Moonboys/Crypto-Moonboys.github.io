💱 XRPL DEX

XRPL DEX refers to the decentralized exchange functionality built into the XRP Ledger. It allows issued assets to trade against XRP and other XRPL assets through ledger-native order book mechanics.

Why it matters

XRPL meme coins and community tokens often depend on issuer verification, trust lines and available liquidity. A listing or ticker name alone is not enough to prove safety or value.
