Jamaican Bear

Waterfront Roamers · Selector and record mender · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

Jamaican Bear inherited a record case with a broken hinge and a paper list of the people who had lent the records inside. He rebuilt the case and took it down to the Waterfront when he heard workers were running a small listening session after the shops closed. Several of the names in the case were still working across the harbor.

The watermarked sleeve

He likes a set that makes room for somebody else’s favourite. Once he borrowed a rare pressing from a neighbour and left it backstage long enough for the sleeve to get wet. He spent a month finding a replacement and learned that his neighbour cared most about a note on the original sleeve. He now checks for marks, dates and dedications before he takes a record out of someone’s home.

Off duty

His hobby is mending old sleeves with hands that were never particularly steady. While the glue dries he asks the Walking Boombox for one song he would never pick. The two of them disagree happily about which song that should be.
