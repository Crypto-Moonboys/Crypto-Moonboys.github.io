Police Car

Waterfront Roamers · Mobile report desk · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

The Police Car is a self-driving Waterfront patrol unit. Dockworkers nicknamed it the Waiting Room after it held its cabin warm for a stranded mechanic while they waited for the evening crossing to clear. Its compact frame and roof lamps are visible above a parked row of freight carts.

The wrong cart

The car carries dry report forms, spare route boards and a seat for whoever has the longest explanation. It once left a signed removal notice on a cart that belonged to another shift. The driver lost an evening’s work setting the matter straight. These days the car asks the desk to read back a location and a name before it marks a report closed.

Off duty

Between rounds it matches loose dashboard screws to their original fittings. It keeps the cup holder free for its next passenger, even when it has nowhere else to put the screws. Its preferred sign-off is “I’ll leave the light on until you’re through.”
