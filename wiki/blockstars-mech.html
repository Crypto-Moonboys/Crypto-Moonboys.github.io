Blockstars Mech

Waterfront Roamers · Mobile stage crew · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

The Blockstars Mech began as a touring platform that could carry its own lamps. Its gold and red frame still looks ready to roll into a broadcast. On the Waterfront it brings lighting for small shows, announcements and recordings made amid forklift noise and wind off the water.

Who gets the closing credit

A promoter once credited its operator with staging a whole evening alone. Three people spent the hour after the show coiling cable and carrying lamps to the repair room. Their names never made the broadcast. The operator asks crews to prepare the closing credits while there is still time to check who is present.

Off duty

A service panel holds ticket stubs from shows the operator attended as an audience member. They prefer the ones with the seat number torn away. They can remember the performance without remembering whether they were supposed to sit somewhere better.
