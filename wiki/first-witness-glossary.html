First Witness Glossary

First WitnessReference · Section 25Last updated: October 2026

The same word often changes meaning over nearly a thousand years. This glossary records the convergence meaning and major later uses.

Core terms

Covenant
The 2030 shared human text created without replacing older religions.
First Witness
The Covenant itself, its Bitcoin provenance event, or the wider tradition depending on context.
Witness
Evidence preserved so later people can inspect rather than merely trust authority.
Keeper
A temporary custodian of power, resources, memory or systems.
Wall
In 2030, public expression and cultural memory; by 3008 also literal graffiti infrastructure, signal and alternative archive.
Chain
Continuity between records and generations; never automatically a synonym for truth.
Fork
A legitimate or destructive separation from a shared system, depending on purpose and consequence.
Hold / HODL
Preserve what must survive pressure. Later crypto and warrior traditions add specialised meanings.
Unwritten Block
The Covenant's refusal to let one generation author the final word for all future people.
Bitcoin Witness
The 2030 cryptographic provenance anchor for the Covenant.
Sacred Chain / Aether-Chain
The later mythic memory/proof tradition carrying authenticated scripture, identity and cultural records.
World Chain
The later civilisation-scale infrastructure that unified money, identity, governance and historical record before the 2880 collapse.
Triple Fork
The 2880 event in which the World Chain produced three conflicting realities.
Chainfire
Survivor term for the collapse process after the Triple Fork.
Great Unravelling
Historical term for the wider post-2880 breakdown of consensus and civilisation.
True Bitcoin Fork
Block Topia's later protected identity/governance anchor; not simply another name for the Sacred Chain.
GK Grid
The Year 3008 street-level tactical and cultural network connecting walls, factions, routes and memory systems.
Paint Path
GraffPUNK communication and memory practice using symbols, murals, smart pigment and cultural context.
Forty Paths
The forty-faction civilisation model: one inherited scripture interpreted through different cultures and survival roles.
Bitcoin Kids
Alfie Blaze's escaped or outside rebel lineage.
Bitcoin X Kids
The engineered next generation raised inside Block Topia's controlled system.
HODL Warriors
In convergence 3008 lore, collective defence when the Sacred Chain, memory and reality are under existential threat; the term also has a current real-world creator-builder use.
HODL X Warriors
A distinct archive tradition whose exact relationship to HODL Warriors remains unresolved.
NULL / Antichain
Erasure force or tradition threatening the reliability and meaning of records, names and memory.
Child of Fire
Later interpretation of Book VIII often applied to Alfie Blaze; never a direct 2030 name for him.

Reading terms by era

A term's later religious importance should not be pushed backwards into 2030 unless the Book actually contains it. “HODL Warriors,” for example, is a later title. “Hold” is the scripture word. The same separation applies to the Sacred Chain, World Chain, Block Topia and named factions.

This protects the world from retroactive exposition and lets language accumulate history naturally.

Words migrate

A thousand years is long enough for ordinary words to become sacred and sacred words to become slang. “Hold” becomes HODL. “Witness” becomes a technical provenance term and a spiritual title. “Fork” moves from software into politics, theology and identity.

Real languages do the same. Words that once named a legal office, a school, a ritual, a stranger or a philosophical idea accumulate emotional meanings their earliest speakers would not recognise.

Real-life reading: define the word before fighting about it

Many arguments are actually collisions between definitions. Freedom, respect, loyalty, violence, censorship, ownership, faith, decentralisation and community can mean different things to people using the same sentence.

Before winning the argument, ask the boring question: what do you mean by that word? The answer can reveal that two people agree more than they thought—or disagree somewhere deeper than the slogan.

The Life Readings use this discipline throughout: open the reader-facing guide.
