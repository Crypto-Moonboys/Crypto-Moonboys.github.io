Police Robot

Waterfront Roamers · Crossing marshal · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

The Police Robot took its current shift at the Waterfront after a repair crew trained it to recognise a queue from the back. It had previously reported clear passage whenever the person at the front stepped aside. Now it counts the people still waiting and asks who has been there longest. Its lamp is easy to recognise where the harbor streets narrow into the island paths.

A place to wait

It keeps two records of every obstruction. One gives the time and location. The other records the account of whoever had to turn back. A shopkeeper once asked it to remove a folding stool from a landing. The stool belonged to the person who opened the landing each morning. The robot reported the blocked space and asked the shopkeeper to find that person somewhere else to sit.

Off duty

After the shift it collects damaged door numbers and sorts them by the depth of the paint. It knows every shop that has changed names twice. Its favourite conversation begins with a simple question. “Could you get through here carrying something heavy?”
