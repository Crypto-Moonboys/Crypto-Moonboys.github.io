Posh Bear

Waterfront Roamers · Condition assessor and salvage valuer · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

Posh Bear came to the Waterfront in a good suit and earned the nickname before he had finished his first shift. His green monocle magnifies the old tool marks that can tell a repairer whether two pieces belong together. He has spent more time assessing battered cabinet hinges than buying anything expensive.

The smallest sign

A dealer once offered a generous price for a group of workshop signs on the understanding that the smallest one would be thrown away. It carried the only legible last name from an old cooperative. Posh Bear walked it back to the family that had asked about the sale and had to admit he had nearly written the thing off himself.

Off duty

He likes estimating the age of a stitch through a layer of later mending. The seams of his own plaid suit are a running diary of repairs. He accepts a cup of tea for a small appraisal and insists on paying for a good stitch.
