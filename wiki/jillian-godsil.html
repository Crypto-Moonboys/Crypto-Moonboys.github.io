Project connection

The connection recorded here is coverage and recognition, not faction membership. Crypto Moonboys is the Graffiti Kings creator umbrella, carrying public culture, creator work and a separate fictional world. A person can report on that world without becoming its operator, endorsing every proposal or joining one of its forty fictional factions.

The fictional acknowledgement

The inherited lore treatment associates her name with measured perspective, accountability and thoughtful engagement. It can be understood as a symbolic tribute to reporting and scrutiny. It is not evidence that the real person operates the Sacred Chain, controls the Dream Sovereign’s simulations or designs the Hard Fork Games. No new fictional title, office, event or character biography is assigned here.

Reporting and responsibility

In the same way, appearing in a project’s lore does not transfer the reporter’s identity, writing or other rights to collectors.
