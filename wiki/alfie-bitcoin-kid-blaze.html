Lore

Within the embattled walls of Block Topia, Alfie 'Bitcoin Kid' Blaze rises as the indomitable leader of the Bitcoin Kids, a faction of escaped rebels whose very existence spits in the face of centralized tyranny in the Crypto Moonboys universe. Known for his audacious escapes from digital prisons and his fiery rhetoric, Alfie embodies the raw, untamed spirit of decentralization, a beacon for those crushed under the weight of oppressive algorithms.

His story is one of relentless liberation, forged in the crucible of countless skirmishes where he outwitted enforcers of the old order to free his comrades from virtual shackles. As a central figure in the GraffPUNKS saga, Alfie’s actions reverberate through HODL WARS and the Hard Fork Games, his guerrilla tactics turning the tide against larger, better-equipped armies. His name is scrawled in digital graffiti across the blockchain, a rallying cry for the disenfranchised to rise up and claim their freedom.

Yet, for all his bravado, Alfie carries the weight of his people’s hopes, knowing that each victory brings them closer to a dream of true autonomy, even as the walls of Block Topia threaten to close in tighter with every passing battle.
