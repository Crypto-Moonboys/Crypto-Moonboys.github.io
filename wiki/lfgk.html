🪙 $LFGK

🪙 Cryptocurrencies 📅 Last updated: March 2026 📂 Cryptocurrencies

$LFGK is a WAX ecosystem token documented in a GraffPUNKS greypaper dated 22 May 2025. The archive connects it to liquidity, community rewards, token listings and NFT-related activity. That document mixes a dated token snapshot with future plans; it does not establish which programmes operate today.

Project relationships

The real project record places LFGK among GKniftyHEADS and GraffPUNKS community activities, with a proposed liquidity pairing for NoBallGames’ $NBG. These are specific relationships between projects, not evidence that one token is the currency of all forty fictional factions. Crypto Moonboys remains the wider creator umbrella.

The archive also uses LFGK in a Bitcoin Cash token list. A shared ticker does not prove that two issued assets are interchangeable, bridged or redeemable for one another. The WAX greypaper identifies its own contract and precision; those identifiers belong to that record.

The dated WAX token record

The greypaper gives the symbol LFGK, contract account kingsofgraff, four decimal places and a maximum supply of 99,999,999,999. It describes the supply as fully circulating. Holder totals, price, liquidity and market capitalisation are explicitly snapshots from 22 May 2025; they are not current market measurements.

Its allocation model assigns 40% to liquidity, 20% to staking rewards, 20% to future exchange listings, 5% to community incentives, 5% to NBG pairing and approximately 10% to public holders. These headings explain the proposed uses of the supply. They do not show that every allocation reached its destination.

The document contains arithmetic and status tensions. The community fund’s stated “0.5%” Year 1 release is paired with a quantity that does not reconcile with either the stated fund or total supply. It also calls the token fully circulating while describing escrowed allocations. Those inconsistencies remain visible rather than being silently converted into a new release schedule.

Rewards, coupons and the burn-site history

The staking proposal would release 2% of its reward pool annually, split between NFT/project incentives and LFGK Fun Coupons. Exchange-listing reserves would be released on a recurring schedule, with unused allocations returned to escrow. These are greypaper mechanisms, not a verified current yield or listing commitment.

Separate archive promotions describe collecting ten eligible NFTs and swapping or burning them for an LFGK Fun Token. A retrospective account describes the LFGK Burn Site as a route for burning ten NFTs from GK collections for rewards. The wording differs by campaign; neither account establishes that any ten assets qualify today.

Another promotion promises LFGK rewards three months after the public release of HODL WARS. This is a conditional historical announcement. The text alone establishes neither that release date nor an active claim window. Current Arcade XP, Moonpet progression and website engagement must be read from their own implemented rules.

Liquidity protection and its limits

The greypaper proposes permanently nullifying roughly 75% of project-held LP tokens through eosio.null, with a longer-term ambition of 100%. It also describes multisignature escrow and timed releases. The same archive alternates between completed and future-tense descriptions, so an implementation claim requires the corresponding accounts, permissions and transactions.

LP-token nullification is different from burning the traded LFGK supply. Restricting withdrawal of a pool position would not by itself prevent price loss, thin markets, contract faults or misuse elsewhere. Promotional phrases such as “rug-proof” are the source’s claims, not security findings made by this reference.

The separate LFGK Vault tradition

A fictional codex places an LFGK Vault beneath the London Dream Loop. It describes a quantum-locked reserve funding rebel operations, gear and Codex completions, with entry requiring seven synchronised faction tags and a Dream Echo Node readout. That is a Year 3008 story tradition; it is not the location or access procedure of a real treasury.

The older wiki repeatedly recast the real token as a reward forged from the 2880 catastrophe, with predictive powers and “Blood Bits” metadata. The archive supports the Vault motif but does not establish those added minting claims. The World Chain disaster, the Sacred Chain’s inherited memory and a present WAX token are separate subjects.

Present use and project status

A historical specification can help identify the intended asset and explain a proposal. A current reward requires published eligibility, an operating distribution route and its own terms. No token balance establishes fictional rank, a blanket artwork licence or a right to withdraw project funds.

The greypaper itself calls its details changeable and disclaims a financial promise. Its supplied contract record should be checked against current project material before any interaction; this article makes no new claim that a listed mechanism is live.
