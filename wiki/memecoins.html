Meme Coins

✅ Verified📅 Last updated: October 2026📂 Cryptocurrencies

Memecoins are crypto assets whose identity is strongly associated with internet humour, characters or social trends. Some run on their own networks; others are tokens on an existing chain. Culture and speculation can both drive attention, but neither establishes a predictable return. A large paper valuation does not mean holders can sell an entire position at the quoted price.

History and origins

Dogecoin (2013)

Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer launched Dogecoin in December 2013 using the popular Doge image and humour. Its community became known for tipping and fundraising, including support connected with the 2014 Jamaican bobsled team. Dogecoin is a native proof-of-work coin, which distinguishes it technically from a token issued on Ethereum.

Public attention, including Elon Musk’s comments and 2021 television appearance, accompanied periods of intense trading. That history does not show that every early holder became wealthy or that publicity has a consistent effect on price.

Shiba Inu (2020)

Shiba Inu launched on Ethereum in 2020 under the pseudonymous Ryoshi identity. An unsolicited allocation sent to Vitalik Buterin later featured in donations and burns. Such transfers did not make him the project’s founder or establish his endorsement.

A headline wallet valuation needs context before it can describe a realised return: acquisition cost, dates, token quantities, transfers and available liquidity. Multiplying an old balance by a later quoted price does not establish that its owner received that amount.

PEPE, BONK and dogwifhat

BONK’s December 2022 distribution became associated with Solana community activity. PEPE, launched on Ethereum in 2023, used imagery based on Matt Furie’s Pepe the Frog; use of an image does not itself establish the artist’s endorsement or licensing. Dogwifhat became another prominent Solana-associated example.

Launch platforms lowered the effort needed to issue new tokens. A familiar interface does not certify a token’s creator, distribution or safety. Examples should be identified by exact network and contract or asset identifier, since names and tickers can be copied.

Political tokens (2025)

TRUMP and MELANIA tokens attracted attention around the January 2025 US inauguration. Political branding adds questions about promotion, conflicts of interest and how buyers interpret association with public office. It does not make an asset government-backed or confer a legal right to access an official.

How they work

A memecoin can use standard token infrastructure, a native blockchain or specialised contract features. It may include minting authority, transfer restrictions, taxes, governance or application functions. “Memecoin” therefore does not guarantee a simple contract, fixed supply, absence of insiders or zero utility.

Trading depends on available markets and liquidity. Some tokens use bonding-curve launch mechanisms before moving to other pools. The resulting price path is not a universal lifecycle, and an exchange listing or trending post does not establish the ability to sell a large position. No equal probability of enormous gain and total loss can be inferred from the category.

Culture, participation and speculation

People may engage through humour, collecting, tipping, community activity or speculation. Their motives are not all the same. The visibility of a successful community can obscure abandoned tokens and people who suffered losses, so a few famous examples cannot establish a typical outcome.

Collective attention can give a symbol cultural importance while its token remains highly volatile. Participation, authorship of the image and ownership of a token are separate relationships. None automatically grants rights to the underlying artwork or control of the community.

Risks and limitations

  • Concentrated holdings, undisclosed promotion and coordinated trading can distort apparent demand.
  • Transfer restrictions, hostile contract logic or missing liquidity can prevent an intended sale.
  • Copied tickers and artwork can make impersonation difficult to spot from a name alone.
  • Large nominal supply, burns and advertised scarcity say little without the full distribution and control rules.
  • Loss can be severe or total. A reliable failure rate would require a defined population, observation period and treatment of abandoned or illiquid tokens.

Memes in Crypto Moonboys

Crypto Moonboys uses humour, characters and market vocabulary as part of its cultural expression. The FOMO Plague, Rug Pull Wars and NGMI Chronicles can be read as fiction and commentary; they are not a verified statistical record of all token outcomes.

The creator umbrella supports building original work under a Moonboy or an existing identity. Community participation does not require chasing a launch, holding through a crash or following a supposed exit formula. Current asset terms govern any real collectible or token discussed by the project.
