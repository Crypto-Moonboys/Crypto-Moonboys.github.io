Police Bike

Waterfront Roamers · Route rider and dispatch bike · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

The Police Bike is the Waterfront’s robot rider and bike pair. They make good time when radio reception breaks up between buildings. The rider carries written messages between crossings and brings back a person’s answer in their exact words. The bike has had three front mudguards. The rider still has the first one, bent nearly in half and marked with the names of everyone who helped retrieve it.

The handover

A dispatch officer once praised the pair for arriving before the route closure. The rider accepted the praise, then realised the warning had reached a shop door while the shopkeeper was away. Now it records the handover as well as the arrival. It will wait with a notice long enough for the relief shift to show up.

Off duty

The rider sketches wheel tracks after rain to remember the places carts naturally turn. On a quiet evening it picks one curve and tries to take it smoothly enough to keep a cup of tea upright in the saddlebag.
