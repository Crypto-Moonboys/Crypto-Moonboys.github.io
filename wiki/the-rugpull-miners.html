⚔️ The RUGPULL MINERS

⚔️ Lore 📅 Last updated: March 2026 📂 Lore

The Rugpull Miners are trust predators: scammers and exploiters who build convincing temporary systems, attract resources through credibility and disappear once extracting value becomes more profitable than maintaining the promise.

Mining Trust, Not Blocks

Their name is deliberately cynical. What they mine is not honest computational work but the social trust accumulated by other people.

A successful operation looks legitimate long enough to acquire deposits, labour, data, access or reputation. The exit only works because the bait looked like something worth joining.

The Bait Network

W81 describes false farms, trading hubs and investment systems assembled from convincing fragments of older infrastructure. The Miners mimic whatever their target already wants to believe.

AllCity Bulls are tempted with privileged markets; rebels with restricted information; isolated crews with apparently pristine caches. The mechanism changes. The exploit remains the same.

Digital Disguise

Impersonation and borrowed credibility are central tools. A Miner may copy faction symbols, fake provenance or attach a respectable intermediary to a bad offer.

The First Witness makes this an especially serious offence because the scam weaponises the same verification habits meant to protect people.

Enemies

Blockchain Furies hunt them as accountability targets. Finance Guild systems try to keep them outside trusted settlement. Bally Boys dislike them for a more practical reason: repeated rug pulls destroy the minimum market trust brokers need to keep earning.

These rivalries can create strange temporary alliances between factions that otherwise dislike one another.

Cursed Code and Hidden Vaults

Older wiki material describes cursed code, supernatural vault guardians and artefacts that poison anyone who touches them. Those belong to later street mythology unless a specific story locks them.

The grounded threat is sufficient: a faction capable of making fraud look like legitimate opportunity can destabilise entire communities without magic.

The Faction Weakness

The Rugpull Miners understand greed and urgency in other people. Their problem is that successful predation consumes the trust environment that makes the next scam possible.

They can become so good at extracting value that they leave no market worth exploiting.
