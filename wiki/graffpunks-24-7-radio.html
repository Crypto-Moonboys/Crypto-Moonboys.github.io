GRAFFPUNKS 24/7 RADIOThe sound of London street culture rebuilt as a worldwide digital station

GraffPUNKS 24/7 is the continuous online radio arm of the GraffPUNKS and Crypto Moonboys world. It broadcasts music around the clock while carrying forward a culture that existed long before Web3: London graffiti, BMX, skateboarding, underground clubs, tower-block transmitters, independent record shops, pirate radio and the refusal to wait for permission before creating a platform.

The station is real, public and listenable. Its wider blockchain, token, NFT, gaming and lore layers form the evolving GraffPUNKS network around that broadcast. Music is the centre. Everything else—art, characters, digital ownership, communities and stories—is built around the signal.

What the Station Is

GraffPUNKS 24/7 describes itself as a worldwide online radio blockchain station powered by the wider GKniftyHEADS and Crypto Moonboys ecosystem. The broadcast is designed as a permanent meeting point between DJs, listeners, artists, collectors and the international GraffPUNKS network.

It is not limited to one narrow genre or one generation of club music. The station moves across house, techno, dance, Balearic, hip hop, grime, drum and bass, jungle, dubstep, trap, disco, funk, soul, roots, culture, dub, downtempo electronica and other sounds selected by its resident DJs and guests.

Always broadcasting

The station is presented as an online 24/7/365 network rather than a single weekly programme or occasional podcast.

Human selectors

Named DJs and hosted shows give the station personality, history and musical judgement rather than reducing it to an anonymous automated playlist.

Global reach

The line-up brings together DJs and listeners beyond London while retaining the attitude and visual language of the culture that created it.

Connected culture

The broadcast sits beside Graffiti Kings art, GraffPUNKS, GKniftyHEADS, free NFT activity, community channels and the Crypto Moonboys narrative world.

From London Walls to Pirate Airwaves

The roots of the station are not a retrospective branding exercise. The people behind GraffPUNKS came through overlapping London street cultures stretching from the 1970s into the 1980s and 1990s: BMX, skateboarding, graffiti vandalism and art, independent music, raves, record shops and pirate broadcasting.

Graffiti claimed physical space. Pirate radio claimed invisible space. Both worked through the same basic principle: when the official system does not represent your culture, build your own channel and transmit anyway. Walls, trains and tower-block aerials became parallel forms of public publishing.

The network connects this history to Graffiti Kings, the early London graffiti movement, Jonny and Laurence Nelson of TAG Records, underground music culture and DJ Trevor Fung, named by GraffPUNKS as its Godfather and ambassador. The modern station converts that lived history into a global internet broadcast without smoothing away its rough edges.

GraffPUNKS did not move from graffiti into radio by changing culture. The wall and the airwave were always two versions of the same act: make the signal visible, make it audible, and do not wait to be invited.

Trevor Fung and the Acid-House Lineage

Trevor Fung occupies a special position in the network. GraffPUNKS identifies him as its Godfather and ambassador, connecting the station to the formative energy of British club culture and the movement of music through informal networks, parties and scenes before those sounds were absorbed by mainstream institutions.

His Version Excursion show represents more than a celebrity name on a timetable. It links the contemporary station to the generation that understood the DJ as organiser, cultural transmitter and community catalyst. In the GraffPUNKS model, the DJ does not merely play tracks. The DJ keeps a public culture moving.

Art, Music and Blockchain

GraffPUNKS presents the station as the broadcast centre of a broader blockchain-enabled cultural network. The public vision connects listening with $PUNK, free collectable NFTs, digital art, designer toys, Telegram activity, games and multi-chain communities across WAX, Bitcoin Cash, Solana and XRPL.

Those elements must be described according to their current status. The radio stream and published programme schedule are public station functions. Token rewards, Listen2Earn, track-triggered drops, NFT unlocks and game integrations should only be described as live where a current interface, contract or official announcement proves that they are operating. Elsewhere they remain vision, design or planned utility.

This distinction does not weaken the idea. It clarifies it. The long-term proposition is to transform passive listening into participation: a station where a listener can discover music, enter a community, collect art, support creators and move between media without leaving the same cultural world.

The $PUNK Connection

$PUNK is presented as the token associated with the radio network and its intended audience economy. In the published GraffPUNKS vision, it supports ideas such as listener participation, artist and DJ rewards, event activity, digital collectables and wider community utility.

The token should not be described as guaranteed income, guaranteed yield or automatic payment for listening. Any reward mechanic requires a live contract, published rules, eligibility conditions and a functioning distribution system. The radio’s cultural value does not depend on financial promises.

At its strongest, the $PUNK concept treats attention differently from conventional streaming platforms. Instead of extracting listener data while paying creators through distant corporate systems, the proposed model attempts to keep more value, recognition and participation inside the community that generates the music and culture.

A Station for Artists and Outsiders

GraffPUNKS 24/7 exists within a wider belief that cultural infrastructure should not belong only to large labels, broadcasters, advertising platforms or technology companies. Its identity is deliberately independent, loud and difficult to sanitise.

The network creates room for established DJs, underground selectors, artists, collectors and listeners who recognise the connection between music and visual culture. Graffiti supplies the public mark. Radio supplies the public rhythm. Blockchain is used as a possible ownership and participation layer rather than the source of the culture itself.

This order matters. GraffPUNKS was not invented by a token. The token, NFTs and online tools were added to a culture that already possessed history, relationships, visual language, music and credibility.

GraffPUNKS Radio in the Year 3008 Lore

Inside Crypto Moonboys fiction, GraffPUNKS 24/7 becomes the signal that survives when controlled systems attempt to erase memory. Block Topia can clean a wall, block a route or rewrite an official record, but a broadcast can move through hidden receivers, abandoned infrastructure and unauthorised channels faster than the Authority can fully contain it.

The station functions as the soundtrack of the Street Kingdoms and the GraffPUNKS rebellion. Music carries names, warnings, crew signals and fragments of the old world. A mix can become a memory archive. A shout-out can confirm that a missing faction still exists. Static can conceal a message. Silence can warn listeners that a district has fallen.

This is lore, not a claim that current songs provide live gameplay buffs, token drops or secret coordinates. The fictional station translates the real history of pirate radio into the Year 3008 war over culture and memory.

When the walls are watched, transmit through the air. When the air is filtered, hide the truth inside the rhythm.

Why GraffPUNKS 24/7 Matters

The station ties together several generations of independent culture. It connects the spray can to the transmitter, the record shop to the internet stream, the rave flyer to the NFT, and the local crew to a worldwide audience.

Its deepest purpose is not technological. It is cultural continuity. GraffPUNKS 24/7 keeps the sound of the streets active rather than treating it as museum history. It gives the wider Crypto Moonboys universe a real soundtrack created by working DJs and rooted in actual scenes.

The result is both simple and ambitious: a radio station that never turns off, carrying four decades of disruptive art and underground music into whatever comes next.
