HODL WARS Mech

Waterfront Roamers · Relief chassis and memorial carrier · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

The HODL WARS Mech is a Waterfront relief chassis painted in the visual language of the wars. Its local crew keeps spare order blanks, lifting straps and a compartment for damaged objects people request back. The insignia remembers a campaign whose losses still shape arguments about who gets help first.

The returned effects

The published history of Holdfast has a particular weight for this crew. A retrieval order can be stamped complete while somebody in the queue still waits for news. After one shift the operator found a box of returned effects catalogued by repair intake number alone. They took it back to the desk and stayed while a clerk linked each entry to the person who had asked for it.

Off duty

The operator whittles rough chess pieces from cracked handles. They keep the mistakes and lend the set out at breaks. The player who takes the last pawn is responsible for getting it back before the next shift.
