Graff Bear

Waterfront Roamers · Letterer and paint-mixing apprentice · Year 3008

Life on the Waterfront

Graff Bear’s white shirt still carries a scatter of blue paint from the first cover-up he botched. He keeps a capped spray can close at hand and tests colours on scrap carried back from the workshops. Eddie Stax’s practice of crediting the people behind a wall appeals to him in part because he has already learned the cost of being left off one.

The initials on the door

He once accepted a rush job for a doorway he remembered as blank. A faded set of initials belonged to the person who used to close the shop each night. She caught him before he painted over them and offered to show him the original lettering in a photograph. He kept the initials, repainted the bad masking and lost half his fee to the delay.

Off duty

At home he mixes small batches of blue until he can tell which one will survive a yellow light. On his day off he paints price boards for vendors who are happy to let him leave a small signature in the corner.
