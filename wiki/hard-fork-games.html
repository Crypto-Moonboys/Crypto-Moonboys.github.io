Year 3008 / forty-faction summons / selection and control

Hard Fork GamesThe city opens its gates on terms of its own

The Hard Fork Games are Block Topia's recurring institution of competition, recruitment and public selection in the Year 3008 saga. The forty factions receive summons; candidates enter the protected city seeking status, access or a future beyond the ruins. The same gathering gives the Authority information about people it cannot otherwise govern.

The Games matter outside the arena. Families weigh the chance of security against the price of admission. Factions send talent they may never recover. Rebels see a rare opening into the city. Block Topia presents the process as civilisation choosing who can help it survive.

The Forty-Faction Summons

The annual summons is retained in the First Witness convergence account. Forty is the civilisation-wide faction model; it does not mean every surviving roster reliably names all forty crews.

The detailed Games tradition describes ten male champions and two female emissaries from each faction: 400 champions and 80 emissaries, 480 people in total. They are brought into Block Topia's outer rings before the trials. These numbers belong to that specialised account and are not a newly imposed rule for every faction, game or public competition.

The selection draws on talent, reputation and bloodline. Calling it an honour does not remove the pressure to comply with a summons from the best-protected city in the world. A faction may resent the institution while still needing what admission offers.

Feast Week

The entrants receive a week of comfort before the Games. Clean air, abundant food, ceremonial clothing and controlled beauty show visitors what the city can provide. For somebody raised among failing supplies and damaged infrastructure, the attraction does not need to be invented.

In the darker Games accounts, that hospitality also conceals observation. Biometric readings, dream scans and emotional tests reveal loyalties and responses the city cannot extract from a formal interview. The invitation turns private vulnerability into usable data.

This is the institution's central deception: candidates think they are being welcomed into a future while the keeper measures which parts of them are valuable, compliant or threatening.

Competing Accounts of the Trials

The street-war account describes dangerous arena contests and male survivors becoming Crypto Moonboys. Higher progress brings Block Node Defender standing and, in HODL X traditions, a golden ticket connected to Seeding rights.

The Citadel account describes optimisation contests: resource allocation, cryptographic puzzles and algorithmic mastery, with access and Ascension placement as prizes. Its insistence that the city avoids wasteful physical violence sits uneasily beside survivor accounts of injury and death.

These are different institutional readings of the Games. The present canon keeps the competition and its political function without forcing every described trial into one universal bracket. A keeper's official description and an entrant's memory can disagree over the same process.

Bonnets and the Three Paths

Within the Moonboy-clan tradition, head bonnets and crowns make recognised status visible. Young bald-headed Moonboys stand before that classification. Earning a symbol changes how the city reads them; it does not create their humanity.

City Service / City Worker

A recognised role within Block Topia, with protected resources and obligations to the system that grants them.

Space Programme

An approved route beyond ordinary city life. Ascension accounts connect it to preserving consciousness in orbital or extra-planetary infrastructure.

Escape

A break from the authorised route into the Street Kingdoms. Outside life offers autonomy alongside uncertainty about shelter, food and protection.

The three paths are a social and narrative framework. They do not settle every entrant's fate or justify the claim that nobody can change after choosing one. The danger of a hard fork is that institutions and people who shared a past may no longer recognise one another's future.

The Emissaries and the Harvest

The emissary narrative follows women promised an honourable route into the Crypto Moongirl Order. The Clone Harvest account says the real purpose is to collect emotional patterns and artistic instincts for a synthetic, compliant caste.

Jodie ZOOM 2000 recognises the mismatch between welcome and custody. In the Forkborn account, she refuses an offer that requires deleting her past and submitting to neural resynchronisation, then escapes with Aleema and another rejected recruit. Their story exposes what admission costs when a keeper treats identity as raw material.

These details remain attached to their specialised narrative rather than becoming an explanation for every Moongirl's origin. They preserve the Games' human stakes: somebody can consent to a visit without consenting to have their memories copied, their future assigned or their history removed.

Bitcoin Kids, Bitcoin X Kids and HODL

Bitcoin X Kids are the engineered generation inside Block Topia. Alfie Blaze's Bitcoin Kids are the escaped or outside rebel lineage. In infiltration accounts, the rebels use the Games' distraction to assist people within the walls. That does not make the two groups interchangeable.

Golden-ticket and Seeding accounts must retain their HODL X Warriors attribution. The HODL Warriors of the 3008 convergence reading are collective defence when memory itself faces an existential threat. Their exact relationship to HODL X remains unresolved.

The title also has a current real-world creator-builder meaning for qualifying 1/1 holders under published terms. None of these meanings establishes that winning a fictional trial automatically grants a present-day licence, NFT or reward.

The Covenant and the NULL Crisis

Block Topia justifies the Games through its controlled reading of the Keeper, Chain and Hold: a damaged civilisation needs competent people and enforceable responsibilities. The First Witness also asks what happens when the keeper makes exit impossible or treats a person as property.

The NULL crisis exposes another limit. When trusted chain logic becomes a carrier of contradiction, compatibility with the city's systems can become a weakness. The rebellious minds and cultural memory previously treated as defects become valuable because the Authority cannot fully model them.

The Games continue to organise ambition and fear in 3008. Their outcome cannot settle the older Triple Fork of 2880, and the Final Fork remains an unresolved endgame tradition. The First Witness Concordance preserves the institution while keeping its disputed mechanics identifiable.
