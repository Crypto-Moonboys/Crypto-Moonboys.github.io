Faction Commentaries on the First Witness

First WitnessFaction theology · Section 17Last updated: October 2026

This page records the currently evidenced faction readings without pretending the undefined remainder of the Forty has already been named.

Current evidenced readings

Bitcoin Kid Army

Centres Fork, Hold and Fire: corrupt thrones must be broken, freedom must survive pressure, and a signal can cross divided crews. Its danger is messianic certainty—turning Alfie’s ability to unite into permission to obey him without question.

GKniftyHEADS

Centres Keeper, Wall and Unwritten Block. Identity and creative lineage should outlive one holder. Its danger is believing architectural importance grants permanent cultural authority.

Nomad Bears

Read Fork and Keeper through mobility, routes and life beyond fixed systems. Independence is their virtue; isolation is their weakness.

AllCity Bulls

Read the Wall as visibility and the Hold as relentless presence. They understand signal better than most factions, but can confuse being seen everywhere with deserving to dominate everywhere.

GraffPUNKS

Treat the Wall as living archive and the Fork as permission to reject controlled history. Their weakness is romanticising chaos and distrusting coordination even when survival requires it.

Bally Boys

Read Keeper and Chain through street leverage, status and transaction. They expose hypocrisy quickly but can reduce every obligation to a deal.

Crypto Moongirls

Emphasise Chain, Keeper and Hold as continuity, verified order and disciplined defence. Their danger is converting emergency stewardship into permanent control.

Ducky Boys

Read Keeper literally: fix what breaks, maintain what others neglect, keep systems alive. Their weakness is neutrality that can become service to whoever controls the parts.

Nice & Easy Bois

Emphasise Common Blood and Fork: enemies remain human and separation need not become permanent war. Their weakness is mistaking a truce for a solution.

Squeaky Pinks

Read Hold and Keeper through enforcement. Decisive protection is their virtue; order treated as proof of legitimacy is their corruption.

High Hats

Emphasise custody, inheritance and influence as stewardship. Privilege can easily call itself responsibility while extracting from everyone below it.

Hard Fork Rockers

Centre Book VI. A corrupt structure should be split rather than polished. Their strength is rupture; their weakness is collateral damage.

Blockstars

Read the Wall through performance, fame and public culture. They understand repetition and recognition. Their weakness is confusing audience with truth.

Blockchain Furies

Emphasise Hold and Fork as militant defence of conviction. Courage is their strength; escalation is their habitual failure.

Rugpull Miners

Quote Fork, freedom and voluntary exchange while violating Witness and Keeper through deception. They weaponise decentralised language to excuse extraction.

Aztec Raiders

Read Keeper as the right of those strong enough to protect territory. Their discipline can become conquest disguised as stewardship.

Tuskon OGs

Emphasise lineage, memory and hard-earned authority. Continuity is their strength; seniority hardening into unquestionable status is their weakness.

Crypto Stoned Boys

Emphasise doubt, perspective and refusal of clean narratives. Scepticism protects against propaganda but can decay into passivity.

Code Alchemists

Read Chain and Fork as transformable systems. They are powerful repairers and dangerous editors; their temptation is believing anything technically rewritable is morally theirs to rewrite.

Finance Guild

Emphasise promises, reserves, settlement and debt. They understand obligations must close; their weakness is treating everything that matters as priceable.

Information Mercenaries

Centre Witness but sell access to witness. They understand evidence better than almost anyone while making truth available according to payment rather than public need.

Salvagers

Live Book III: waste becomes survival when someone knows how to recover value. Their danger is applying salvage logic to living people.

Moonlords

Read Keeper through large-scale control of resources and networks. They may keep systems functioning while becoming exactly the dependency the Covenant warns about.

Shard Mothers of Manhattan

Emphasise Common Blood, memory, anomaly and protection of lives official systems cannot classify. Inherited trauma can become permanent suspicion of every outside structure.

Chain Scribes

Centre Witness and Chain. Their archives support reconstruction; their weakness is archivist power—deciding what is preserved can become deciding what is real.

EVM Punks

Read Fork as executable dissent: if rules are corrupt, deploy alternatives. Permissionless building is their virtue; mistaking technical escape for consequence-free action is their weakness.

OG Pixel Saints

Emphasise Wall and Unwritten Block as preservation of early digital creation. Their danger is nostalgia becoming a veto over new forms.

Gasless Ghosts

Read Fork and Witness through low-friction movement and hidden routes. Access is their virtue; unaccountability is their danger.

The unnamed remainder

The wider canon still points to forty GK paths, but the surviving archive does not justify naming every missing faction. Those spaces remain deliberately open. A future faction is added only when its name, culture and role are evidenced or explicitly approved.

HODL Warriors are also not used here to fill a missing slot. Their convergence role is a collective defence protocol above ordinary faction rivalry, even though some older lists count them differently.

The verse each faction loves too much

A faction's most revealing scripture is often not the passage it rejects but the passage it quotes constantly. Selective devotion can distort a text more effectively than open rejection because the distortion still sounds holy.

The Crypto Moongirls can quote stewardship until custody becomes ownership. GraffPUNKS can quote dissent until cooperation feels like surrender. Finance Guild can quote obligation until mercy looks like accounting failure. Chain Scribes can quote witness until the archivist becomes a hidden judge. Bitcoin Kid Army can quote fire until charisma becomes proof.

How to read any faction—and any real group

Ask five things: What does it fear? What virtue does it praise? Who benefits when that virtue becomes absolute? Which passage does it avoid? What would make it admit it was wrong?

This method works outside the lore too. Brands, movements, workplaces, religions, crews and families all reveal themselves through the questions they make difficult to ask.

A healthy culture can explain its blind spot without treating the explanation as betrayal. That idea is expanded in Life Readings.
