🗺️ Maidstone Base

🗺️ Lore 📅 Last updated: March 2026 📂 Lore

Maidstone Base is the archive name for the real-world Maidstone, Kent operating location associated with the Graffiti Kings / Crypto Moonboys build. It belongs to the project's real-world layer. Older SAM copy turned it into a fortified Block Topia command centre; that fiction is now treated as metaphor rather than a literal Year 3008 location.

Real-World Role

Maidstone is part of the practical production side of the project: art, planning, digital work, collection administration and the day-to-day process of turning Graffiti Kings culture into public Crypto Moonboys material.

Exact addresses, opening arrangements and operational details can change and should not be frozen into lore as permanent canon. The durable fact is the Maidstone, Kent connection.

Not a Block Topia Fortress

Block Topia is a Year 3008 city-state centred on the surviving Queens, New York region. Maidstone Base is not secretly one of its physical fortresses.

Future stories may use a fictionalised Maidstone memory, archive node or ancestral location, but those stories should be labelled as fiction rather than presented as the same thing as the present-day operating base.

Why the Page Still Matters

The page represents the project's two-layer structure: a real creator operation produces art, software, archives and stories; the fictional universe grows from that work.

Keeping the layers separate makes both stronger. Real-world proof does not need fictional military language to matter, and the Year 3008 story does not need to pretend every current workspace survives unchanged for a thousand years.
