⚔️ XRP Army / ARMY

XRP Army is the common name for one of crypto’s most recognisable XRP supporter communities. ARMY is also listed by Coinranking as an XRP meme coin, making the term both a community identity and a token-market reference in the wider XRP meme-token category.

Community identity

The XRP Army identity is built around long-term XRP belief, online advocacy, meme culture, XRPL education and defence of the XRP ecosystem. In wiki terms, XRP Army belongs beside other crypto-native community formations such as Bitcoin maximalists, Solana meme traders and WAX NFT collectors.

ARMY token context

Coinranking listed ARMY as one of the visible top XRP meme coins when checked. Rankings, prices and market caps move quickly, so this page treats ARMY as a market-listed XRP meme-token reference rather than a permanent ranking claim.

Research checklist

Before interacting with any token using the ARMY name, verify the issuer, trust line, market pair, liquidity, official social channels and whether the asset is the same one listed by the source you are using. XRP Army community identity and an ARMY token are not automatically the same thing.
