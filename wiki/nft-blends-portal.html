Transformation has a material cost

A blend changes the relationship between existing artworks and a new result. Its appeal lies in the sense that a collection can develop rather than remain a static shelf. For a participant, the important questions concern what goes in, what can be lost, how the outcome is selected and which credit travels with the transformed work.

The portal account records an earlier invitation and its proposed benefits. Current recipes and programme terms determine actual availability. Read the design through the artist as well as the collector: a transformation can extend a visual language, but it should leave a clear route back to the makers and the works on which the new object depends.
