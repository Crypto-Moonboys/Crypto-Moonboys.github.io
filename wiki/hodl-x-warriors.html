HODL X Warriors are a distinct Year 3008 champion/combat order preserved in the Block Topia and Hard Fork Games archive. They are associated with Hard Fork champions, earned bonnets or combat status and high-risk service, but the archive does not justify treating them as the same thing as the wider HODL Warriors Calling.

The “X” belongs to a specific later order. It should not be used as shorthand for “stronger HODL Warrior,” nor should HODL X Warriors be silently merged into HODL Warriors after an archive reset.

Where They Sit in the Archive

W81 roster and hierarchy material places HODL X Warriors beside Crowned Royal Moongirls, Bitcoin X Kids and other Block Topia status groups. They are repeatedly described as Hardfork champions and bonnet collectors with battle-ready champion gear.

That is strong evidence for their cultural position. It is weaker evidence for exact population counts, laboratory origin or one permanent battlefield doctrine.

Hard Fork Champions

The Hard Fork Games provide the clearest setting for HODL X status. Champions survive public trials, faction pressure and changing rules, earning visible markers that make their achievement legible inside Block Topia.

A champion title can carry prestige, political usefulness and combat obligation at once. The same institution that celebrates resilience can also use status to bind high-value people more tightly to the city.

Relationship to Bitcoin X Kids

Bitcoin X Kids are the wider inside-generation. HODL X Warriors are a narrower champion/combat order. Some X Kids may later become HODL X Warriors through the Games or related service, but the categories should not be collapsed.

This distinction also leaves room for HODL X members from other qualified Block Topia lineages where later canon supports them.

Bonnets, Armour and Public Identity

W81 visual rules associate HODL X Warriors with champion helmets/bonnets and battle-ready gear. In Block Topia, visible equipment is social language as much as protection: the city makes rank readable.

Repeated war can complicate that clean identity. Repairs, scars, replaced components and unauthorised marks turn a standard champion into a walking record of what actually happened to them.

Not the HODL Warriors Calling

HODL Warriors form across faction lines during existential crisis. HODL X Warriors are a specific standing or recognised order associated with Block Topia/Hard Fork culture.

A HODL X Warrior may answer the HODL Calling. Doing so does not prove that the two institutions share command, origin or permanent allegiance.

The NULL Test

NULL makes the distinction between trained resilience and moral judgement visible. A champion can be excellent at following changing procedures and still fail if the procedure itself becomes corrupted.

The strongest HODL X stories therefore ask whether a warrior selected to preserve Block Topia can recognise the moment preserving the wider world requires disobeying Block Topia.

What Remains Unresolved

Older pages added detailed claims about engineered bodies, cross-faction deployment, exact order size and universal command rights. Those details are not strong enough to lock universally from the current source hierarchy.

The convergence page keeps what the archive supports: HODL X Warriors are distinct champion-level combat figures tied to Hard Fork and Block Topia status culture. Their complete institutional history remains open for later canon.
